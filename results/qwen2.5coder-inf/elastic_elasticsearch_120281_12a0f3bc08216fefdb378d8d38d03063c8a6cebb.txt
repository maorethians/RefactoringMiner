#0W50Q, #716GX, #1R4YR, #AY6PX: The change removes a check for `remaining.size() > 1` which was previously ensuring that there is more than one field remaining after pruning. The TODO comment and the associated GitHub issue suggest that this condition was necessary due to a limitation in handling zero lookup fields. Removing this check could lead to issues if `remaining` ends up being empty, as it might not be handled correctly by `LookupFromIndexOperator`. Additionally, the added comment about `InsertFieldExtraction` not being used in LOOKUP JOIN right index should clarify why the change is made but does not address the potential issue with zero fields.
