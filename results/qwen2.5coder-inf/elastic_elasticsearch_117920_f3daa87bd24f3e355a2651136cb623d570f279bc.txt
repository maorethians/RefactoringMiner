#Y4VB3, #V8Q3Y: The change from `priorityProcessWorker.shutdown()` to `priorityProcessWorker.shutdownNow()` is more aggressive as it attempts to stop all actively executing tasks immediately. This could lead to data corruption or inconsistent states if the tasks are not designed to handle abrupt termination. Additionally, the `awaitTermination` call with a fixed timeout of 30 seconds might not be sufficient in all scenarios, leading to potential resource leaks if the worker does not terminate within this time.

#W2V4H: The removal of the `handleAlreadyShuttingDownWorker` method could lead to issues if there are cases where the worker is already shutting down and needs to notify waiting requests. This method was previously responsible for logging a debug message and notifying any queued runnables, which might be necessary for proper cleanup or state management.
