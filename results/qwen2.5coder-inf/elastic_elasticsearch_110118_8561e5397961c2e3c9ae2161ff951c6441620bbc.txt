#S0VRP, #8XCTF: The `localRefs.mustIncRef()` call has been moved from before the `client.execute` method to inside the `processResponse` method. This change could lead to a potential resource leak if an exception occurs between the `client.execute` call and the `processResponse` method being invoked, as `localRefs.decRef()` would not be called in such cases. Ensure that `localRefs.mustIncRef()` is only called when it is guaranteed that `localRefs.decRef()` will also be executed to maintain proper reference counting.
