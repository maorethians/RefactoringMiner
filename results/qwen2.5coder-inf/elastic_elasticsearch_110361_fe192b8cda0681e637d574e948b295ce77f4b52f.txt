#9K3JJ, #WTWTW: The removal of `acquireShared(-1);` and the addition of a busy-wait loop with `Thread.onSpinWait();` can lead to performance issues and increased CPU usage, especially under high concurrency. Busy-waiting is generally discouraged as it consumes CPU cycles without making progress. Consider using a more efficient synchronization mechanism like `wait/notify` or `LockSupport.parkNanos`.

#XX2FB, #X1X6X: The comments added to explain the reasoning behind not using `acquireShared(-1);` are helpful but could be improved for clarity and completeness. It would be beneficial to specify why false-positive deadlock detection is a concern in this context and how the new approach avoids it.
