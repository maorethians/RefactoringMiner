#2B020, #1HKDY, #7JRNV: The addition of `!brokerConfig.isPopConsumerKVServiceEnable()` to the conditionals introduces a dependency on the broker configuration that was not previously present. #7JRNV: In the modified block, the removal of `if (pullOffset < 0) { pullOffset = brokerOffset; }` could lead to incorrect behavior if `popBufferMergeService.getLatestOffset(topic, group, queueId)` returns a negative value and `offsetManager.queryOffset(group, topic, queueId)` also fails to provide a valid offset.
