#F7A95: The test method `testMultilingual` is testing the tokenizer's ability to handle multilingual text by comparing the tokenized results against expected tokens. However, there are a few points that could be improved for better maintainability and correctness:
1. **Logging**: The logging statement `logger.info(i);` does not provide much context about what is being logged. It would be more helpful if it included information such as the text being processed or the test case number.
2. **Exception Handling**: While the tokenizer is wrapped in a try-with-resources block, there are no specific checks for exceptions that might occur during tokenization. Adding assertions to check for expected exceptions (if any) could make the test more robust.
3. **Boundary Conditions**: The test currently iterates over predefined texts and compares them against expected tokens. It would be beneficial to add boundary condition tests, such as empty strings or very long strings, to ensure the tokenizer handles these cases gracefully.
