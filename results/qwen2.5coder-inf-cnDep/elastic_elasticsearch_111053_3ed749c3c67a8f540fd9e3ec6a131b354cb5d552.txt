#K06WJ, #HRVH7, #R9DSP: The removal of the `SleepFunction<Long, Void>` parameter and replacing it with a `Runnable` simplifies the interface but may not be as flexible. The original `SleepFunction` allowed for more control over the sleep behavior, such as logging or additional actions during the sleep period. If this flexibility is no longer needed, the change is fine, but if it might be required in the future, consider keeping the `SleepFunction`.
