#VJN92: The addition of `this.id = stream.id();` ensures that the `id` field is updated when a stream is set, which aligns with the logic in the `id()` method. However, this change introduces a potential race condition if `setStreamAndProperty` can be called concurrently from multiple threads. The `id` field is not modified atomically, and there is no synchronization mechanism to ensure that reads and writes to `id` are consistent across threads.
