#0KQ5D, #KM0Z6: The `hotThreads()` method is introduced to fetch hot threads information, which is then used within `assertNoSearchesRunning()`. However, the nested `assertBusy` call in `assertNoSearchesRunning()` is incorrect. The outer `assertBusy` already retries the condition until it passes or times out, so nesting another `assertBusy` inside it is redundant and can lead to unexpected behavior.
