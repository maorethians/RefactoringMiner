#K8K5K: The fields `stoppedCompilationTime` and `logShutdownCompilations` are accessed and modified across multiple threads (via `submitForCompilation`) without synchronization or volatility. This can lead to race conditions where the retry timer is not consistently observed across threads, or the shutdown warning is logged multiple times. These should be marked as `volatile` or replaced with `Atomic` types.

#K8K5K: In the `STOP_COMPILATION` block, the compilation queue is flushed (all queued targets for the engine are cancelled) on every call to `submitForCompilation` until the `StoppedCompilationRetryDelay` has elapsed. If many compilation requests are triggered during this window, repeatedly iterating over and cancelling the queue may cause significant performance overhead. Consider flushing the queue only once when the transition to `STOP_COMPILATION` is first detected.
