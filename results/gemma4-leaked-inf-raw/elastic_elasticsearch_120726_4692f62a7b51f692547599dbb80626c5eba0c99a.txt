#R24M5: The `wrapSingleValueQuery` method is called within `getQuery(int position)`, meaning a new `SingleValueMatchQuery` is instantiated and rewritten for every single position in the block. Since the `field` and `searchExecutionContext` are constant for the `QueryList` instance, the `SingleValueMatchQuery` (and its rewritten form) should be created once and cached to avoid significant performance overhead during query generation.

#R24M5: In `wrapSingleValueQuery`, the `IOException` caught during `singleValueQuery.rewrite(...)` is ignored. If the rewrite fails, the code proceeds to wrap the unrewritten query into a `BooleanQuery`. Because Lucene queries typically require rewriting to be functional, swallowing this exception may lead to incorrect search results or runtime failures during execution.
