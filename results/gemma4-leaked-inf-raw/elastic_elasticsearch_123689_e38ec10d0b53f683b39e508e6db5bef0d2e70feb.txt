#NPS68: The method `isAbsolutePath(String path)` does not check for `null` before calling `path.isEmpty()`. While the current callers in `FilesEntitlement` appear to handle nullability, as a public utility method, adding a null check (e.g., `if (path == null || path.isEmpty())`) would prevent potential `NullPointerException`s if this utility is used in other parts of the system.
