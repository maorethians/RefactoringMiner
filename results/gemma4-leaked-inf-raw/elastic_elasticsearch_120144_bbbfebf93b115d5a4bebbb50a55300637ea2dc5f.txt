#EMJE0: The method `checkAndResetDestinationIndexBlock` performs a direct call to `.equals()` on `updatedIndex` without checking if it is null. While a valid `TransformConfig` is expected to have a destination index, this pattern is risky and inconsistent with the null-safe approach used in `TransformConfigUpdate.changesDestIndex` (Diff #D225Y). 
If `config.getDestination()` or `updatedIndex` were null, this would trigger a `NullPointerException`. It is recommended to use `java.util.Objects.equals(updatedIndex, currentIndex)` or the existing `isNullOrEqual` utility to ensure robustness and consistency across the codebase.
