#MSYSP: The call to `brokerController.getConsumerOffsetManager().commitPullOffset(...)` is placed inside the conditional block checking for `isPopConsumerKVServiceEnable()`. This means that offsets are only persisted during a reset if the Pop Consumer KV service is enabled. Since offset commitment is a general requirement for ensuring persistence across all consumer types (not just Pop consumers), this call should likely be moved outside of the `if` block so it executes regardless of whether the Pop service is active.
