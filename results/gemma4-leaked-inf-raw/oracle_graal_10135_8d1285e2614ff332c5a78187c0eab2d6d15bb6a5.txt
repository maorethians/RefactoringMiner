#61SQM: The `SHUTDOWN_COMPILATION` case in `isCompilationStopped()` calls `runtime().getCompileQueue().shutdownAndAwaitTermination(100)` every time the method is invoked. Since `isCompilationStopped()` is called on the hot path within `compile()`, this will introduce a significant latency penalty (up to 100ms per call) whenever compilation is shut down. This operation should be performed once rather than repeatedly inside a predicate.

#61SQM: In the `STOP_COMPILATION` case, the logic iterates over all queued targets and calls `cancelCompilation()` if the queue size is greater than 1. Because cancelled tasks typically remain in the execution queue until they are picked up by a worker thread, `getQueuedTargets(null)` will continue to return these targets. This results in an O(N) operation that repeatedly calls `cancelCompilation()` on the same set of targets for every call to `compile()` during the stop period, which may degrade performance.
