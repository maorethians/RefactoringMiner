#H2WK9, #ZAG9N: The implementation of `hasRollupIndices` and the associated `PARSER_CONFIGURATION` assume that the index mapping is wrapped in a top-level `_doc` field (path: `_doc` -> `_meta` -> `_rollup`). However, in Elasticsearch 7.x/8.x, the mapping source typically places `_meta` at the root of the mapping object.
As seen in the accompanying test changes (e.g., in `docs/build.gradle` and `x-pack/plugin/src/yamlRestTest/...`), indices are created with `_meta` as a top-level key. Because `hasRollupIndices` explicitly checks for `"_doc".equals(parser.nextFieldName())` first, it will return `false` even when rollup metadata is present. This will cause the `TransportPutRollupJobAction` to incorrectly block the creation of new rollup jobs in clusters that actually have rollup usage.
