#T6MD6, #Z9VGJ: The Javadoc for `logLocalCurrentThreads` states that it "does not sample and captures current state only," implying an instantaneous snapshot. However, the implementation calls `detect()`, which invokes `innerDetect`. Inside `innerDetect`, there is a mandatory `Thread.sleep(interval.millis())` between two snapshots to calculate CPU time deltas. This means the method still performs sampling and introduces latency, contradicting the "does not sample" claim.

#7R25B, #Z9VGJ, #X6X2G: The removal of the `SleepFunction` abstraction in `innerDetect` in favor of a direct call to `Thread.sleep()` reduces the testability of the code. Previously, tests (as seen in `#X6X2G`) could provide a no-op function to bypass the sleep interval and speed up execution. By hardcoding `Thread.sleep()`, all subsequent tests calling this logic will be forced to wait for the actual duration of the sleep interval, potentially increasing the total test suite runtime.
