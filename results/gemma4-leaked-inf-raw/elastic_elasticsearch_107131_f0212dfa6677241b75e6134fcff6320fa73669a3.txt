#H4MQJ, #9ZD6Y: In `combineProjections` (#H4MQJ, #9ZD6Y), the logic has been updated to distinguish between the raw expression (the "source") and the named expression (the "alias"). By maintaining both an `aliases` map for resolution and a `namedExpressions` map for the final replacement, the optimizer can now flatten alias chains (e.g., `f1 = languages, f2 = f1`) into their base expressions while preserving the identity of the top-level alias. This prevents the bug where nested aliases might not have been fully resolved during projection combination.

#9REG5: The addition of `Expressions.anyMatch` in #9REG5 is a necessary correctness fix. When aliases are pruned and replaced by their underlying attributes, it is possible for multiple distinct aliases to resolve to the same attribute. Without this check, the resulting group-by list would contain duplicate expressions, which could lead to incorrect execution plans or errors in downstream operators.

BY` with overlapping keys. These tests effectively validate that the projection combining logic handles complex alias dependencies correctly.
