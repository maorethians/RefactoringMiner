#73MFH: The line `fieldNames.addAll(IndexResolver.INDEX_METADATA_FIELD);` may cause a compilation error if `IndexResolver.INDEX_METADATA_FIELD` is defined as a `String`. In Java, `Set.addAll()` expects a `Collection`, whereas `Set.add()` is used for adding a single element. Given the singular naming of the constant and its usage in the previous version (`return result.withFieldNames(IndexResolver.INDEX_METADATA_FIELD);`), it is highly likely to be a `String`. If so, this should be changed to `.add()`.
