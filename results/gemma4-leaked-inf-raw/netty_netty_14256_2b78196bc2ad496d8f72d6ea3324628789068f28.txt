#HEJ1K: The increment of `pooledChunkAllocations` is placed within `allocateNormal`, which tracks when a normal-sized buffer is allocated from a chunk. However, the corresponding deallocation counter in `#088Y1` and the metric `numActiveChunks()` in `#ZP0MS` suggest that these counters are intended to track the lifecycle of the `PoolChunk` objects themselves (the large memory blocks), not individual buffer allocations within them. 
If the intent is to track the number of chunks allocated from the system, this increment should occur when a new `PoolChunk` is instantiated and added to the arena, rather than every time a chunk is used to satisfy a normal allocation request. As currently implemented, `numActiveChunks()` will return an incorrect value (Total Normal Allocations - Total Chunks Destroyed).
