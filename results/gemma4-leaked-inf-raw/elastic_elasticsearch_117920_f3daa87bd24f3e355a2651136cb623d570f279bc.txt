#BJY0X: In `forcefullyStopProcess` (#BJY0X), replacing `shutdown()` with `shutdownNow()` and adding `awaitTermination(30, TimeUnit.SECONDS)` ensures that the thread is interrupted and the system blocks until it actually stops (or times out), which is appropriate for a forceful shutdown.
The handling of `InterruptedException` by restoring the interrupt status (`Thread.currentThread().interrupt()`) follows Java concurrency best practices.

#0S0Z0, #P473A: The removal of `handleAlreadyShuttingDownWorker` (#0S0Z0) and the simplification of `stopProcessAfterCompletingPendingWork` (#P473A) remove redundant state checks in favor of a unified termination signal/wait path, which aligns with the goal of ensuring shutdown is always awaited.
