#JPHYR: **`SystemIndexMigrator.java`**: In several asynchronous callbacks, exceptions are thrown directly instead of calling `markAsFailed(Exception e)`. Since these callbacks are executed within the context of a client request's `ActionListener`, throwing an exception will not trigger the task's failure mechanism; instead, it may cause the worker thread to crash or leave the persistent task in a hanging state until it times out.
Specifically:
- In `migrateDataStream` (diff `#JPHYR`), if `startMigrationResponse.isAcknowledged()` is false, an `ElasticsearchException` is thrown.
- In `checkDataStreamMigrationStatus` (diff `#JPHYR`), the helper method `dataStreamMigrationFailed` throws an `ElasticsearchException`.
- In `cancelExistingDataStreamMigrationAndRetry` (diff `#JPHYR`), if the cancellation response is not acknowledged, an `ElasticsearchException` is thrown.
These should be replaced with calls to `markAsFailed(ex)`.
