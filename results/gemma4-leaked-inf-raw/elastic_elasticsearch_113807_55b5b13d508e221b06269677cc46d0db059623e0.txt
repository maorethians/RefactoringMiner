#890TM: Replacing `assertAcked(...)` with a direct call to `.get(TimeValue.timeValueSeconds(30))` removes the verification that the cluster actually acknowledged the settings update. While the surrounding `try-catch` block handles transport-level exceptions, it will no longer detect cases where the request completes successfully but the cluster returns a response indicating that the change was not acknowledged (e.g., `"acknowledged": false`). This could lead to tests proceeding with incorrect cluster configurations and failing later in non-obvious ways.
