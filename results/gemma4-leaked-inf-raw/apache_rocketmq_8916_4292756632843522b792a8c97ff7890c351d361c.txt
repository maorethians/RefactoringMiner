#B4KZN: The implementation of the lock expiration mechanism introduces a critical concurrency bug regarding semaphore permit management:
1. **Permit Imbalance**: In `lock()`, if the lock is considered expired, a thread can return a timestamp and enter the critical section without actually acquiring a semaphore permit (`this.semaphore.tryAcquire` returned `false`). However, in `unlock()`, that same thread may call `this.semaphore.release()` if its own session has not yet expired. This increases the total number of available permits beyond the initial capacity of 1.
2. **Loss of Mutual Exclusion**: Once the permit count exceeds 1, multiple threads can successfully acquire the semaphore and enter the critical section simultaneously, defeating the purpose of the lock.
3. **Race Condition**: When a thread "steals" the lock via the expiration path while another thread still holds the actual semaphore permit (e.g., due to a hang or slow processing), both threads will operate on the `HandleData` concurrently.
To implement a timeout-based recovery, consider using a different synchronization primitive or ensuring that any "forced" acquisition is paired with logic that prevents illegal releases and maintains strict mutual exclusion.
