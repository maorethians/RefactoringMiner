#PE8SZ: The `assertNoSearchesRunning` method contains nested calls to `assertBusy`. Since `assertBusy` is typically a retry mechanism that polls until an assertion passes or times out, wrapping one `assertBusy` inside another creates redundant retry loops. This can lead to unexpected timing behavior and excessive log spamming from the `logger.warn` call. The inner `assertBusy` should be removed, and its logic placed directly within the outer loop.
