#R24M5: The `IOException` in `wrapSingleValueQuery` is caught and swallowed without logging or propagation. While Lucene's searcher will typically attempt to rewrite the query again during execution, swallowing exceptions can hide underlying issues (e.g., index corruption or I/O failures) and makes debugging difficult. Consider logging the exception at a warning level or defining a clear fallback strategy instead of relying on a `TODO`.
