#4637K: The regex implementation using capturing groups for boundaries (`(^|[\\s,])` and `([\\s,]|$)`) effectively ensures that only whole tokens are replaced, avoiding accidental replacements within other words (e.g., it won't replace "LP" inside "HELP"). 
One minor technical detail: because the boundary characters (like spaces or commas) are captured and consumed as part of the match, if two target tokens were to appear immediately adjacent to each other without a separating space (e.g., `LP,LP`), only the first one would be replaced. However, since ANTLR typically formats expected token lists as comma-separated with spaces (`LP, OPENING_BRACKET`), this edge case is unlikely to occur in practice and does not impact the overall functionality.
