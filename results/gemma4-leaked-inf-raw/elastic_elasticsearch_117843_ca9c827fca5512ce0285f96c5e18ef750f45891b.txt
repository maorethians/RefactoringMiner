#QNWQ5: The `findLookupIndexNames` method identifies indices to be excluded from concrete index planning. However, the third scan targets any `Join` and its right side without verifying if it is specifically a lookup join or if the index is configured with `IndexMode.LOOKUP`. This could lead to standard data indices being incorrectly treated as lookup indices and excluded from data node planning when used on the right side of a non-lookup join.
