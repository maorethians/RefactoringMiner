#JD2H6: The call to `onNextThread.run()` at the end of `innerDetect` may result in an empty log entry. Since `logLocalCurrentThreads` (#1SE5R) uses a callback that clears the writer buffer after every execution, and the logic implies that threads are already being flushed individually within the preceding loop, this final call will likely trigger a log event with an empty string (or just the prefix). It is recommended to verify if the internal loop already handles flushing for all elements; if so, this trailing call should be removed.
