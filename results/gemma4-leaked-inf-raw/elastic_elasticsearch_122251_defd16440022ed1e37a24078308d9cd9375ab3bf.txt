#E6SD1, #C66PC: Broadening the caught exception from `ResourceAlreadyExistsException` to `ElasticsearchException` reduces the precision of the test. While this allows the test to pass and be unmuted, it means the test will now succeed regardless of which `ElasticsearchException` is thrown, potentially masking other failures that are not related to duplicate requests. If a specific exception is expected from the implementation, it should be targeted here instead.
