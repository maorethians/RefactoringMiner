#K8Y51: The implementation of `appendMessageUsingFileChannel` has a critical flaw in error handling: if an exception occurs during `this.fileChannel.write`, the exception is caught and logged, but the method still proceeds to update the wrote position via `WROTE_POSITION_UPDATER.addAndGet` and returns `true`. This results in data loss (a "hole" in the file) while the system incorrectly reports a successful write.

#K8Y51: The use of `this.fileChannel.position(currentPos)` followed by `this.fileChannel.write(...)` is not thread-safe. Since `FileChannel` maintains an internal position state, concurrent calls to this method will lead to race conditions where threads overwrite each other's data. To ensure thread safety and atomicity, consider using the positional write method: `this.fileChannel.write(ByteBuffer src, long position)`.
