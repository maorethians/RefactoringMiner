#HA000: The error message in `SpdyHttpDecoder` reports only one part of the total length (`spdyDataFrame.content().readableBytes()`) rather than the actual total size that exceeded the limit. To be consistent with other changes in this chapter (e.g., #D6NYN, #K4TY4), it should report the sum of `content.readableBytes()` and `spdyDataFrame.content().readableBytes()`.
