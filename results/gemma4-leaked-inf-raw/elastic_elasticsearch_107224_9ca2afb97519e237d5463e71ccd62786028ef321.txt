#92NSE: The removal of `parser.declareString(HighlightBuilder::tagsSchema, ...)` and `parser.declareString(HighlightBuilder::encoder, ...)` from the `HighlightBuilder` parser breaks global configuration for these settings. While the goal is to enable these at the field level, they should still be supported at the top level as global defaults. Since `HighlightBuilder` does not call the common registration logic defined in `AbstractHighlighterBuilder`, these fields will now be ignored when provided at the root of the "highlight" object.
