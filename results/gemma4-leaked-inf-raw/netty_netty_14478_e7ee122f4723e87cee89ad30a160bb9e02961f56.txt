#ZT8RS: The modulo operation used to simulate uint32 wrap-around is incorrect. `0xFFFFFFFFL` is $2^{32}-1$. To correctly wrap a 32-bit unsigned integer (where values range from $0$ to $2^{32}-1$), the modulus should be $2^{32}$, which is `0x100000000L`. Using `0xFFFFFFFFL` will cause the sequence number to wrap prematurely and incorrectly.
