#6FXMQ: In `initOptions`, `writeOptions.setSync(true)` is configured for record writes. Since these writes occur on the hot path of every pop request, forcing a synchronous disk flush for every operation will significantly degrade throughput and increase latency. Consider using asynchronous writes or relying on the WAL for durability.

#P3819: In `popAsync`, the lock acquired via `consumerLockService.tryLock(groupId, topicId)` is held across an entire chain of asynchronous operations (`CompletableFuture`). Because the unlock occurs in the `whenComplete` block of the final future, the lock is held for the duration of the network I/O and message retrieval process. This will lead to high contention and serialize all pop requests for a specific group/topic pair, negating the benefits of the asynchronous architecture.
