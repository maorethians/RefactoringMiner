The logic accounts for the specific requirement of TSDB indices where the document ID is derived from routing fields and thus calculated later in the parsing process than in standard indices. By deferring the assignment of IDs to nested documents until `TimeSeriesIdFieldMapper#postParse`, the implementation ensures that child documents are correctly linked to their parent for Lucene deletion purposes.
