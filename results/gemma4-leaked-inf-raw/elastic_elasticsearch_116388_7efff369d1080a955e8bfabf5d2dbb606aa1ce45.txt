#0N3S5, #QGJJJ: The current implementation for deduplicating shard failures in `SampleIterator` and `TumblingWindow` relies on calling `.toString()` on the `ShardSearchFailure` object to use as a key in a map. This is a fragile approach as string representations can change or may not be unique enough to distinguish between different failure types across shards. While noted as a TODO in the code, it would be more robust to deduplicate based on specific fields such as index name and shard ID.
