#ZB1VY, #1D4R3: The timeout for `latch.await()` has been reduced from 10 seconds to 1 millisecond in `testHandlingShardFailures` and `testHandlingNoClusters`. This is extremely aggressive and likely to cause flaky tests (race conditions) in CI environments, as asynchronous callbacks rarely complete within 1ms. It is recommended to restore a more reasonable timeout (e.g., several seconds).
