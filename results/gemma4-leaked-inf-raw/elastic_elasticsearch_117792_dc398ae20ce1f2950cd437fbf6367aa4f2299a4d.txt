#EAT8Q: The use of a `ConcurrentMap` keyed by thread ID to provide thread-local instances of `SourceProvider` is less efficient than using Java's built-in `ThreadLocal`. Since `getSource` is likely called in a tight loop over many documents, the overhead of map lookups and potential synchronization in `computeIfAbsent` could impact performance. Replacing the map with a `ThreadLocal<SourceProvider>` would provide faster access and more idiomatic code.
