#XBEKX: The optimizer rule only checks if the left side of the comparison is a `ChangeCase` function (`bc.left() instanceof ChangeCase`). Since equality and inequality operators are commutative, this optimization should also be applied when the `ChangeCase` function is on the right side (e.g., `WHERE "FOO" == TO_UPPER(first_name)`). Currently, such queries will not be optimized or pushed down.
