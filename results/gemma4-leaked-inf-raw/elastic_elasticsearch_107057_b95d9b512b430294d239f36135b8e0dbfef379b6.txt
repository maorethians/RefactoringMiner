#5VW1W, #V3H4M: The `try...finally` block used to ensure the `HttpEntity` is consumed starts after the initial setup lines (e.g., `XContentType.fromMediaType(...)`). If an exception occurs during these initial calls, the entity will not be consumed, potentially leading to resource leaks in the connection pool. It is recommended to wrap the entire method body within the `try` block to guarantee cleanup regardless of where the failure occurs.
