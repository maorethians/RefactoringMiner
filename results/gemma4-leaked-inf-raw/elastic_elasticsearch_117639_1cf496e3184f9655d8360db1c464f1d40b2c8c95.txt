#GMBH8: In `Join.java` (#GMBH8), the filter predicates use `== false`:
```java
.filter(attr -> matchFieldNames.contains(attr.name()) == false)
```
While functionally correct, using the logical NOT operator (`!`) is more idiomatic in Java:
`.filter(attr -> !matchFieldNames.contains(attr.name()))`.
