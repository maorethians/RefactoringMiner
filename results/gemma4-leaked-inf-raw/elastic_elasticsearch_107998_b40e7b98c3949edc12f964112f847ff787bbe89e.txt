#GF4WX: The `try-catch` block for `StackOverflowError` is placed directly inside the recursive `forEachUp` method. This means a new exception handler is pushed onto the stack for every single node visited during traversal, which introduces unnecessary overhead in deep trees. 
To improve performance and follow the pattern used in `forEachDown(Class<E> typeToken, Consumer<? super E> action)`, the recursion should be isolated into a private method, with the `try-catch` wrapper residing only at the public entry point.
