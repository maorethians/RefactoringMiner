#RJ1R8: In `Analyzer.UnionTypesCleanup`, changing the return type from `UnresolvedAttribute` to `UnsupportedAttribute` (diff `#RJ1R8`) is the primary driver for the reported fix. By explicitly marking these as unsupported rather than unresolved, the engine can treat them as values that cannot be retrieved rather than a structural error in the query.

#WTADP: The removal of the aggregate re-resolution logic in `Analyzer.doRule` (diff `#WTADP`) further reduces validation overhead and removes a potential source of "overzealous" errors when union types are present in grouping keys.

#XVG0C: The addition of extensive test cases in `union_types.csv-spec` (diff `#XVG0C`) and the updates to YAML REST tests cover various scenarios including `KEEP`, `DROP`, `WHERE`, `SORT`, and aggregation functions (`STATS`, `INLINESTATS`). This ensures that the transition from "Error" to "Unsupported/Null" is verified across most ESQL operators.
The introduction of `toAttributeUnchecked()` in the expression hierarchy (`NamedExpression` and its subclasses) provides a mechanism to obtain an attribute representation without triggering immediate validation errors. This is correctly utilized in `Analyzer.resolveStats` to avoid premature failures during the resolution of grouping expressions that might contain union types.
The use of a capability flag `UNION_TYPES_FIX_RENAME_RESOLUTION` allows for controlled rollout and testing of these changes in different environments, which is a good practice for core analyzer changes.
