#W1QVA: The implementation of `combineUpperGroupingsAndLowerProjections` assumes that both the grouping expressions and their resolved values from projections are always attributes. Specifically, the casts to `(Attribute)` and the use of `AttributeSet` will cause a `ClassCastException` if a user groups by a computed column (e.g., `EVAL x = salary * 2 | STATS BY x`). In such cases, `aliases.resolve()` returns a complex `Expression` rather than an `Attribute`. To support computed columns in groupings, you should avoid casting the resolved expression to `Attribute` and use a collection that supports general `Expression` types (e.g., `Set<Expression>` or `List<Expression>`).
