#RTS4M: In `castStringLiteral`, the return type is changed from `UnsupportedAttribute` to `UnresolvedAttribute`. Since this method handles casting of string literals (constants), a failure here is a definitive incompatibility rather than a lack of information that could be resolved later in the analysis process. Changing this to `UnresolvedAttribute` may degrade error reporting by replacing a specific "unsupported" cast error with a generic "unresolved attribute" error.
