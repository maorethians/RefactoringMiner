#N8W7R, #8TGNN, #D8BHV, #2X3D6: All changes in this chapter correctly address the issue where `BytesRef` offsets were ignored, which is a common pitfall when working with Elasticsearch's memory-efficient byte representations. 
The fixes in `IpPrefix.java` (`#N8W7R`, `#8TGNN`) properly incorporate `ip.offset` into array indexing and comparison logic.
