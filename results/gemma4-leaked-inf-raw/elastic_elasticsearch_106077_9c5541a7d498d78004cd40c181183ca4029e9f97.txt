#FCXZW: In `createDynamicMapperBuilderContext()`, the `MergeReason` is hardcoded to `MergeReason.MAPPING_UPDATE`. Given that this method specifically creates a context for **dynamic** mapping, it seems more appropriate to use `MergeReason.MAPPING_AUTO_UPDATE`. This is especially evident when compared to `MetadataMappingService.java` (#3N3JT), where a clear distinction is made between `MAPPING_AUTO_UPDATE` and `MAPPING_UPDATE` based on whether the request is an auto-update. Using the wrong reason here might lead to inconsistent behavior during dynamic field creation compared to manual mapping updates.
