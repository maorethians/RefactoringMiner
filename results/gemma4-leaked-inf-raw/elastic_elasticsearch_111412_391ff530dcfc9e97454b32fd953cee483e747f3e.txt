#MG3E3, #6AQ1J: The new overload of `Setting.parseLong(String s, long minValue, String key)` is recursive; it calls itself instead of delegating to the four-argument version (e.g., `parseLong(s, minValue, key, false)`). This will result in a `StackOverflowError` when the `enrich.cache_size` setting is parsed from a provided string.
