#ZT8RS: The modulo operation `(sequenceNumber + value) % 0xFFFFFFFFL` is incorrect for simulating a 32-bit unsigned integer wrap. `0xFFFFFFFFL` is $2^{32}-1$. This causes the sequence number to wrap back to $0$ when it reaches $2^{32}-1$, effectively reducing the range to $[0, 2^{32}-2]$. To correctly wrap at $2^{32}$, use `(sequenceNumber + value) & 0xFFFFFFFFL` or `% 0x100000000L`.
