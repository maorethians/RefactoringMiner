#BEGFT: There is a unit mismatch when assigning `maxTaskProcessingQuantumMs` to `this.maxTaskProcessingQuantumNs`. The parameter is provided in milliseconds, but it is assigned directly to a field intended to hold nanoseconds without conversion. This results in the quantum being significantly smaller than intended (e.g., 1000ns instead of 1,000,000,000ns) when a custom value is specified. It should be converted using `TimeUnit.MILLISECONDS.toNanos()`.
