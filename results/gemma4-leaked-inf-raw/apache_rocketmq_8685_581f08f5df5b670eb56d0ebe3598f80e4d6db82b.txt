#6WB6B, #YB402: The removal of `lastReconnectTimestamp` and the associated time-based check (which used `nettyClientConfig.getMaxReconnectIntervalTimeSeconds()`) removes a safety throttle for reconnections. Without this interval, if a server repeatedly sends `GO_AWAY` responses, the client could enter a rapid reconnection loop ("reconnect storm"), potentially leading to high CPU and network utilization. It is recommended to retain a minimum interval between reconnection attempts.

#XCHBA: The addition of `retryRequest.setExtFields(request.getExtFields())` is a positive improvement for correctness, ensuring that request metadata is preserved during transparent retries.
