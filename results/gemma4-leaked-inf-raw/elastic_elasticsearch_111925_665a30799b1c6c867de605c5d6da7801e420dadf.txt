#JBRVD: The assignment `floatArraySupplier = sim == COSINE ? FLOAT_ARRAY_RANDOM_FUNC : floatArraySupplier;` mutates the variable within a loop. Since `COSINE` is the first element in the list, all subsequent similarity measures (`DOT_PRODUCT`, `EUCLIDEAN`, etc.) will incorrectly use `FLOAT_ARRAY_RANDOM_FUNC` instead of the original supplier passed to the method. Consider using a local variable for the current iteration's supplier instead of overwriting the shared one.
