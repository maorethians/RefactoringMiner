#V5WWT: The `userEventTriggered` implementation does not propagate the event to the next handler in the pipeline (e.g., by calling `super.userEventTriggered(ctx, evt)` or `ctx.fireUserEventTriggered(evt)`). This effectively swallows all user events—not just `SslCloseCompletionEvent`—which may break functionality for any handlers positioned after this one in the Netty pipeline.
