#0821F: The implementation of `readEmpty` calls `in.readInt()` three times after reading the boolean flag. Assuming that a `Source` object consists of a line number, a character position, and the source text (as implied by the constructor in `readFrom`), the third call should likely be `in.readString()` or a proper skip operation to consume the serialized string. Using `readInt()` here will fail to correctly consume the bytes of a serialized string, leading to stream corruption for any subsequent reads from the `StreamInput`.
