#BQZJ4: The change replaces `totalTargetAllocations()` with `getTaskParams().getNumberOfAllocations()`. If `totalTargetAllocations()` previously accounted for dynamic adjustments (such as those from adaptive allocations), switching to the static parameter in `TaskParams` may cause `isEmpty()` to return `true` incorrectly for models that have zero base allocations but positive adaptive allocation requirements.
