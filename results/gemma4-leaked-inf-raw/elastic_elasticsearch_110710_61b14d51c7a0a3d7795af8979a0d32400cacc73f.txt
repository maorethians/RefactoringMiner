#5W8KF: The `readNodesFrom` and `writeNodesTo` methods in `NodesFeaturesResponse` are implemented using `TransportAction.localOnly()`. This prevents the response from being serialized or deserialized over the network. While this may be sufficient for the internal use case within `NodeFeaturesFixupListener` (where aggregation happens on the master), this action is registered globally in `ActionModule` and assigned security privileges in `Constants.java`, making it accessible via REST/Transport clients. Any remote call to this action will fail with an exception during response serialization. These methods should be implemented to properly serialize the list of `NodeFeatures`.
