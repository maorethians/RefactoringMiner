#38SG2: In `buildTsidHash()`, when iterating through dimensions to populate the fixed-size portion of the TSID hash, only the first value of a multi-valued dimension is used:
`final BytesRef dimensionValueBytesRef = values.get(0).toBytesRef();`.
While the final 128-bit hash at the end of the method incorporates all values via `values.forEach(...)`, this specific section of the TSID will be identical for any two documents that share the same first value for their dimensions, even if subsequent values differ. If this is a known limitation due to the fixed-size nature of the TSID structure (where only one hash per dimension can fit), it should be documented; otherwise, it may lead to unexpected collisions in this part of the identifier.
