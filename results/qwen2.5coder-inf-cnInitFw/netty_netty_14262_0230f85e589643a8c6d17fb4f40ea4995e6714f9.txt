#3HXWH, #4AA33, #40WAF: The methods `numChunkAllocations`, `numChunkDeallocations`, and `numActiveChunks` lock and unlock the arena for each call. This could lead to performance issues if these methods are called frequently, as acquiring and releasing locks can be expensive operations.

#10D6X, #40WAF: The method `numActiveChunks` calculates the number of active chunks by subtracting `pooledChunkDeallocations` from `pooledChunkAllocations`. This calculation assumes that every allocation corresponds to a deallocation in a one-to-one manner. However, if there are any scenarios where allocations do not result in deallocations (e.g., due to errors or other exceptional conditions), this method could return negative values, which might be unexpected.

#3FK04: The test `testAllocationCounter` checks the chunk allocation and deallocation counts but does not verify that these counts are updated correctly under concurrent scenarios. It would be beneficial to add tests that simulate concurrent allocations and deallocations to ensure thread safety and correctness of the counters.
