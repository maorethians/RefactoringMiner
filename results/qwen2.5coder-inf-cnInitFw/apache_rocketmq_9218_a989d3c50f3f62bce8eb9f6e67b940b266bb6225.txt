#2B020, #1HKDY, #7JRNV: The addition of `!brokerConfig.isPopConsumerKVServiceEnable()` to the condition checks in these methods introduces a dependency on the broker configuration for pop consumer KV service. Ensure that this configuration is correctly set and understood by all users, as it will affect the behavior of the lag calculation when `isPop` is true.

Consider adding a check or handling mechanism to ensure that `pullOffset` is always non-negative before proceeding with further calculations.
