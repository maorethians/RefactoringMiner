#9T5BH, #E9VRG, #QNFD2, #ZDPAT, #AN1BX, #N4P8Z: The introduction of `ReadOnlySlicedByteBuf` and `ReadOnlyDuplicatedByteBuf` classes seems to duplicate functionality that already exists in the base classes. These new classes override methods like `asReadOnly`, `slice`, and `duplicate` but do not add any additional logic beyond what is already provided by their superclasses. This could lead to unnecessary complexity and maintenance overhead.

#AN1BX: The test cases added for release scenarios are mostly redundant. For example, `testDuplicateSliceRelease` and `testSliceDuplicateRelease` both call `buf.duplicate().duplicate()`, which is the same operation. Similarly, `testSliceSliceRelease` and `testDuplicateDuplicateRelease` also perform identical operations. It would be more effective to test distinct scenarios rather than repeating similar ones.
