#EWDN2, #ASX49, #BMM83: The removal of `refreshFailure` and its usage indicates that the previous mechanism for handling refresh failures is being replaced with a simpler approach by setting `refreshed` to `false`. However, this change might not be fully correct or complete:
- The comment `// TODO: Temporary until we fail unpromotable shard` suggests that there was an intention to handle shard promotion issues, which seems to have been overlooked in the current changes.
- Setting `refreshed` to `false` in `#BMM83` might not be sufficient to capture all failure scenarios. If `refreshFailure` was used to store specific exceptions or error details, simply setting a boolean flag might lose important information.
