#G9GR8: The addition of `assertBusy` to wait for the writes to be greater than zero is a reasonable approach to handle potential contention issues with `LongAdder`. However, the timeout and polling interval of `assertBusy` are not specified, which means it defaults to 10 seconds. This might be too long or too short depending on the expected behavior and performance characteristics of the system under test. Consider specifying a more appropriate timeout and interval if possible.
