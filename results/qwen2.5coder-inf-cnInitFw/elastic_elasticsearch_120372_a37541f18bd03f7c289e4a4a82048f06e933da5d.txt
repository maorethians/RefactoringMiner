#PWMWN: The method `fieldNames` now always adds `IndexResolver.INDEX_METADATA_FIELD` to `fieldNames`, even if `fieldNames` is empty and `enrichPolicyMatchFields` is also empty. This change removes the conditional check that previously returned `IndexResolver.INDEX_METADATA_FIELD` directly in such cases, which might not be the intended behavior. The original logic ensured that `_index` was returned only when no other fields were specified, while now it will always include `_index` along with any other fields.
