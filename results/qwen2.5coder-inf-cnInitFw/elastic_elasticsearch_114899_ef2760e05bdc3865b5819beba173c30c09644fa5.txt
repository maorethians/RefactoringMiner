#REQ0K: The logic introduced to remove attributes from aliases in the `computeReferences` method could lead to unexpected behavior if `groupings` contains non-Alias expressions that are also present in `aggregates`. The removal operation is based on the assumption that all elements in `groupings` that are instances of `Alias` should have their corresponding attributes removed from the result set. However, this might not be the intended behavior if there are other conditions or contexts where such elements could exist.
