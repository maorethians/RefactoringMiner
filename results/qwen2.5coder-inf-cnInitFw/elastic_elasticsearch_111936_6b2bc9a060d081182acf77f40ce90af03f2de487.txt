#A58MC: The added cases for `BIG_INTEGER` and `BIG_DECIMAL` in the switch statement are correct, but they rely on the assumption that `parser.numberValue()` will return a `BigInteger` or `BigDecimal` when `numberType()` is `BIG_INTEGER` or `BIG_DECIMAL`, respectively. This should be ensured by the contract of `XContentParser`. However, it would be good to add assertions or checks in the parser implementation to enforce this.

However, there are no specific tests for edge cases such as very large `BigInteger` or `BigDecimal` values, or null values for these types. Consider adding tests to verify the behavior in these situations.
