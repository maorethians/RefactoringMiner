#P0V19: The addition of a new request handler for backward compatibility (BwC) purposes is clear, but the use of `EsExecutors.DIRECT_EXECUTOR_SERVICE` with `forceExecution=true` and `canTripCircuitBreaker=false` might not be optimal. This combination can lead to potential issues if the task execution takes too long or consumes too many resources, as it will not be rejected and does not check for circuit breaker limits.
Consider whether this configuration is necessary or if a more controlled executor service should be used.
