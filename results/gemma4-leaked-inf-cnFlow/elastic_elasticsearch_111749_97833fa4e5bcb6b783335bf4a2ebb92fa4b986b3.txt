#AYH23: In `processDoubles`, `processInts`, and `processLongs`, the logic for handling sorted blocks (`if (valuesBlock.mvSortedAscending())`) incorrectly uses relative indices as absolute indices when accessing values from the block. 
The methods `getDouble`, `getInt`, and `getLong` expect an absolute value index. For a given position, these indices range from `getFirstValueIndex(position)` to `getFirstValueIndex(position) + getValueCount(position) - 1`. However, the current implementation uses indices like `0`, `valueCount - 1`, `lowerIndex`, and `upperIndex` directly, which are relative offsets.
This will lead to incorrect results or `ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException` whenever `firstValueIndex` is non-zero. All accesses in these sorted branches should be offset by `firstValueIndex`.
Specifically:
- In `processDoubles`: lines 238, 240, and 244.
- In `processInts`: lines 276, 278, 282, and 283.
- In `processLongs`: lines 324, 326, and 330.
