#NMZDW, #F4DFD: The changes introduce helper methods `getDataStreamBackingIndexNames` in `ESIntegTestCase` (#NMZDW) and `ESRestTestCase` (#F4DFD) to retrieve the actual backing indices of a data stream from the cluster, replacing predictive naming logic (`DataStream.getDefaultBackingIndexName`) or other internal helpers across various integration tests. This improves test robustness by relying on the actual state of the cluster rather than assumed naming conventions.
