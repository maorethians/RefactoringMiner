#KM0Z6, #RZ50A: The nesting of `assertBusy` calls creates a redundant and confusing timeout mechanism that contradicts the documentation in `#RZ50A`. The outer loop waits up to 1 minute, but since it wraps an inner `assertBusy` (which defaults to a 10-second timeout), the actual total wall-clock time spent waiting could significantly exceed one minute. A single `assertBusy` call with the desired duration is sufficient and more predictable.

#KM0Z6, #0KQ5D: Calling `hotThreads()` inside the inner polling loop of `assertBusy` will cause excessive network traffic and log spam. Because `assertBusy` uses exponential backoff to poll frequently, a search that takes several seconds to terminate will trigger numerous requests to `/_nodes/hot_threads` and flood the logs with warnings. This diagnostic information should ideally be captured only once upon final failure rather than on every polling iteration.
