#BBFGB: The logic in `wrapSingleValueQuery` is executed for every position via `getQuery(int position)`. However, the creation and rewriting of `SingleValueMatchQuery` depend only on the `field` and `searchExecutionContext`, which are constant for the `QueryList` instance. Performing this operation per-row is inefficient; the resulting rewrite should be computed once (e.g., in the constructor or lazily cached) and reused across all positions.

#BBFGB: In `wrapSingleValueQuery`, the `IOException` during `singleValueQuery.rewrite(...)` is caught and ignored. If an exception occurs, the code proceeds to wrap the original query with the unrewritten `singleValueQuery`. This may lead to inconsistent results or runtime errors during search execution, as the "single value" constraint is silently bypassed or improperly applied.
