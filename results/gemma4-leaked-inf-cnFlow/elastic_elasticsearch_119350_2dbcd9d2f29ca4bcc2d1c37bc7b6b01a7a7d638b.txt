#F30E6, #NGWDH: The removal of `addOriginalIndexIfNotLookup` and its replacement in `planOriginalIndices` removes a check that explicitly excluded indices with `IndexMode.LOOKUP`. While `forEachFromRelation` now structurally excludes the right side of joins (where lookup indices typically reside), any index marked as `IndexMode.LOOKUP` that appears on the left side of the plan will now be included in the original indices list. If this filtering by mode was intended to prevent auxiliary indices from being treated as primary data sources regardless of their position in the tree, this is a regression in logic.
