#DSCMQ, #2KTMQ: Broadening the exception catch from `ResourceAlreadyExistsException` to `ElasticsearchException` reduces the precision of this test case. Since `testDuplicateDownsampleRequest` specifically targets duplicate requests, catching a generic base class may mask unexpected errors (e.g., internal server errors or validation failures) that are not related to the resource already existing, potentially leading to false positives in the test results.
