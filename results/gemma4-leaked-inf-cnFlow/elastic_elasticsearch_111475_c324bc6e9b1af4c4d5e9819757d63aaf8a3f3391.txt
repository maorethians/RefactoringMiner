#0X6BJ: The change from `AttributeMap<Expression>` to `ArrayList<Attribute>` for the `resolved` collection removes the implicit deduplication of attributes. If multiple expressions in `groupings` resolve to the same `Attribute`, the resulting `resolvedList` passed to `NamedExpressions.mergeOutputAttributes` will contain duplicates, which may lead to duplicate output attributes in the logical plan.

#E7TTX: The removal of the check for `EsRelation` means that ambiguous fields (`InvalidMappedField`) within an `EsRelation` are now processed by `UnionTypesCleanup::checkUnresolved`. Combined with the changes in #SS25M, this will cause these fields to be converted to `UnsupportedAttribute` immediately during this phase, rather than remaining as `InvalidMappedField` for later processing.
