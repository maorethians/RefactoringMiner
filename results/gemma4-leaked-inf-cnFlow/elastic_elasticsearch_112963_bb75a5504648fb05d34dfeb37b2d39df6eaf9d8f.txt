#Z1MHA: The addition of `IgnoredSourceFieldMapperConfigurationTests` (#Z1MHA) provides targeted validation for the two new settings (`SKIP_IGNORED_SOURCE_READ_SETTING` and `SKIP_IGNORED_SOURCE_WRITE_SETTING`), ensuring that both the ingestion (write) and retrieval (read/synthetic source) paths are correctly affected.

#DBYSZ, #YB3B1, #ST4EC, #H08EF, #MNFNV, #EAPZF, #XWATH, #5TYM6, #29TGV: In several test diffs (#DBYSZ, #YB3B1, #ST4EC, #H08EF, #MNFNV, #EAPZF, #XWATH, #5TYM6), `null` is passed as the `indexSettings` argument to `NestedObjectMapper.Builder`. 
While these tests may not trigger the specific code paths that utilize `indexSettings` (such as synthetic source loading in `#29TGV`), this introduces a risk of `NullPointerException` if those tests are expanded or if the mapper's internal logic assumes settings are always present. It is recommended to pass a default/empty `IndexSettings` object instead of `null` in test mocks to ensure robustness.
