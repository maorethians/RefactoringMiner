#TZV12, #N4H71: The fields `stoppedCompilationTime` and `logShutdownCompilations` are accessed and modified by multiple threads in `submitForCompilation` without synchronization or volatility. Since these variables control the timing of compilation retries and the emission of warning logs across the entire runtime, they should be declared `volatile` to ensure visibility and prevent race conditions (e.g., multiple threads printing the shutdown warning or inconsistent retry timing).

#N4H71: The logic for handling `CompilationActivityMode.STOP_COMPILATION` causes a redundant performance penalty. When the mode is `STOP_COMPILATION` and the retry delay has not yet elapsed, every call to `submitForCompilation` will execute the loop that calls `queue.getQueuedTargets(...)` and iterates over all queued targets to cancel them. 
Since no new compilations are submitted while in this state, the queue is effectively emptied after the first flush. The flushing loop should be moved inside the `if (stoppedCompilationTime == 0)` block so that it only executes once when the stop condition is first detected, rather than on every submission attempt during the wait period.
