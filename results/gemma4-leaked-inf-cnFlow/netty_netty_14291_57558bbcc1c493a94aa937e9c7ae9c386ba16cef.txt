#KJQE2: The reduction of `INIT_DATUM_TARGET` from 8192 to 9 is an extreme change. Since this constant determines the number of allocation samples collected before rotating histograms and updating the preferred chunk size, a value as low as 9 may lead to high variance in statistics and instability in the adaptive pooling logic (e.g., the "99th percentile" would be based on a very small sample size).
