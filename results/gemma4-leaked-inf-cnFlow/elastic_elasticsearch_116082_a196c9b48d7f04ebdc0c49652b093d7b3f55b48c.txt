#E109A, #BKX2D: The constant `XOR_BIT_COUNT_STRIDE_AS_INT` and its accompanying documentation specifically reference `xorBitCount`, yet it is used exclusively to determine the stride for `andBitCount`. Using a constant named after one operation (XOR) to control the logic of another (AND) is misleading. The constant and the comment should be renamed to reflect that this optimization applies to bit counting in general or specifically to AND operations.
