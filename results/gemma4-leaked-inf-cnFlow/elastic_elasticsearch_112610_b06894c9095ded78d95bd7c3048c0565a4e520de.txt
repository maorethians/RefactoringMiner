#PDWDY, #4R7C5, #48ZZ2: The capability `UNION_TYPES_NUMERIC_WIDENING` is introduced in `#PDWDY` specifically to support numeric widening for union types. However, the corresponding logic changes in `#4R7C5` and `#48ZZ2` apply this widening unconditionally without checking if the capability is enabled. This renders the feature flag ineffective.
