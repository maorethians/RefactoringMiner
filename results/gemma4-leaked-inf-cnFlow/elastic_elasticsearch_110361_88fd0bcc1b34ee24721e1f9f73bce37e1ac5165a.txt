#YPQ8R: Replacing the blocking `acquireShared(-1)` call with a spin-wait loop (`while (isDone() == false)`) increases CPU consumption during contention. While `Thread.onSpinWait()` provides a hint to the processor to optimize the loop, threads will remain in a `RUNNABLE` state rather than `WAITING`. In scenarios with high concurrency or delayed completion, this could lead to CPU saturation and potentially starve other threads on the same core that are needed to actually complete the future.
