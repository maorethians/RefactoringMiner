#823CJ: The removal of the `synchronized` modifier from `pauseExecution()` introduces a potential race condition with the `start()` method (#H1J49), which remains `synchronized`. 
Since both methods modify the engine's operational state (`isRunning`) and the `schedules` collection, they should share the same synchronization lock to ensure state transitions are atomic. Without this, it is possible for `pauseExecution()` to interleave with `start()`, potentially resulting in an inconsistent state where the engine is marked as running (`isRunning = true`) but its schedules have been cleared by a concurrent pause operation.
