#7HSA2: The `while (true)` loop in `compareAndExchangeRegister` will result in an infinite loop if a `BaseServiceException` is thrown with a status code other than `RestStatus.PRECONDITION_FAILED` or `RestStatus.TOO_MANY_REQUESTS`. In such cases, the exception is neither thrown nor returned, causing the loop to restart immediately and repeatedly. You should throw the exception if it does not match these specific handled status codes.
