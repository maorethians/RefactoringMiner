#RTP4Y: The call to `spawnResult.exitCode()` is made unconditionally. According to the Javadoc and the TODO in the `SpawnResult` dependency, the return value of `exitCode()` is only meaningful if the subprocess was actually executed (i.e., when `setupSuccess()` is true). In cases such as a cache hit (`spawnResult.isCacheHit()`), the subprocess is not executed, and calling this method may populate the `ExecutionInfo` proto with a non-meaningful value. Consider guarding this call with a check for `setupSuccess()` or ensuring it does not run during cache hits.
