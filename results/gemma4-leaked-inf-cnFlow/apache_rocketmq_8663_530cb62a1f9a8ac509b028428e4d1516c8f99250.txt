Since this lock is used for high-frequency operations in the `CommitLog`, adding map overhead to every single lock acquisition will likely introduce significant contention and performance degradation, potentially outweighing the benefits of the adaptive locking strategy.

#0W0WT: The implementation uses two busy-wait loops during the lock swapping process:
1. In `lock()`, threads spin on `this.state.get()` while a swap is in progress.
2. In `swap()`, the thread performing the swap spins on `this.currentThreadNum.get() == 0`.
This can lead to significant CPU spikes during the transition period, as all attempting threads will be burning cycles waiting for the swap to complete.
