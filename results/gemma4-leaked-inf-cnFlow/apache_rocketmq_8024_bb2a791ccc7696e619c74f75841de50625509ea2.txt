#H4WP3: In both implementations of `pullBlockIfNotFoundWithMessageSelector`, a timeout value is retrieved via `getConsumerPullTimeoutMillis()` and passed to `pullAsyncImpl` and `pullSyncImpl`. However, based on the provided implementation of `pullSyncImpl`, when the `block` parameter is set to `true`, the passed `timeout` argument is ignored in favor of `this.defaultMQPullConsumer.getConsumerTimeoutMillisWhenSuspend()`. This makes the use of `getConsumerPullTimeoutMillis()` misleading as it has no effect on the actual blocking duration.
