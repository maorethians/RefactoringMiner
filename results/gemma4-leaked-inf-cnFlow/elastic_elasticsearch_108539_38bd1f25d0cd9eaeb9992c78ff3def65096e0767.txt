#FF6JT: The implementation of `onRejection` calls `doRun()` directly on the current thread to prevent task loss during executor shutdown. While this avoids leaks, be aware that if `doRun()` throws an exception, it will propagate to the caller of the executor rather than being handled by `AbstractRunnable.run()`'s try-catch block (which would normally trigger `onFailure`). Given the context of node shutdown, this is likely acceptable, but ensure that callers of `fork` are prepared for potential synchronous exceptions during shutdown phases.
