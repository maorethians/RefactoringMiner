#HM50S, #J8TT4: The "sniffing" logic in `hasRollupIndices` and the associated `PARSER_CONFIGURATION` assume that rollup metadata is nested under a `_doc` field (path: `_doc._meta._rollup`). However, in Elasticsearch 8.x, mappings are type-less, and metadata is stored directly at the root of the mapping object (path: `_meta._rollup`).
This discrepancy is highlighted by the test changes in `#010Q5`, `#J7J74`, and `#9RF27`, which create dummy indices with `_meta` at the root. Because the logic in `#J8TT4` explicitly requires the `_doc` field to be present, it will fail to detect these indices (and any actual 8.x rollup indices), causing the check in `TransportPutRollupJobAction` to throw an `IllegalArgumentException` even when rollup usage exists. The filter and the nested `if` conditions should be updated to look for `_meta._rollup` at the root.
