#5TJQP: The `SearchHit` instances are created with `refCountedHit`, which is `null` when `accountMemoryWithCircuitBreaker` is false. However, `SearchHit.decRef()` (as seen in the dependencies) calls `refCounted.decRef()` without a null check. If memory accounting is disabled and an exception occurs during the fetch phase, the subsequent call to `purgeSearchHits` will trigger a `NullPointerException` when calling `decRef()` on these hits. To maintain consistency with other parts of the codebase (e.g., `#Z4SKW`), `ALWAYS_REFERENCED` should be used instead of `null`.
