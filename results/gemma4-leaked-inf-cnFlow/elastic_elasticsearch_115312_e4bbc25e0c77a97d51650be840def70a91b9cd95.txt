#VQF9G: The explicit cast to `FilteredGroupingAggregatorFunction` when calling `next.addIntermediateRowInput` is unsafe. It assumes that if the target aggregator is filtered, the source aggregator (`input`) must also be a `FilteredGroupingAggregatorFunction`. If the pipeline contains a mix of filtered and non-filtered aggregators, this will result in a `ClassCastException`. A safer approach would be to use an `instanceof` check or define a method in the base `GroupingAggregatorFunction` interface to retrieve the underlying aggregator.
