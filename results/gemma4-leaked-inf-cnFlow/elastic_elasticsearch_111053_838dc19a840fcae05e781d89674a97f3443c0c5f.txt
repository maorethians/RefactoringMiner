#9C02X: The logic in `captureThreadStacks` assumes that `threadElementsSnapshotCount` is at least 1. If this value is set to 0 or a negative number via the public `threadElementsSnapshotCount(int)` setter, the line `result[threadElementsSnapshotCount - 1] = ...` will throw an `ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException`. Consider adding validation to the setter or a guard clause in this method.
