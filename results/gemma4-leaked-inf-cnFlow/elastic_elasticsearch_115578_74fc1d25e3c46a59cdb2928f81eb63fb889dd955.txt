#PM63G: The test synchronization logic is flawed. The `finished.countDown()` call is triggered solely when `valueAssertions` becomes empty, ignoring the state of `histogramAssertions`. Since histogram metrics are accumulated over time and may arrive after value metrics, the test can terminate prematurely and pass even if some histogram assertions are still pending or have failed to reach their expected totals. The condition should check that both maps are empty before counting down the latch.

#PM63G: In the final timeout error message, `remainingAssertions` is constructed using `Collectors.joining()` without a delimiter. If multiple assertions remain, their keys will be concatenated into a single string (e.g., "key1key2"), making it difficult to identify which specific metrics failed.
