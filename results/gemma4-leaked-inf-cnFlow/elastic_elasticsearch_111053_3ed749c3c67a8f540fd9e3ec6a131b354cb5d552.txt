#K06WJ, #R9DSP: The removal of the `SleepFunction` parameter in favor of a hardcoded `Thread.sleep(interval.millis())` call removes the ability to bypass or mock the sleep interval during testing. As seen in `#R9DSP`, tests that previously used a no-op lambda to avoid delays will now incur the actual sleep penalty, which may significantly increase the execution time of the test suite.

#HNME2: The javadoc for `logLocalCurrentThreads` states that it "does nto sample and captures current state only." However, the implementation calls `innerDetect`, which performs CPU/Memory sampling by taking two snapshots separated by a mandatory `Thread.sleep(interval.millis())`. While setting `threadElementsSnapshotCount(1)` avoids multiple stack trace snapshots, the method still performs time-based sampling to identify "hot" threads, contradicting the documentation.
