#3TCQA, #ESG9V: The test timeout in `latch.await` has been reduced from 10 seconds to 1 millisecond. While this may pass in local environments where mocks execute synchronously, a 1ms timeout is extremely aggressive and likely to cause flaky tests in CI/CD pipelines due to OS scheduling or resource contention. It is recommended to use a more generous timeout (e.g., several seconds), as the test will still complete immediately upon success.
