#MG8Q4: The modulo operation `(sequenceNumber + value) % 0xFFFFFFFFL` is incorrect for simulating a 32-bit unsigned integer wrap. The result of `% N` is in the range `[0, N-1]`, meaning this implementation will wrap at $2^{32}-1$ and the value `0xFFFFFFFF` (the maximum uint32) will never be reached. To correctly wrap at $2^{32}$, use a bitmask: `(sequenceNumber + value) & 0xFFFFFFFFL`.
