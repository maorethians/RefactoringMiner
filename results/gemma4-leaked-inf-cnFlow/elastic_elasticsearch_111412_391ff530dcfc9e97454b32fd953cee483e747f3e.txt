#PPNG5, #X1F2K: In `#PPNG5`, `createCache` is called with `null` as the second argument (`weigher`). However, in `#X1F2K`, this value is passed directly to `CacheBuilder.weigher()`. According to the provided dependencies for `CacheBuilder`, the `weigher` method performs `Objects.requireNonNull(weigher)`, which will cause a `NullPointerException` when the cache is initialized via the constructor in `#PPNG5`.
