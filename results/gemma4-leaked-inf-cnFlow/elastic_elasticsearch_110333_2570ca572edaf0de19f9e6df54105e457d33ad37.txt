#18Q0C, #7VDFR, #G07DS, #KY4HM, #P0VR3: The changes in this chapter represent a significant architectural shift, removing the `Processor` abstraction layer (and its associated serialization infrastructure) in favor of direct evaluation via the `fold()` method within expression classes. 
The migration of logic from the deleted `Processor` implementations to static helper methods or direct implementation in the expressions is consistent and preserves the original behavior. Specifically:
- The 3-valued logic for `IN` operations has been correctly moved from `InProcessor` to `In.apply` (#18Q0C, #7VDFR).
- The boolean negation logic has been correctly moved from `NotProcessor` to `Not.apply` (#G07DS).
- The regex matching logic has been successfully refactored from an inner class of `RegexProcessor` to a top-level `RegexOperation` class (#KY4HM), with corresponding updates in `RegexMatch` (#P0VR3).
- The removal of the `Processors` registry and the associated `NamedWriteable` implementations simplifies the codebase by eliminating unnecessary indirection during expression evaluation.
The updated tests confirm that the new direct-call approach (e.g., calling `BinaryLogicOperation.OR.apply(...)` instead of creating a `BinaryLogicProcessor`) yields the same results as the previous implementation.
