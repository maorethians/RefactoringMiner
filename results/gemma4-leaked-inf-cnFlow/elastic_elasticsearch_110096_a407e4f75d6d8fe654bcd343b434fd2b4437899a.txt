#6V06T: In the `MvExpand` transformation block, the `Alias` constructor is called with `null` for the qualifier:
`new Alias(f.source(), f.name(), null, Literal.of(f, null), f.id())`.
If the original `FieldAttribute` (`f`) had a qualifier (e.g., `table.field`), this qualification is lost in the resulting plan, which may lead to naming collisions or resolution errors in subsequent operators. It should likely use `f.qualifier()` instead of `null`.

#Z264M: The test case `testMissingFieldInMvExpand` verifies that the overall logical plan structure remains intact after optimization, but it does not assert that the missing field was actually replaced by an `Alias` containing a null literal. Adding an assertion to inspect the contents of the `MvExpand` node would ensure the logic in `ReplaceMissingFieldWithNull` is functioning as intended.
