#EJWNX, #2XFNV: There is a unit mismatch when assigning `maxTaskProcessingQuantumMs` to `this.maxTaskProcessingQuantumNs`. The parameter is provided in milliseconds, but it is assigned directly to the nanoseconds field without conversion (e.g., via `TimeUnit.MILLISECONDS.toNanos()`). This will cause the task processing quantum to be $10^6$ times shorter than intended when a non-zero value is passed to the constructor.
