#6V64K: The use of `Math.min(2, ...)` appears to be a logic error. 
Given that the subsequent static block throws an `IllegalArgumentException` if `CENTRAL_QUEUE_CAPACITY < 2`, and the accompanying documentation describes the capacity scaling with `NettyRuntime#availableProcessors()`, it is highly probable that `Math.max(2, ...)` was intended. 
As currently implemented:
1. On systems with more than 2 processors, the capacity is capped at 2, ignoring the scaling logic described in the comments.
2. On systems with fewer than 2 processors (e.g., single-core environments), `Math.min` will return a value less than 2, causing the application to crash during initialization due to the check in the static block.
