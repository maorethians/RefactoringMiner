#BCR52: Moving the logic to mark target fields as ignored from `postParse` (which iterated over a `Set` of unique target fields) to `createCopyToContext` introduces redundant entries. Since `createCopyToContext` is called for every source field that performs a `copy_to` operation, any target field receiving data from multiple sources will have multiple identical "void" markers added to `ignoredFieldValues`. This results in unnecessary duplicate `StoredField` entries being written to the Lucene document in `#EAW4C`, increasing index size without providing additional functionality.
