#J547T, #F10MQ: Incorrect use of XOR operator for exponentiation in tests
In `DoubleBucketedSortTests` and `LongBucketedSortTests`, the method `randomValue()` uses the expression `2L ^ 50`. In Java, the `^` operator is a bitwise XOR, not an exponentiation operator. Consequently, this evaluates to $2 \oplus 50 = 48$ instead of $2^{50}$. This results in tests using a significantly smaller range of random values than intended by the accompanying comment ("2L^50 fits in the mantisa..."). It should be replaced with `1L << 50` or `Math.pow(2, 50)`.
