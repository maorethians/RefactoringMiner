#MEDV6: The check for link-local addresses `(ipv6[0] == (byte) 0xfe && ipv6[1] == (byte) 0x80)` is too restrictive. According to RFC 4291, IPv6 link-local unicast addresses are defined by the prefix `fe80::/10`. This means the first byte must be `0xfe` and the second byte's two most significant bits must be `10`, allowing the second byte to range from `0x80` to `0xbf`. 
To correctly identify all link-local addresses, consider using a bitmask:
`(ipv6[0] == (byte) 0xfe && (ipv6[1] & 0xc0) == (byte) 0x80)`
