#1YCJK: The use of `Math.min(2, ...)` combined with the validation check in the static block (which throws an `IllegalArgumentException` if the value is less than 2) effectively forces `CENTRAL_QUEUE_CAPACITY` to be exactly 2 for any input $\ge 2$, and causes a crash on systems where the system property or available processors are less than 2. If the intention was to ensure a minimum capacity of 2, `Math.max(2, ...)` should be used instead.
