#T50S8: The projection logic in `planLookup` contains an off-by-one error and a potential `IndexOutOfBoundsException`:
- The layout is constructed by appending $N$ merge values to a source layout of size $S$. This results in indices $[0, S-1]$ for source columns and $[S, S+N-1]$ for merge values.
- The projection creates a list containing range $[0, S)$ and range $[S+1, S+N]$. 
- This effectively drops the first merge value (index $S$) and attempts to include an index ($S+N$) that is outside the layout's bounds.
