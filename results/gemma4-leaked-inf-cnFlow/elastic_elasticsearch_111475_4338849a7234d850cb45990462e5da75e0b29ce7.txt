#55F2X, #74JAY: The change from `Expressions.attribute(e)` to `Expressions.attributeUnchecked(e)` alters the behavior when `e` is an `UnresolvedNamedExpression`. Previously, `Expressions.attribute(e)` would return an `UnresolvedAttribute`, and the subsequent check `attr.resolved()` would evaluate to `false`, causing the expression to be silently skipped. Now, `toAttributeUnchecked()` throws an `UnresolvedException`, changing a "skip" behavior into a hard failure. This appears to contradict the accompanying comment about avoiding type checks during union type resolution.
