#5Z7SB, #RHHN0: The removal of the `IndexVersion` parameter from the `wrap` method and its replacement with a hardcoded call to `IndexVersion.current()` reduces test coverage. Specifically, in `testFloatGetVectorValueAndGetMagnitude`, the loop that previously verified functionality across both a random compatible version and the current version has been removed. This prevents the tests from detecting potential regressions in how different index versions are handled during vector encoding.
