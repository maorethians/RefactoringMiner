#T2D5M: The removal of `TimeValue.timeValueMillis()` when adding the ingest time cell to the table changes the value from a `TimeValue` object to a raw `long`. In Elasticsearch cat APIs, `TimeValue` objects are automatically formatted into human-readable durations (e.g., "10ms", "2s"), whereas raw longs will be printed as plain numbers. This results in a regression in the output format of the API.
