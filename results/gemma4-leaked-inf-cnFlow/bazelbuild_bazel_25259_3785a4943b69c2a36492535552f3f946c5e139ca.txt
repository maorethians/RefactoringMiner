#0PW7F: The use of `.startsWith()` to check if a label belongs to a package (when `flagToPropagate` ends with `/...`) is prone to false positives. For example, if `flagToPropagate` is `//my/pkg/...`, the substring used for the prefix check is `//my/pkg`. A label like `//my/pkg_extra:foo` also starts with `//my/pkg`, meaning it would be incorrectly propagated even though it belongs to a different package. To fix this, ensure that the match is followed by a package boundary character (e.g., `:` or `/`) or that the strings are exactly equal.
