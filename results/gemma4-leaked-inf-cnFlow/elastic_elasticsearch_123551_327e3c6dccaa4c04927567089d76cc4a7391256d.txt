#M14K0, #0P94N, #TXZ53, #6QWBN: The versioning constants (`NO_UPGRADE_REQUIRED_VERSION`, `NO_UPGRADE_REQUIRED_INDEX_VERSION`, and `UPGRADED_TO_VERSION`) are defined in `TransportGetFeatureUpgradeStatusAction` (#M14K0), but they are referenced as members of `SystemIndices` in other parts of the code (#0P94N, #TXZ53, #6QWBN). This will lead to compilation errors. Furthermore, `UPGRADED_TO_VERSION` is marked as `private`, making it inaccessible to `SystemIndices` even if the class reference were correct. These constants should be moved to `SystemIndices`.
