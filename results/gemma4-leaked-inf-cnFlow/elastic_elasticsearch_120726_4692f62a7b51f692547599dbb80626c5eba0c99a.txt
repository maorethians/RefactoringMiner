#BBFGB: The `wrapSingleValueQuery` method is called within `getQuery(int position)`, meaning it executes for every position in the block. However, the creation and rewriting of `SingleValueMatchQuery` depend only on the `field` and `searchExecutionContext`, which are constant for the lifetime of the `QueryList` instance. Performing a query rewrite (which involves iterating over index leaves) per position is a significant performance bottleneck. This logic should be computed once (e.g., in the constructor or lazily cached) and reused across all positions.

#BBFGB: In `wrapSingleValueQuery`, the `IOException` during `singleValueQuery.rewrite(...)` is caught and ignored. If the rewrite fails, the method proceeds to return the original query without the single-value filter. This may lead to incorrect results where multi-valued fields are matched even when `onlySingleValues` is enabled. The exception should be handled more robustly or logged.
