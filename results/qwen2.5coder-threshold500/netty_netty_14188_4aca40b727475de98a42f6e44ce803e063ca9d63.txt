#9E1ZK, #V2NGT: The conditional check for including `scopeId` in `Inet6Address.getByAddress` is too restrictive and does not cover the full range of link-local addresses. Specifically, it only checks for the first two bytes being `0xfe 0x80`, which corresponds to the unique local address range, but not other valid link-local ranges like `fe80::/10`. Additionally, the condition does not handle cases where `scopeId` is non-zero but the address is not link-local. This can lead to incorrect handling of valid link-local addresses and omitting scope IDs from addresses where they are explicitly required, potentially causing network connectivity issues or misconfigurations.

#HF52S, #GJPRE: The newly added test class `NativeInetAddressTest` and its methods do not cover scenarios where `scopeId` is non-zero but the address is not link-local. This leaves a gap in test coverage for this specific case, which can lead to undetected bugs related to the handling of scope IDs for non-link-local addresses, reducing the reliability of the code.
