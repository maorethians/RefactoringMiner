#3GGBZ, #M15XK: The removal of `assertAcked` means that the test will no longer assert that the update settings operation is acknowledged. This could lead to scenarios where the method proceeds even if the cluster settings update fails silently, potentially causing subsequent tests to fail or behave unpredictably. Additionally, the introduction of `.get(TimeValue.timeValueSeconds(30))` makes the operation synchronous and waits for a response within 30 seconds, which increases test execution time and introduces potential bottlenecks. This change ensures that the settings update operation is completed before the method proceeds but no longer checks if the update was acknowledged, leading to silent failures that are difficult to diagnose and increased variability in test performance.
