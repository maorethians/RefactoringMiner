#3ZNA3, #G57VQ, #R2MYC, #HVMTF, #4BBRT, #P4GFE: The removal of the `refreshFailure` variable and its associated checks in both `maybeFinish` and `onFailure` methods introduces significant risks to error handling. In `maybeFinish`, the direct call to `respond.onSuccess(refreshed.get())` without checking for prior exceptions can lead to incorrect behavior if a refresh failure occurs, as it bypasses the previous mechanism for handling such failures. Additionally, in `onFailure`, repurposing `refreshed` to indicate both successful and failed refresh operations introduces ambiguity in determining the state of the system, potentially affecting logging and debugging capabilities.
