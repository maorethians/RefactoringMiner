#ERJJH: The addition of the line `this.id = stream.id();` introduces a redundant assignment that is already ensured by the assertion `assert id == -1 || stream.id() == id;`. This redundancy does not alter the functional behavior but introduces unnecessary complexity and potential confusion during maintenance. The method `setStreamAndProperty` will still correctly associate a stream with an ID and set the property on that stream, but the redundant assignment can lead to confusion as developers might question why the assignment is made twice.
