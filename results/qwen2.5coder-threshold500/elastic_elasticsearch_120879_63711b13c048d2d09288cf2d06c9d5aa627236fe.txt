#66B5Q, #01D56: The introduction of `assumeTrue("LOOKUP JOIN requires corresponding capability", EsqlCapabilities.Cap.JOIN_LOOKUP_V12.isEnabled());` in both `testValidJoinPattern` and `testInvalidJoinPatterns` ensures that tests only run when the necessary capability is enabled. However, this approach can lead to undetected issues if the `JOIN_LOOKUP_V12` capability is not available, as the tests will silently skip execution without any explicit logging or reporting mechanism. This poses a systemic risk of missing potential bugs related to LOOKUP JOINs in environments where the capability is disabled and makes it difficult to trace why certain tests are being skipped.
