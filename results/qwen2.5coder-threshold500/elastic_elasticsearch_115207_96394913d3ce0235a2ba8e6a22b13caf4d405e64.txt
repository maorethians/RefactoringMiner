#SH7N7, #R9011: The change from a conditional flush based on `randomBoolean()` to an unconditional flush can lead to increased resource usage and potential performance degradation in tests, as the flush operation is now always performed regardless of necessity. This divergence from the original intent, which allowed for variability to simulate real-world scenarios more accurately, introduces a risk where unnecessary I/O operations could occur, impacting test performance and resource efficiency.
