#FKJHK, #ZS4QE: The introduction of `.version(getOldClusterTestVersion())` in place of a hardcoded version setting is intended to make the cluster version dynamic, enhancing flexibility and maintainability. However, this change inadvertently removed the `cluster.logsdb.enabled` setting from both the `<before_change>` and `<after_change>` contexts. This omission could lead to a misconfigured test environment, where critical logging features are disabled. As a result, important compatibility issues related to logsdb functionality might be missed during the upgrade process testing.
