#RBQQ5, #6MY03, #8SJ8C, #4A07S: The test case no longer verifies that binary field data is not included in the _source or stored in the index when `syntheticSourceSupport` and `store` are set to false. The changes disable synthetic source support and storage of binary fields but do not include assertions to verify that these fields are indeed not part of the _source or retrievable from the index, introducing a risk that the system may incorrectly assume binary data is handled as expected when it is not, leading to potential data retrieval failures that go undetected by this test case.
