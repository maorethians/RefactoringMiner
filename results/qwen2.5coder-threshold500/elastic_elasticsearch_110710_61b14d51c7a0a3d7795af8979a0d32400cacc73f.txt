#6E17P, #338VZ, #QHVEM: The `readNodesFrom` and `writeNodesTo` methods in `NodesFeaturesResponse` call `TransportAction.localOnly()`, which does not perform any actual serialization or deserialization. This implementation is incomplete and does not fulfill the intended purpose of these methods, as they are supposed to handle the serialization and deserialization of node features. This systemic risk poses a significant issue because it means that the feature data cannot be correctly transmitted over the network, leading to potential data loss or incorrect behavior when querying node features.

#VHD0G, #YCBDJ: The implementation of `NodeFeaturesFixupListener` includes a retry mechanism that schedules retries indefinitely without any limit or condition to stop retrying in case of persistent failures. This can lead to infinite loops of failed attempts, consuming resources unnecessarily and potentially overwhelming the system with repeated requests. Additionally, the listener logs node IDs directly without any sanitization or privacy considerations, which exposes sensitive information. Furthermore, the logging for failed nodes and retry attempts lacks context and details, making it difficult to diagnose issues.
