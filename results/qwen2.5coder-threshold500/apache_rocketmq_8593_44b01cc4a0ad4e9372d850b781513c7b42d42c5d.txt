#1TEW0, #Z1HAT, #QA083, #9TM3A: The change removes the addition of the backlog (max offset - consumer offset) to `restNum` when a queue is blocked in order consumption. While this modification optimizes performance by reducing unnecessary wake-ups of long-polling requests and CPU usage, it introduces a risk of under-reporting remaining messages. This could lead to message starvation or incorrect consumer behavior, as consumers might assume no more messages are available when there are actually blocked messages waiting to be processed.

#WTBF9, #7R78T, #4ZNP5, #QJQH9, #MJP36, #0DKQ9, #MDRSW, #PJWWV: The added comments explain the rationale behind the change: current requests calculate the total number of messages waiting to be filtered for new message arrival notifications in the long-polling service. Including the backlog would lead to frequent unnecessary wake-ups of long-polling requests, resulting in unnecessary CPU usage. When a client acknowledges a message, the long-polling request is notified by `AckMessageProcessor.ackOrderly()`, and messages are not delayed. However, this change could result in under-reporting of remaining messages when queues are blocked during order consumption, potentially causing message starvation or incorrect consumer behavior.
