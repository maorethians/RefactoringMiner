#3BEYN, #K9169, #GDNW3, #S4G29: The change from `ResourceAlreadyExistsException` to `ElasticsearchException` introduces a risk of catching unintended exceptions, leading to false positives in the test. The test now passes for any `ElasticsearchException`, not just `ResourceAlreadyExistsException`. This broadening of the scope of caught exceptions beyond what was originally intended could lead to false positives, where the test incorrectly passes due to unrelated `ElasticsearchException` instances, thus reducing the reliability and effectiveness of the test suite.
