#ANDGD, #36ZTR, #6V2YK, #8PYTD: The test method `testNoStackOverflow` only checks for exceptions that are instances of `ParsingException | org.elasticsearch.xpack.esql.parser.ParsingException`. However, the implementation introduces try-catch blocks in methods (`execute`, `forEachDown`, `forEachUp`) to throw a `ParsingException` when a `StackOverflowError` occurs. This discrepancy means that if a `StackOverflowError` is not caught and rethrown as a `ParsingException`, it will go unhandled in the test, leading to potential undetected stack overflow issues during execution planning. This introduces a risk of system instability and unhandled exceptions in production.
