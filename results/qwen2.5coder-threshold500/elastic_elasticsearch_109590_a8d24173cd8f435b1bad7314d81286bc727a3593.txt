#0YPC1, #E1522: The removal of `throws Exception` from the method signature simplifies the method signature but may hide potential exceptions that could occur during execution, leading to unhandled exceptions in the broader testing framework. This introduces a risk where exceptions are silently ignored or propagated unexpectedly.

#PJ4GK: The deletion of the comment indicating that all ports bound to the data node need to be tested removes important context for future maintainers about the intent behind testing multiple ports. This introduces a risk of misunderstanding or misinterpretation of the test's purpose.

#T5R2C, #R6EYT, #BJ1G6, #5TKG4, #9Z384, #644Q7, #1ES70: The loop that iterates over a fixed number of client ports is replaced with `transportClient.addTransportAddresses(targetAddresses)`, enhancing flexibility and adaptability. However, this change may introduce risks if `targetAddresses` does not contain any valid addresses or if the random selection process fails to include critical ports. Additionally, the dynamic collection of addresses and logging introduces a performance overhead if the number of addresses is large, and the logging lacks detailed information about each address, which could be useful for debugging or observability.
