3. **Test Coverage**:
   - While new test cases (`testPreserveParanthesis`) have been added, they only cover specific scenarios involving parentheses and brackets. It would be beneficial to add more comprehensive tests that cover a wider range of error conditions and ensure that the regex replacements are applied correctly in all cases.
