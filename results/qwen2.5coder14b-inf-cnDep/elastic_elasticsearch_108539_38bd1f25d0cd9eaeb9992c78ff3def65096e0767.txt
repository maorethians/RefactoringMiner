#FF6JT: The addition of `logger.error(() -> "unexpected error during [" + task + "]", e);` and `assert false : e;` in the `onFailure` method will log an error message and assert false, which could lead to a test failure or application crash. This might not be the desired behavior for all scenarios. Consider adding more context or handling the exception differently based on the nature of the error.
The `onRejection` method is added to handle executor shutdown by executing on the current thread. Ensure that this approach does not introduce new concurrency issues or performance bottlenecks, especially in high-load scenarios.
