#YPQ8R, #QES0C: The change from using `acquireShared(-1)` to a busy-wait loop with `Thread.onSpinWait()` can lead to increased CPU usage and potential performance issues, especially if multiple threads are completing the future concurrently. This approach might also hide underlying concurrency issues that could be better addressed by proper synchronization mechanisms. The introduction of the `FutureCompleter` class and the `testAssertCompleteAllowedAllowsConcurrentCompletes` test method is a good addition to ensure concurrent completions are handled correctly. However, the busy-wait loop in `FutureCompleter.run()` can be improved for better performance and readability. Consider using a more efficient synchronization mechanism or a condition variable to wait for the future to complete.
