#GTR25, #RM888, #W16R5: The removal of the constructor and the `append` method from the `MockLogAppender` class and their replacement with a new inner class `RealMockAppender` introduces a significant change in how logging events are handled. The new approach uses a static parent appender that manages multiple instances of `MockLogAppender`. This change is necessary to support concurrent log handling and lifecycle management, but it should be thoroughly tested to ensure correctness and thread safety.
