#823CJ, #H1J49: The removal of the `synchronized` modifier from `pauseExecution()` while maintaining it on `start()` introduces a potential race condition during lifecycle transitions. 
If `pauseExecution()` is invoked concurrently with `start()`, the following sequence could occur:
1. `start()` sets `isRunning` to `true`.
2. `pauseExecution()` sets `isRunning` to `false` and clears the `schedules` map.
3. `start()` completes its execution by calling `schedules.putAll(startingSchedules)`.
This results in an inconsistent state where the engine is logically paused (`isRunning == false`), yet the schedules map remains populated with watches. To ensure atomicity of these lifecycle changes, `pauseExecution()` should remain synchronized.
