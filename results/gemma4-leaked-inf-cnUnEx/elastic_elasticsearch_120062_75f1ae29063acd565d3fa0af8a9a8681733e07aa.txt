#NT186: The change replaces the use of the shard's global ID (`shardTarget().getShardId().id()`) with the request-specific ordinal (`request().shardRequestIndex()`). This is a significant semantic shift; while the former identifies the physical/logical shard in the cluster, the latter identifies the shard's position within the context of a specific search request. Assuming this change is intended to support scenarios where multiple requests are sent to the same shard or where virtual shards are involved, the logic is correct.
