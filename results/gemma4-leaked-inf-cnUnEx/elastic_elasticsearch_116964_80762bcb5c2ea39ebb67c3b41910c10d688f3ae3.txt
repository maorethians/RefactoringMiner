#SNEZ0: The test method `testVisitGeoShapeWrap` is named to suggest it tests longitude wrapping, but it calls `SpatialEnvelopeVisitor.visit(geometry, false)`, which explicitly disables wrapping. This means the test only verifies that wrapping is not performed when disabled, leaving a gap in coverage for complex geometries (e.g., Polygons or MultiPolygons) that cross the dateline with wrapping enabled.
