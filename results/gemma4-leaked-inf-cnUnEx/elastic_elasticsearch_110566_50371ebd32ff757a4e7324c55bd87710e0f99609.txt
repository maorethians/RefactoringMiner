#GMD2Y: The use of a Java `assert` statement to verify that the `logicalPlan` is analyzed is potentially problematic. Since assertions are typically disabled in production environments (via the `-da` JVM flag), this precondition check will be skipped. If the `logicalPlanOptimizer.optimize()` method requires an analyzed plan to function correctly, failing to enforce this check with a proper exception (e.g., `IllegalStateException`) could lead to unpredictable behavior or hard-to-debug failures in production.
