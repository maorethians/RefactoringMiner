#QYNZX: The use of `thenCompose` combined with a manually created `CompletableFuture` (e.g., `future0`) to handle synchronous logic and checked exceptions is non-idiomatic. Since the operations inside the lambda (decoding headers and populating a POJO) are synchronous, this can be simplified using `thenApply`. Any checked exceptions from `decodeCommandCustomHeader` can be wrapped in a `RuntimeException` or handled via a helper method to improve readability and reduce overhead.
