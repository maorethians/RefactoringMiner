#N9VGB: The cast of the grouping expression `g` to `(Attribute)` is unsafe. Since `groupings()` returns a list of `Expression`, and ESQL allows expressions (not just attributes) in grouping clauses, this will throw a `ClassCastException` if a non-attribute expression is used for grouping. It should be replaced with an `instanceof` check.
