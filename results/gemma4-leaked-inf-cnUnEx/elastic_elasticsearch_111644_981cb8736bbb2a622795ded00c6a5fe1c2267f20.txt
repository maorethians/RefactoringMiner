#HAP8A: The addition of the check `if (lastUsedCell == mean.length - 1)` correctly prevents an `ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException`. Without this, if the internal buffer reached its maximum capacity, the subsequent `else` block would increment `lastUsedCell` beyond the array bounds before attempting to access `mean[lastUsedCell]`. Forcing `addThis = true` in this boundary condition ensures that remaining data is merged into the final centroid rather than crashing the process.
