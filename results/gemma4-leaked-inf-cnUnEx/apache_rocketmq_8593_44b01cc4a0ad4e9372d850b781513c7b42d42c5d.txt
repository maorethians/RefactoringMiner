#03PR1: The logic change to use `future.complete(restNum)` instead of including the backlog accumulation for blocked orderly queues is well-justified by the accompanying comment regarding CPU usage and long-polling wake-ups.
However, there is a regression in how `clearInFlightMessageNum` is called. In the original code, this method was called once after the block check. In the new implementation:
1. A call to `clearInFlightMessageNum` has been added inside the new `if (isOrder)` block.
2. The original call to `clearInFlightMessageNum` remains immediately following that block (only its formatting was updated).
As a result, for orderly consumption requests that are **not** blocked, `clearInFlightMessageNum` will be invoked twice consecutively. You should remove the redundant call—either by removing the one inside the `if (isOrder)` block or by moving the original call into that block and removing its duplicate.
