#WYAKD: In the `FlatNumberOrByteSizeValue.parse` method, a bitwise OR operator (`|`) is used instead of a logical OR operator (`||`) in the condition `value.endsWith("%") || value.endsWith("b") | value.endsWith("B")`. While this works for boolean operands in Java (as a non-short-circuiting logical OR), it is non-standard and potentially confusing to other developers. It should be replaced with `||` for consistency and clarity.
