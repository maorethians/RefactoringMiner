#MBJYW: The call to `remainingTime.timeUnit().toChronoUnit()` will likely cause a compilation error. `java.util.concurrent.TimeUnit` (the return type of `timeUnit()`) does not have a `toChronoUnit()` method in the standard JDK. To add a `TimeValue` to an `Instant`, it would be more straightforward to use `currentTime.get().plusNanos(remainingTime.nanos())`.
