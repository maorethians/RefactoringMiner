#ZEWQR: The addition of `this.parent == null` to the condition for returning `ScoreMode.TOP_DOCS` is a correct safeguard. In Elasticsearch, sub-aggregations operate on a subset of documents defined by their parent; applying the `TOP_DOCS` optimization (which typically relies on the global top documents) can lead to incorrect results when the aggregator is nested. Forcing these cases into `COMPLETE` or `COMPLETE_NO_SCORES` ensures correctness at the cost of some performance, which is the expected trade-off for sub-aggregations.

#Q4Z2C: The new test case `testScoring()` effectively exercises the logic changed in `#ZEWQR`. By nesting a `CardinalityAggregationBuilder` inside a `TermsAggregationBuilder`, which is itself inside a `NestedAggregationBuilder`, it ensures that the cardinality aggregator has a non-null parent, thereby triggering the updated conditional path.
