#VQF9G: The explicit cast to `FilteredGroupingAggregatorFunction` when calling `next.addIntermediateRowInput` introduces a risk of `ClassCastException`. Since this method overrides a member of `GroupingAggregatorFunction`, it narrows the contract by assuming that any `input` passed to it must be a filtered aggregator. If the system allows non-filtered aggregators to provide intermediate input to a filtered one, this will crash. Consider using an `instanceof` check or adding a method to the base interface to retrieve the underlying aggregator if applicable.
