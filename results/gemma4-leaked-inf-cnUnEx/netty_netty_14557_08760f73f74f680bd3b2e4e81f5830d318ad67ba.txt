#EJWNX, #2XFNV: There is a unit mismatch when assigning the `maxTaskProcessingQuantumMs` parameter to the `maxTaskProcessingQuantumNs` field. The parameter is specified in milliseconds (as indicated by its name and Javadoc), but it is assigned directly to the nanoseconds field without conversion. This will result in the processing quantum being $10^6$ times shorter than intended when a custom value is provided. It should be converted using `TimeUnit.MILLISECONDS.toNanos()`.
