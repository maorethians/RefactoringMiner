#MEDV6: The check for link-local addresses `(ipv6[0] == (byte) 0xfe && ipv6[1] == (byte) 0x80)` is slightly too restrictive. According to RFC 4291, Link-Local Unicast addresses are defined by the prefix `fe80::/10`, meaning the first byte must be `0xFE` and the second byte's two most significant bits must be `10` (binary). This covers a range of values for the second byte from `0x80` to `0xBF`. 
To fully comply with the RFC, consider using a bitmask:
`(ipv6[0] == (byte) 0xfe && (ipv6[1] & 0xc0) == (byte) 0x80)`
