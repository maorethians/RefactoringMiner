#MG8Q4: The modulo operation `(sequenceNumber + value) % 0xFFFFFFFFL` is incorrect for implementing a uint32 wrap-around. Using `0xFFFFFFFFL` ($2^{32}-1$) as the divisor results in values in the range $[0, 2^{32}-2]$, meaning the maximum possible uint32 value ($4,294,967,295$) will never be reached. To correctly wrap at $2^{32}$, you should use `(sequenceNumber + value) % 0x100000000L` or a bitwise mask: `(sequenceNumber + value) & 0xFFFFFFFFL`.
