#B4Z4H: The change in `#B4Z4H` relocates the call to `localRefs.mustIncRef()` from the beginning of the `accept` method to inside the `processResponse` callback. 
In the original implementation, the reference count was incremented before the asynchronous `client.execute` call but only decremented within the completion callback of a successful chunked response. This created a resource leak if the request failed or timed out before reaching the `processResponse` stage. By moving the increment inside the callback, the reference is now held strictly for the duration of the chunked response delivery, ensuring that every increment is paired with a corresponding decrement regardless of whether the initial request succeeded.
