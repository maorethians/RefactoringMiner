#0M4T8: The change replaces `Strings.split(c, ":")` (which splits only at the first occurrence of the delimiter) with `String.split(":")` (which splits at all occurrences). Combined with the check `if (nameWithType.length != 2)`, this changes the validation logic: headers that previously contained multiple colons (e.g., `"field:type:extra"`) were accepted as `name="field"` and `typeName="type:extra"`, but will now throw an `IllegalArgumentException`. If the intention was to maintain "split at first occurrence" while supporting escaped double-colons, `Strings.split(escapeTypecast(c), ":")` should be used instead.
