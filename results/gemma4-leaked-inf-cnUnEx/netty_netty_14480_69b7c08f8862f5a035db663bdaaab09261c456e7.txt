#TCNQE, #AQN8A: The logic has changed from pre-emptive checking to post-read validation. This allows the stream to read up to one byte (in `read()`) or one additional byte beyond the limit (in `read(byte[], int, int)`) before throwing an `IOException`. In a bounded stream, the bound should typically be a hard limit that prevents any data beyond the threshold from being consumed from the underlying source.

#B9YWS, #TCNQE, #AQN8A: The previous implementation protected against integer overflow by checking if `sum < 0` in `checkMaxBytesRead(int n)`. The new implementation removes this check and performs validation after the increment (`numRead++` or `numRead += b`). If `numRead` overflows and wraps around to a negative value, the condition `numRead > maxBytesRead` will evaluate to false, effectively bypassing the read limit.
