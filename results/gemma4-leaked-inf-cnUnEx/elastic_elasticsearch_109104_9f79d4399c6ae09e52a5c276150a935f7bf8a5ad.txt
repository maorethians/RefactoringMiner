#PWYN9: The implementation in `TransportFindFieldStructureAction` manually offloads work to the generic thread pool using `threadPool.generic().execute(ActionRunnable.supply(...))`. This bypasses the `TextStructExecutor` abstraction introduced in this chapter, which was specifically created to encapsulate this workaround and facilitate its future removal (as noted in the TODO in `#WBD3E`). For consistency and maintainability, this action should inject `TextStructExecutor` and use its `execute` method.
