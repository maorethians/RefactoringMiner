#D8GXG: The constants `ALLOWED_SETTING_KEYS`, `ALLOWED_SETTINGS_PREFIXES`, and `EXPLICITLY_DENIED_SETTINGS` are accessed without their class qualifier (`UpdateWatcherSettingsAction`). Since these are defined in `UpdateWatcherSettingsAction` and no static imports are provided in the changes, this will result in a compilation error. The previous implementation correctly used the qualified name for `ALLOWED_SETTING_KEYS`.
