#RVHE9: The logic in `isCompilationStopped()` may lead to a deadlock. This method is called within a `synchronized(this)` block in `OptimizedCallTarget#compile` (#JANT2). When the compilation activity mode is `STOP_COMPILATION` and the queue size exceeds 1, the code iterates over all queued targets and calls `target.cancelCompilation()`. If multiple threads are attempting to compile different targets simultaneously, they may each hold their own target's lock while attempting to acquire another target's lock for cancellation, creating a circular dependency.

#RVHE9: In the `SHUTDOWN_COMPILATION` case, `runtime().getCompileQueue().shutdownAndAwaitTermination(100)` is called every time any call target attempts to compile. While `ExecutorService.shutdownNow()` is idempotent, repeatedly calling `awaitTermination` (which blocks for up to 100ms) inside a synchronized block on a call target is inefficient and may cause unnecessary contention during the shutdown phase.
