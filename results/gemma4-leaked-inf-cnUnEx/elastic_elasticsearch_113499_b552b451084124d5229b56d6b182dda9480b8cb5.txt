#P0R13: The `write` method performs a destructive read by clearing the `ignoredValues` list (`ignoredValues = List.of();`) after copying its contents into a `TreeSet`. This makes the `write` operation non-idempotent; if the synthetic source needs to be written more than once for the same document, subsequent calls will lose the ignored values. Since a `reset()` method is already implemented in `#0KA3J`, it is recommended to remove this side-effect from the `write` method and rely on the lifecycle management provided by `reset()`.
