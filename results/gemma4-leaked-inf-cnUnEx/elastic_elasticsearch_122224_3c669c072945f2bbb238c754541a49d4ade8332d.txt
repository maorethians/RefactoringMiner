#FEYS2: removes the validation check that previously prohibited nested fields when the index is in TSDB mode.

#HCC2E: modifies the nested context creation logic. For standard indices, it continues to require and copy the parent's `_id` immediately. For time-series indices, it bypasses this requirement because the ID is not yet available during initial parsing.

#1SWTS: implements the deferred assignment in `TimeSeriesIdFieldMapper#postParse`. After the time-series ID is generated and assigned to the root document, it iterates through all non-root (nested) documents and adds the encoded `_id` field. This ensures that nested documents can be correctly identified and deleted alongside their parent.
