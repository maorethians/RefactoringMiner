#9929M: The error message construction for sets of size 3 or less is non-deterministic because it appends the set directly (`errorMessage.append(e.getValue())`). Since `Set` implementations like `HashSet` do not guarantee iteration order, the resulting error message may vary across different executions. To ensure consistent and deterministic output (and to avoid requiring specific collection types in tests), consider sorting the indices for all cases, not just when the size exceeds 3.
