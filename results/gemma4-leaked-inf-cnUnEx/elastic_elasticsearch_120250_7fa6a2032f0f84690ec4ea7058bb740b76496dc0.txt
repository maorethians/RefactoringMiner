#7HSA2: The retry loop in `compareAndExchangeRegister` contains a logic flaw that can lead to an infinite loop. If a `BaseServiceException` occurs with a status code other than `RestStatus.PRECONDITION_FAILED` or `RestStatus.TOO_MANY_REQUESTS`, the `catch` block completes without returning or throwing, causing the `while (true)` loop to repeat indefinitely. Any unhandled service exceptions should be thrown to exit the loop.

However, according to the implementation in `#7HSA2`, when retries for `TOO_MANY_REQUESTS` are exhausted, a `finalException` is thrown rather than returning the current value.
