#QJ3B4: The change switches the registry passed to `ImplicitCasting.cast` from the general function registry to the `snapshotRegistry()`. Given that `SnapshotFunctionRegistry` (seen in #V3KCB) throws an `IllegalStateException` if instantiated in a non-snapshot build, there is a high risk of a `NullPointerException` or unexpected behavior in production environments if `snapshotRegistry()` returns null when not in a snapshot build. Please ensure there is a null check or a fallback registry provided for non-snapshot builds.
