#XT08W: In the methods `processDoubles`, `processInts`, and `processLongs`, the logic for the case where `valuesBlock.mvSortedAscending()` is true uses relative indices (e.g., `0`, `valueCount - 1`, `lowerIndex`) when calling `getDouble`, `getInt`, or `getLong`. According to the `DoubleBlock`/`IntBlock`/`LongBlock` API, these methods require absolute value indices. These calls should be offset by `firstValueIndex` (e.g., `valuesBlock.getDouble(firstValueIndex)` and `valuesBlock.getDouble(firstValueIndex + valueCount - 1)`).
