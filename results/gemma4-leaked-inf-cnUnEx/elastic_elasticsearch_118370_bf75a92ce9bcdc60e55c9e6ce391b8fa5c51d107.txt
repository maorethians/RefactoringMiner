#DJ9MA, #YZ9VR: The use of a `ConcurrentMap` keyed by `currentThread.threadId()` to provide per-thread `SourceProvider` instances introduces a potential memory leak. Since entries are never removed from the map, it will grow indefinitely as new threads are created and access this provider. 
A `ThreadLocal<SourceProvider>` would be a more appropriate and standard way to implement per-thread storage, as it allows the JVM to reclaim the provider when the thread is garbage collected.
