#1YCJK: The use of `Math.min(2, ...)` caps the `CENTRAL_QUEUE_CAPACITY` at a maximum of 2. This effectively ignores any value higher than 2 provided via the system property `"io.netty.allocator.centralQueueCapacity"` and limits the capacity to 2 even on systems with many available processors. This appears to contradict the accompanying Javadoc which states that the default size is `NettyRuntime#availableProcessors()`. Please verify if `Math.max` was intended to ensure a minimum capacity, or if this drastic cap is intentional.
