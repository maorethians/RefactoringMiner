#S1DAF, #CECJZ: The entity is consumed redundantly. Since `entityAsMap` now ensures that the underlying `HttpEntity` is consumed in its own `finally` block, calling `EntityUtils.consumeQuietly(response.getEntity())` in the outer `finally` block results in a second consumption attempt upon successful execution of `entityAsMap`. While `consumeQuietly` is safe to call multiple times, this redundancy can be avoided by relying on the helper method's internal resource management or following the pattern used in `assertAcknowledged`.
