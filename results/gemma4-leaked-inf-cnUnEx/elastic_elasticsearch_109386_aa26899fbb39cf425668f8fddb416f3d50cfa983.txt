#KSTPN, #8N9WN: The use of the `^` operator in `randomLongBetween(-2L ^ 50, 2L ^ 50)` is incorrect. In Java, `^` is the bitwise XOR operator, not the exponentiation operator. This results in values far smaller than the intended $2^{50}$. Use `1L << 50` or `Math.pow(2, 50)` to define these bounds.

#KSTPN: In `DoubleBucketedSortTests.collect`, the value is cast to a `long` before being passed to `sort.collect((long) value, bucket)`. Since `DoubleBucketedSort` is designed to handle `double` precision, this cast causes a loss of precision and prevents the tests from verifying the aggregator's behavior with floating-point values.
