#NCDFR: The `magazine` field was changed from `final` to non-final. Since this field is accessed and modified across different threads (e.g., in `allocateFallback` and during chunk transfers), it should be marked as `volatile` to ensure that updates made by one thread are visible to others.

#J2240: The `detachFromMagazine()` method accesses `magazine.usedMemory` before setting the reference to `null`. If this method is called on a chunk that is already detached or was never attached, it will throw a `NullPointerException`. Adding a null check for `magazine` would make this operation more robust.
