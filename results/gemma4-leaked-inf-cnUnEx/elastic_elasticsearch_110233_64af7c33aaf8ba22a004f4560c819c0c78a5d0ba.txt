#AVQCA: The constructor call within the parser lambda is missing the new `k` parameter. While the indices for `numCands` and `vectorSimilarity` have been updated to match the new parser order (shifting from 2 $\rightarrow$ 3 and 3 $\rightarrow$ 4), the value of `k` at `args[2]` is not passed to the constructor. This results in the `k` parameter being ignored whenever a `query_vector_builder` is provided in the request.
