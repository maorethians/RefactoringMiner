#AM1N5, #7W897, #3Y7BH, #SB2G4: The introduction of `hasScoring()` method improves readability by encapsulating the logic for checking if scoring is needed. However, the repeated calls to `esQueryExec.hasScoring()` in different places might lead to performance issues if `attrs().stream().anyMatch(...)` is computationally expensive. Consider caching the result of `hasScoring()` if it's called multiple times within a single execution path.
