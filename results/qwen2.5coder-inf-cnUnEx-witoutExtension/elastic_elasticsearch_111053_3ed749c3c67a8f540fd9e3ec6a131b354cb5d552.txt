#K06WJ, #HRVH7, #R9DSP: The removal of `SleepFunction<Long, Void> threadSleep` and replacing it with `Runnable onNextThread` seems to change the intended functionality. The original `threadSleep` was used to introduce a delay between capturing thread states before and after, which is crucial for measuring time differences. Replacing this with a generic `Runnable` that does nothing (`() -> {}`) in the `detect` method could lead to incorrect results as it removes the necessary sleep interval.

#J6N05: The change in the loop condition from `j < threadElementsSnapshotCount` to `j < threadElementsSnapshotCount - 1` and adding a separate call to `threadBean.getThreadInfo(threadIds, Integer.MAX_VALUE)` outside the loop could lead to inconsistent behavior if `threadElementsSnapshotCount` is less than or equal to 1. This change should be reviewed to ensure it does not introduce any edge case issues.
