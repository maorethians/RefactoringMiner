#B4Z4H: The `localRefs.mustIncRef()` call has been moved from before the client execution to inside the `processResponse` method. This change could lead to a potential race condition if the response processing is delayed or if there are multiple concurrent requests, as `localRefs` might not be incremented until after the request has been executed. Ensure that this change does not introduce any concurrency issues and that `localRefs` is properly managed in all scenarios.
