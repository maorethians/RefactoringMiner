#E9VRG, #QNFD2, #DK8TH, #4ZB6G: The constructors for `ReadOnlyByteBufferBuf`, `ReadOnlySlicedByteBuf`, and `ReadOnlyDuplicatedByteBuf` are creating new instances of buffers without ensuring that the original buffer is read-only. This could lead to unexpected behavior if the underlying buffer is modified elsewhere.

#DK8TH, #4ZB6G: The `@SuppressWarnings("deprecation")` annotation suggests that these classes might be using deprecated methods or extending deprecated classes. It would be better to address the deprecation warnings rather than suppressing them, as this could lead to issues in future versions of Netty.

#N4P8Z, #QSJFD, #85849, #M3R9Z, #7P03B, #CEVRD, #99D1G, #KE5HJ: The test cases are testing the release behavior of read-only buffers and their derived buffers.
