#MPRDA, #A64EE, #JD11Y, #8EKZM, #SSDQS, #CT6E4, #CJHR1, #BSE7X: The introduction of `RATE_LIMIT_ASSIGNMENT_MAX_WAIT_TIME_IN_SECONDS` and the `waitForRateLimitingAssignments` method is a good step towards ensuring that tests wait for rate limiting assignments to be correctly set up. However, the use of `assertBusy` with a fixed timeout might not be ideal in all scenarios. This approach can lead to flaky tests if the assignment does not occur within the specified time frame due to external factors or increased load. Consider using a more dynamic waiting mechanism that checks for the condition periodically and times out only after a reasonable number of attempts.
