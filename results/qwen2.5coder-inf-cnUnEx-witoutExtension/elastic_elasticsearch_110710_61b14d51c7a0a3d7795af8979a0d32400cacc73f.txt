#6V49M: The `readNodesFrom` and `writeNodesTo` methods in `NodesFeaturesResponse` both call `TransportAction.localOnly()`, which does not make sense as these methods are expected to read from or write to a stream. This should be replaced with actual logic to read/write the nodes.

#W4C82: The `NodeFeaturesFixupListener` class has a retry mechanism that schedules retries on failure. However, there is no limit to the number of retries, which could lead to infinite retries in case of persistent failures. Consider adding a maximum retry count or exponential backoff strategy.
