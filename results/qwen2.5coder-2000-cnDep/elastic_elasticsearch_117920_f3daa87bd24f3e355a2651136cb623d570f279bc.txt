#B4JT9, #94Q6X: The use of `shutdownNow()` followed by `awaitTermination()` is a good approach to ensure that the worker threads are stopped promptly and resources are released. However, the timeout value of 30 seconds might be too short or too long depending on the context. It would be beneficial to make this configurable or at least document why 30 seconds was chosen.

#B4JT9: The `InterruptedException` is caught and handled by interrupting the current thread again and logging an informational message. This is generally correct, but it might be worth considering whether additional actions should be taken in response to the interruption, such as retrying the shutdown or notifying other components of the system.

#K33S7: The removal of `handleAlreadyShuttingDownWorker()` simplifies the code by removing a method that was only called in one place. However, it is important to ensure that the logic previously handled by this method (notifying queue runnables) is still correctly applied elsewhere, particularly in `signalAndWaitForWorkerTermination()`. This change should be verified with tests to confirm that no functionality is lost.
