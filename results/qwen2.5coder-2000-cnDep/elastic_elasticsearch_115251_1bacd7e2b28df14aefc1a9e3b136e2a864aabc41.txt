#82926, #D8GXG: The logic for filtering settings now includes checking against prefixes and explicitly denied settings. However, there is a potential issue with the `EXPLICITLY_DENIED_SETTINGS` check. In `#82926`, the condition `(ALLOWED_SETTING_KEYS.contains(setting) == false && ALLOWLED_SETTINGS_PREFIXES.stream().noneMatch(prefix -> setting.startsWith(prefix + "."))) || EXPLICITLY_DENIED_SETTINGS.contains(setting)` might incorrectly include settings that are explicitly denied but start with an allowed prefix. Similarly, in `#D8GXG`, the condition `(ALLOWED_SETTING_KEYS.contains(setting) || ALLOWLED_SETTINGS_PREFIXES.stream().anyMatch(prefix -> setting.startsWith(prefix + "."))) && EXPLICITLY_DENIED_SETTINGS.contains(setting) == false` might exclude settings that are explicitly denied but should be included based on the prefix check. This could lead to incorrect filtering of settings.
