#21N6P: The change introduces a new condition to include templates that have a non-null `DataStreamTemplate`. However, this might lead to unexpected behavior because it bypasses the check for hidden indices when `isHidden` is true. This could result in data streams being matched by templates intended only for regular indices.

#CRDNJ: The test case `testDataStreamsUsingMatchAllTemplate` covers a scenario where a match-all template is used for both visible and hidden indices, which is good. However, it does not cover cases where there might be other templates that could conflict with the match-all template or scenarios where the data stream name does not match any pattern explicitly defined in other templates.
