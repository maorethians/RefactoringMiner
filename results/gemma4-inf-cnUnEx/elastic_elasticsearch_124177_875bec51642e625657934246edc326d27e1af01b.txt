#QJ0QW, #G9ZVN, #1PP4T, #2FTYC: The constants `BEFORE_REGEX`, `AFTER_REGEX`, `BEFORE_REPLACEMENT`, `AFTER_REPLACEMENT` and the compiled patterns `REPLACE_LP` and `REPLACE_LEFT_BRACKET` are declared as instance fields. Since these values are constant and do not depend on the state of the `EsqlParser` instance, they should be declared as `static final`. This avoids redundant string allocations and expensive regex pattern recompilation every time a new parser is instantiated.
