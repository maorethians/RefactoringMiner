#B5C3T: The transition from `MetadataCreateIndexService` to `baseClient.admin().indices().create()` omits the explicit timeouts used in the previous implementation (`TimeValue.MINUS_ONE` for master node timeout and `TimeValue.ZERO` for ack timeout). Additionally, the previous call passed `null` as the `waitForActiveShardsTimeout`, which typically indicates an infinite wait. Since these are critical migration tasks, omitting these settings may lead to unexpected timeouts during cluster state updates or shard activation in slow environments. Consider explicitly setting these timeouts on the `CreateIndexRequest` to maintain parity with the original logic (as seen in diff #J5MBC).
