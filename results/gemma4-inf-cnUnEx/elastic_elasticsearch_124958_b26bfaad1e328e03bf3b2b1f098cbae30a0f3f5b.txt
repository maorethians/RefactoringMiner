#FS8TJ: The test method `testUnclosedParenthesis` is misnamed; the provided test cases (`"row a = )"`, `"row ]"`, and `"from source | eval x = [1,2,3]]"`) all contain extra closing delimiters rather than unclosed ones. A more accurate name would be `testExtraClosingDelimiters` or `testExtraClosingParenthesis`.
