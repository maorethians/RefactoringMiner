#7JRNV: The fallback logic that sets `pullOffset = brokerOffset` when no offset is found (i.e., `pullOffset < 0`) has been removed. This creates an inconsistency with the similar logic in `getConsumerLagStats` (#2B020) and `getInFlightMsgStats` (#1HKDY), where this fallback is preserved. If `pullOffset` remains -1, subsequent lag calculations may produce incorrect results (e.g., reporting a lag of `brokerOffset + 1` instead of 0).
