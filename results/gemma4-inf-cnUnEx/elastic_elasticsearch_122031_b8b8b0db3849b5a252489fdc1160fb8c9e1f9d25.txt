#KM0Z6: The nesting of two `assertBusy` calls is logically redundant and creates an unpredictable retry mechanism. Since the inner `assertBusy` only returns after succeeding or timing out (10 seconds), the outer loop's exponential backoff will be applied to 10-second blocks of time rather than individual checks. This makes the actual total wait time significantly different from the intended 1 minute and complicates the test logic. A single `assertBusy` call with a 1-minute timeout would be more appropriate.
Additionally, placing the `logger.warn` and the call to `hotThreads()` inside the inner `assertBusy` loop will cause log flooding. Because `assertBusy` retries rapidly (starting at 1ms intervals), the logs will be saturated with "search still running" messages and repeated network requests to `/_nodes/hot_threads` every time a check fails, rather than providing occasional diagnostic snapshots.
