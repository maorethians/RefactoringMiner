#1VVZJ: The use of `ctx.getParent().getText()` in the `ParsingException` may lead to a `NullPointerException` if `ctx` is the root node of the parse tree. Additionally, since this exception is thrown when the expression depth is very high, calling `.getText()` on the parent node might capture an excessively large portion of the query, potentially leading to oversized error messages or memory pressure during string concatenation. Consider using `ctx.getText()` or a truncated version of the context.
