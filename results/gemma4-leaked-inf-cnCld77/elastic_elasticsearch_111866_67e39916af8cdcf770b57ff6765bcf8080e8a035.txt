#D8RX9: In the Docker path of `configureAndRun`, the result of `runContainer(distribution(), builder)` is ignored. Since this method returns a new `Installation` and starts a new container, failing to assign it to the `installation` field means that subsequent calls to `startElasticsearch()` and `stopElasticsearch()` will operate on the original installation created during `install()`, while the newly created container will be leaked.

#D8RX9: In the Docker path of `configureAndRun`, calling `startElasticsearch()` after `runContainer(...)` is redundant, as `runContainer` already starts the Elasticsearch process and verifies its startup.
