#ZHWJ6: The current implementation of `findRedundantSort` only traverses the left side of a `Join` operation:
```java
} else if (p instanceof Join lj) {
    p = lj.left();
    // TODO do it also on the right-hand side?
    continue;
}
```
While lookup joins are often asymmetric, any sorting operation on the right side of a join that is subsequently overridden by a final sort at the root of the plan would also be redundant. For completeness and to maximize optimization, this rule should eventually be extended to traverse both sides of the join.
