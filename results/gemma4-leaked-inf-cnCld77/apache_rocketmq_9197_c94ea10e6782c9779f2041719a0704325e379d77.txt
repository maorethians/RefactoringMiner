#QYNZX: The use of a manually created `CompletableFuture` (`future0`) inside `thenCompose` to wrap synchronous logic is an anti-pattern. Since the response processing and header decoding are synchronous operations, this should be replaced with `.thenApply()`. This would eliminate the need for manual completion calls and the explicit `try-catch` block, as exceptions thrown within `thenApply` are automatically captured by the resulting future.
