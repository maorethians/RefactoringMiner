#9T1V4: The cast `(int) limitSource.fold()` assumes that the limit expression has been folded into an `Integer`. While limits in ESQL are typically constants, if `fold()` returns a different type or `null` (e.g., for a non-constant expression), this will trigger a `ClassCastException` or `NullPointerException`. It is recommended to verify the result of `fold()` before casting or use a safer conversion method.

#P1YFQ, #H9HS1: The internal `limit` field in `MvExpand` is explicitly excluded from serialization (`assert limit == null` in `writeTo` and set to `null` in the `StreamInput` constructor). This implies that the "inner limit" optimization is intended only for the coordinator during the mapping phase. If logical plans are ever serialized and reconstructed on workers or other nodes, this optimization will be lost. Given the current architecture where the coordinator typically handles the `Mapper`, this is likely acceptable, but it creates a dependency on the plan being processed by the coordinator's optimizer before execution.
