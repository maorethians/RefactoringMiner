#TZV12, #N4H71: The fields `stoppedCompilationTime` and `logShutdownCompilations` are accessed and modified across multiple threads within `submitForCompilation`, but they are not declared as `volatile`. This can lead to visibility issues where different threads see inconsistent states, potentially resulting in the shutdown warning being logged multiple times or the compilation retry timer behaving unpredictably.

#N4H71: In the case where `compilationActivityMode` is `SHUTDOWN_COMPILATION`, the method calls `queue.shutdownAndAwaitTermination(100)` on every invocation. Since `submitForCompilation` can be called frequently, repeatedly attempting to shut down the queue and awaiting termination—even if the executor has already terminated—introduces unnecessary overhead and potential latency in a hot path. This shutdown sequence should be triggered only once when transitioning to the shutdown state.
