#7HSA2: The retry loop in `compareAndExchangeRegister` will result in an infinite loop if a `BaseServiceException` is thrown with a status code other than `PRECONDITION_FAILED` (412) or `TOO_MANY_REQUESTS` (429). In such cases, the exception is neither thrown nor returned, causing the `while(true)` loop to repeat indefinitely. A fallback `throw e;` or similar mechanism should be added for unhandled status codes.
