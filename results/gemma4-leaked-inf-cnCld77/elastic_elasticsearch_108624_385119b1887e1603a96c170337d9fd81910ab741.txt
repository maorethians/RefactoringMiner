#J8TT4: The implementation of `hasRollupIndices` iterates through all indices in the cluster state and parses their mapping sources to detect rollup metadata. While the use of a filtered parser avoids materializing the entire mapping into memory, this operation remains $O(N)$ relative to the number of indices in the cluster. In environments with a very large number of indices, this could lead to significant latency during the creation of the first rollup job. Given that this is a deprecation check intended to run only when no active jobs exist, the performance impact may be acceptable, but it is worth noting as a potential bottleneck for clusters with massive index counts.
