#QJ3B4: The change switches the registry passed to `ImplicitCasting.cast` from the general `context.functionRegistry()` to specifically the `snapshotRegistry()`. 
Since the string literal conversion map (`dataTypesForStringLiteralConversions`) has been changed from a static shared map (#W7EX6) to an instance-based map, and the `SnapshotFunctionRegistry` only populates this map with snapshot-specific functions (#V3KCB), any standard functions passed to `ImplicitCasting.cast` will no longer find their corresponding conversion types. This effectively breaks implicit casting for all non-snapshot functions.
