#1TYMH, #7JDG5, #NVNXD: The refactoring to a dispatcher pattern (`RealMockAppender`) breaks the hierarchical nature of Log4j logging for mock appenders. In the previous implementation, adding an appender to a logger (e.g., "org.elasticsearch") would automatically capture all logs from that logger and its descendants (e.g., "org.elasticsearch.Node"). The new implementation uses a `ConcurrentHashMap` lookup based on the exact logger name (`event.getLoggerName()`), meaning it only captures logs from the specific loggers registered in the map, ignoring any child loggers.

#QQR2R: The use of `Class::getCanonicalName` may return `null` for certain types of classes (such as local or anonymous classes). This could lead to a `NullPointerException` when attempting to use the result as a key in the `mockAppenders` map within `#NVNXD`.
