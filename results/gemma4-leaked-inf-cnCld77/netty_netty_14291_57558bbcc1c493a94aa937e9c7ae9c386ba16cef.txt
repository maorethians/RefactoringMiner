#6RFM3, #EBSWB: The implementation of chunk reuse in `HeapArena` caches a `PoolChunk` via `lastDestroyedChunk` and returns it in `newChunk`. However, there is no mechanism to reset the internal state of the reused `PoolChunk` (e.g., its `runsAvail` map, `subpages` array, and initial run configuration). Since `PoolChunk` maintains complex bookkeeping state from its previous lifecycle, reusing it without a full reset will lead to corrupted memory management or fragmented allocations in the new lifecycle.
