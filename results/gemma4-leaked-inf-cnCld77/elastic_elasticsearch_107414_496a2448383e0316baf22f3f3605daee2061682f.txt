#QGZHP: The assertions in `testMultiCountAllWithFilter` have been significantly reduced. The previous version verified the entire physical plan structure, including the specific Elasticsearch query generated (e.g., the range filter on `emp_no`) and the aggregation mode. Replacing these detailed checks with a single `anyMatch(EsQueryExec.class::isInstance)` removes almost all regression testing for this scenario, as it only verifies that some execution node of type `EsQueryExec` exists in the plan without validating its correctness or configuration.
