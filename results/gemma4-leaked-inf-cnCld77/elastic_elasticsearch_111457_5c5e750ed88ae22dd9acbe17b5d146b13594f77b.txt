#NPN19: The call to `.dimension(fieldType().isDimension())` is redundant. The `dimension` parameter is already initialized with a lambda (`m -> toType(m).fieldType().isDimension()`) in the `Builder` constructor, and since it is included in the list returned by `getParameters()` (see #N8PRC), the subsequent call to `.init(this)` will automatically set its value using that initializer.
