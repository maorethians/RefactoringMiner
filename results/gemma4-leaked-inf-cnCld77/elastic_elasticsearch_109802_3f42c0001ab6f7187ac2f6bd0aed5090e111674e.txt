#ASX49, #BMM83: The changes replace the failure reporting mechanism (`respond.onFailure`) with a success response that indicates no refresh occurred (`respond.onSuccess(false)`). While this prevents refresh failures from failing the overall write operation, the exception `e` in `#BMM83` is now silently discarded without being logged or reported. This loss of observability makes it difficult to diagnose and debug the root causes of refresh failures in production.
