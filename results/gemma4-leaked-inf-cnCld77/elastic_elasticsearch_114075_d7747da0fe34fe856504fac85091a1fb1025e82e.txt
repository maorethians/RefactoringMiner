#47TC7: The call to `Sets.difference(clustersRequested, clustersWithResolvedIndices)` (assuming it uses `com.google.common.collect.Sets`) returns an unmodifiable view of the difference between the two sets. Calling `.removeAll()` on this result will throw an `UnsupportedOperationException` at runtime. To fix this, wrap the result in a new mutable set, for example:
`Set<String> clustersWithNoMatchingIndices = new HashSet<>(Sets.difference(clustersRequested, clustersWithResolvedIndices));`
