#1YCJK: The use of `Math.min(2, ...)` imposes a hard cap of 2 on the `CENTRAL_QUEUE_CAPACITY`. This effectively overrides the `io.netty.allocator.centralQueueCapacity` system property for any value greater than 2 and ignores the scaling provided by `NettyRuntime.availableProcessors()`. This may lead to performance bottlenecks or increased contention on systems with high core counts where a larger central queue would be beneficial.
