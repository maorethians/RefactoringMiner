#CJW50, #DSA54: The `makeNullable` helper method has been removed (`#DSA54`), and the previous (albeit commented-out) attempts to use it in `computeOutput` have been replaced by a call to `mergeOutputAttributes` (`#CJW50`). There is no indication that `mergeOutputAttributes` handles nullability. In a `LEFT` or `RIGHT` join, attributes from the non-driving side must be marked as nullable to correctly reflect that a match may not exist; removing this logic without a replacement is a regression in correctness.

#CJW50: The call `mergeOutputAttributes(rightOutputWithoutMatchFields, leftOutput)` for `LEFT` joins (and the inverse for `RIGHT` joins) replaces previous logic (`#CF847`) that explicitly placed the driving side's attributes first in the output list. Depending on whether `mergeOutputAttributes` prepends or appends its first argument, this change may have reversed the expected column order of the join result.
