#F1NZM, #C0JFJ: The removal of the hardcoded addition of `IgnoredSourceFieldMapper.NAME` to `requiredStoredFields` in `SourceLoader.Synthetic` (#F1NZM), combined with adding a conditional load only within `NestedObjectMapper` (#C0JFJ), appears to create a regression for top-level synthetic sources. 
Previously, every synthetic source loader ensured that the `_ignored_source` field was loaded from Lucene. Now, this logic is moved into a specific check in `NestedObjectMapper`. Unless other components (such as the top-level `MapperService` or general synthetic source construction paths) also implement a similar call to `IgnoredSourceFieldMapper.ensureLoaded`, any non-nested fields that rely on the ignored source will no longer be loaded during synthetic source construction, leading to missing data in the resulting source.
