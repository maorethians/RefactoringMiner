#P1YFQ, #H9HS1: The `limit` field is not serialized in `writeTo` and is explicitly set to `null` when reading from `StreamInput`. This means the internal limit optimization is lost if the logical plan is ever serialized and deserialized (e.g., for caching or debugging). Furthermore, the assertion `assert limit == null` in `writeTo` will cause crashes in environments where assertions are enabled whenever an optimized plan containing a limited `MvExpand` is serialized.

#9T1V4: The cast `(int) limitSource.fold()` assumes that the limit expression always evaluates to an integer. While limits are typically constants in ESQL, this could lead to a `ClassCastException` if the system ever allows non-constant expressions or different numeric types for limits.
