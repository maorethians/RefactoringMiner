#ZX353, #6RMC7, #SZ1KN: Changing the signature of `getAge()` to require a `Supplier<Long>` is unnecessary and breaks the existing API. Since a `nowSupplier` field is already introduced in `#Q5ADF`, `getAge()` should maintain its original no-argument signature and internally use the `nowSupplier` field. This would simplify the call sites in `toXContent` (#6RMC7) and the tests (#SZ1KN), as they would no longer need to explicitly pass the supplier.
