#0KR13: The `BEFORE_REGEX` pattern `([\\s,])*` uses a capturing group inside a quantifier. In Java, this results in the group only capturing the last character matched by the repetition. If multiple spaces or commas precede "LP" or "OPENING_BRACKET", all but the final character will be discarded during replacement. To preserve the entire prefix, move the quantifier inside the group: `([\\s,]*)`.
