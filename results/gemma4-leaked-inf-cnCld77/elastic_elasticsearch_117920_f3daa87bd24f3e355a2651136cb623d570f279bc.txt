#B4JT9, #94Q6X: The removal of `handleAlreadyShuttingDownWorker()` (and the associated check for `priorityProcessWorker.isShutdown()`) introduces a regression where queued tasks are no longer notified of failure or rejection when the process is stopped. 
In `AbstractProcessWorkerExecutorService`, neither `shutdown()` nor `shutdownNow()` notify pending runnables; this logic is exclusively contained within `notifyQueueRunnables()`. By removing the calls to the helper method that triggered this notification, tasks remaining in the work queue may be left hanging without a response. 
Specifically:
- In `#B4JT9`, `priorityProcessWorker.shutdownNow()` should be followed by `priorityProcessWorker.notifyQueueRunnables()` to ensure all forcefully stopped tasks are failed.
- In `#94Q6X`, if the worker is already shut down, `signalAndWaitForWorkerTermination()` will likely return normally without calling `notifyQueueRunnables()`, leaving existing queued tasks unnotified.
