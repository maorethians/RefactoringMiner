#V572M: The call to `listener.onFailure` should be followed by a `return` statement. While the subsequent conditional blocks in this method are logically unreachable when `numClusters() == 0`, it is standard practice and safer to terminate execution immediately after completing an `ActionListener` to prevent any possibility of multiple responses being sent to the same listener.

#3TCQA: The timeout for `latch.await` has been reduced from 10 seconds to 1 millisecond. This is excessively low and is likely to cause flaky tests in CI environments where thread scheduling can introduce slight delays, even for synchronous mock-based calls. It is recommended to use a more reasonable timeout (e.g., 1 second).
