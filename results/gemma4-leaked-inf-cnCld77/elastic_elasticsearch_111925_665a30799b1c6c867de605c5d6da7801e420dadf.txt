#MQW4Z: The assignment to `floatArraySupplier` persists across iterations of the loop. Since `COSINE` is the first element in the list, all subsequent similarity types (`DOT_PRODUCT`, `EUCLIDEAN`, etc.) will use `FLOAT_ARRAY_RANDOM_FUNC` instead of the original supplier. This likely defeats the purpose of the conditional check; consider using a local variable for the current iteration's supplier instead of overwriting the shared one.
