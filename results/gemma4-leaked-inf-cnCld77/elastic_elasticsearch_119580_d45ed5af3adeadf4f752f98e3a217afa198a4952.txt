#VM6KA, #ZT1P3, #09G9D, #CBDSE, #9P1Y7, #41B5F, #VZS6J, #QT9Z0, #SYVR4: The refactor to replace `EsIndex` with its components (`indexName` and `indexNameWithModes`) is inconsistently applied to `EsStatsQueryExec`. While `EsRelation`, `EsQueryExec`, and `EsSourceExec` now store both the index name and the map of index modes, `EsStatsQueryExec` only stores the `indexName`. This results in a loss of metadata that was previously available via the `EsIndex` object and deviates from the pattern established in the other physical operators.
