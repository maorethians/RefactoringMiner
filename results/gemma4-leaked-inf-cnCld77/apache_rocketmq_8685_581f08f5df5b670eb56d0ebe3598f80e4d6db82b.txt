#K57D7: The introduction of the early `return` when `prevCW == null` prevents `RemotingHelper.closeChannel(channel)` from being executed. In the previous implementation, the channel was closed regardless of whether it was found in the internal tables. This change may lead to resource leaks (unclosed sockets) if this method is relied upon to ensure the channel is terminated.

#E7JNC, #JB0F4: The removal of `lastReconnectTimestamp` and its associated time-interval check in `reconnect()` eliminates the rate-limiting mechanism for reconnections. Without a minimum interval between attempts, the client may enter a tight loop of reconnection attempts during periods of network instability or when the server is consistently rejecting connections, potentially leading to high CPU usage and network congestion.
