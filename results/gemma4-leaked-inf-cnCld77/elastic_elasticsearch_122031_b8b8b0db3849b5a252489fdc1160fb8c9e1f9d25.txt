#KM0Z6: The `logger.warn` call is placed inside an `assertBusy` block. Since `assertBusy` employs an exponential backoff starting at 1ms, this will cause a flood of log entries and redundant network requests to `/_nodes/hot_threads` (via the `hotThreads()` method) in the first few seconds if searches are still running. This could potentially overwhelm the logs and the cluster under test.

#KM0Z6: The nested `assertBusy` structure is redundant and confusing. The outer loop (1 minute) wraps an inner loop (10 seconds). Because the inner loop only throws an exception after its own timeout has expired, this creates a convoluted retry mechanism that effectively just extends the total wait time. A single `assertBusy` call with a 1-minute timeout would be more readable and achieve the same result.
