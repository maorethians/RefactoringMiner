#EJWNX, #2XFNV: There is a unit mismatch when assigning the `maxTaskProcessingQuantumMs` parameter to the `maxTaskProcessingQuantumNs` field. The parameter is provided in milliseconds, but it is assigned directly to a nanosecond field without conversion (e.g., via `TimeUnit.MILLISECONDS.toNanos()`). This will result in the task processing quantum being one million times smaller than intended when configured via these constructors.
