#NCDFR: The `magazine` field has been changed from `final` to a mutable field. Since this field is accessed and modified across different threads (e.g., during chunk transfer between magazines or when retrieving the magazine in `allocateFallback`), it should be marked as `volatile` to ensure visibility of updates across threads. Without `volatile`, threads may see stale values, potentially leading to incorrect fallback magazine selection or inconsistent memory accounting.
