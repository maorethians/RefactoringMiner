#DTQFH: The call to `brokerController.getConsumerOffsetManager().commitPullOffset(...)` is placed inside the `if (brokerController.getBrokerConfig().isPopConsumerKVServiceEnable())` block. Since updating the consumer offset manager is a general operation for synchronizing offsets on the broker and not specific to Pop consumers, this should likely be moved outside of that conditional block so that standard pull consumers also have their offsets updated during a reset.
