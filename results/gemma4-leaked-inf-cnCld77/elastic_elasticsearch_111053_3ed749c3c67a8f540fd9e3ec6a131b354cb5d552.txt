#K06WJ, #HRVH7: The `onNextThread` Runnable is invoked multiple times within `innerDetect`, but it is passed through a public method `detect(Writer writer, Runnable onNextThread)` without any null validation. If a caller passes `null` for the `onNextThread` argument, a `NullPointerException` will be thrown during execution. Consider adding a null check before calling `.run()` or providing a default no-op implementation in the public `detect` method.
