#J547T, #F10MQ: #### ❌ Logic Error in Tests (Bitwise XOR vs Power)
**Diff IDs:** #J547T, #F10MQ
In `randomValue()`, the expression `-2L ^ 50` and `2L ^ 50` uses the bitwise XOR operator (`^`) instead of calculating a power of 2. This results in values between -48 and 48 rather than the intended range of $\pm 2^{50}$. Use `1L << 50` or `Math.pow(2, 50)` to achieve the desired magnitude.

#J547T: #### ❌ Precision Loss in Tests
**Diff ID:** #J547T
In `DoubleBucketedSortTests.collect`, the `value` (a `double`) is cast to a `long` before being passed to `sort.collect`. This causes truncation of decimal parts, which defeats the purpose of testing a `DoubleBucketedSort` and may hide bugs related to floating-point precision.
