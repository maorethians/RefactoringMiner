#GD6NB: In `processDoubles`, `processInts`, and `processLongs`, the logic within the `if (valuesBlock.mvSortedAscending())` block uses absolute indices instead of offsetting them by `firstValueIndex`. 
Specifically, calls such as `valuesBlock.getDouble(0)`, `valuesBlock.getDouble(valueCount - 1)`, `valuesBlock.getDouble(lowerIndex)`, and `valuesBlock.getDouble(upperIndex)` (and their `Int`/`Long` equivalents) will access the wrong data for any row where `firstValueIndex` is not zero. These should be updated to use `firstValueIndex + index`.
