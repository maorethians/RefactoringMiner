#6DKJ7: The constructor now hardcodes `Priority.IMMEDIATE` in the call to `super()`, removing the `priority` parameter. This reduces the flexibility of `ClusterUpdateSettingsTask` by preventing callers from specifying a different priority for the task, which may be problematic if different priority levels are required in future use cases.

#JVZR1, #A9XWN: The `threadContextUpdater` adds a header to the current thread's context via `putHeader`, but there is no corresponding logic to remove this header after the task execution completes. Since worker threads in the thread pool are reused, this may lead to "context leaking," where subsequent unrelated tasks executed on the same thread inherit an incorrect `X-Opaque-Id`.
