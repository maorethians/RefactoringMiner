#SB2G4: The logic used to detect if a physical plan has scoring is duplicated between the check for `EsQueryExec` and the nested check for `EsRelation`. To improve maintainability and consistency, consider adding a `hasScoring()` method to `EsRelation` (similar to the one added to `EsQueryExec` in #AM1N5) so that the session class does not need to know the internal details of how scoring is identified via `MetadataAttribute.SCORE`.
