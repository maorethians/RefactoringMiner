#0X6BJ: The change from `AttributeMap<Expression>` to `ArrayList<Attribute>` for the `resolved` collection removes the implicit deduplication of attributes. If multiple expressions in `groupings` resolve to the same `Attribute`, that attribute will now be added multiple times to the `resolvedList`. This may lead to duplicate attributes in the plan's output, which could cause issues in downstream execution or analysis stages. Consider using a `Set` (e.g., `LinkedHashSet` to preserve order) instead of an `ArrayList`.
