#HB7JH, #KE236, #0R09S: The addition of `StringUtils.isNotBlank(request.getGroup().getName())` checks for a non-blank group name before adding the resource and permission. This is a good practice to avoid processing empty or null group names. However, it should be ensured that `request.getGroup()` itself is not null to prevent potential `NullPointerException`. Consider adding a null check for `request.getGroup()` as well.
