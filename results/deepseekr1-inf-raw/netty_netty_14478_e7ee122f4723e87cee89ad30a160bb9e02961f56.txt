#SFBX4, #CSM40, #2FK1B, #YYZNY, #57SFQ, #AQTZM: The changes to `sendSegmentNumber` and `receiveSegmentNumber` from `int` to `long` and the introduction of `incrementUintSegmentNumber` are correct for handling large sequence numbers. However, in `TCPPacket.writePacket()`, casting `long` to `int` for `segmentNumber` and `ackNumber` can cause integer overflow and wrap-around issues. Consider masking with `0xFFFFFFFFL` instead of casting to `int`.

#HCNCS, #95ZHS, #F33SB: The new test `writePcapGreaterThan4Gb()` attempts to test large data handling but may not fully validate sequence number wrap-around. The test sends 4GB of data but doesn't explicitly check if sequence numbers correctly wrap beyond `0xFFFFFFFFL`. Adding assertions to verify sequence number wrapping would improve coverage.
