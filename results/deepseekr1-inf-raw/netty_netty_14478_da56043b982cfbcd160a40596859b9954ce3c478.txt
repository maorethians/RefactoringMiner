#SFBX4: The changes promote the use of `long` for TCP sequence numbers, which is correct given the potential size of sequence numbers. However, there are a few issues to address:
1. **Double Increment in `sendSegmentNumber`**  
   In `PcapWriteHandler.java`, the `sendSegmentNumber` is incremented twice in the same operation, leading to an over-increment.  
   - File: `handler/src/main/java/io/netty/handler/pcap/PcapWriteHandler.java`  
   - Lines: 587-592  
   - **Fix**: Remove the duplicate increment of `sendSegmentNumber`.

#2FK1B: The test `writePcapGreaterThan4Gb` uses `0xFFFFFFFFL` to represent 4GB, which is incorrect as it equals 4,294,967,295 instead of 4,294,967,296.  
   - File: `handler/src/test/java/io/netty/handler/pcap/PcapWriteHandlerTest.java`  
   - Lines: 303-304  
   - **Fix**: Update `fourGB` to `0x100000000L` to correctly represent 4GB.

#CSM40: The helper method `incrementUintSegmentNumber` is not consistently used when updating sequence numbers. Direct additions are less safe and less consistent.  
   - Affected diffs: #2FK1B, #CSM40  
   - **Fix**: Use `incrementUintSegmentNumber` in all sequence number updates for consistency and safety.
