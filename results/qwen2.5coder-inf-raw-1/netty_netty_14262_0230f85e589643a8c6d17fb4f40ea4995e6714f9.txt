#WEE4R, #HEJ1K, #088Y1, #7SDG8, #V5TZD, #ZP0MS, #00D4K, #QTTWR, #574CJ, #6P6CR: The new fields `pooledChunkAllocations` and `pooledChunkDeallocations` are introduced to track chunk allocations and deallocations. However, these fields are not thread-safe as they are incremented without synchronization. Since the class already uses a lock for other operations, it is important to ensure that these increments are also protected by the same lock mechanism.
