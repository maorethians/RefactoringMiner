#WEE4R, #HEJ1K, #088Y1, #7SDG8, #V5TZD, #ZP0MS, #82DPX, #YZSSV, #J1W87, #6P6CR: The new fields `pooledChunkAllocations` and `pooledChunkDeallocations` are introduced to track chunk allocations and deallocations. However, these fields are not thread-safe as they are not atomic or guarded by any synchronization mechanism other than the lock in the methods that access them. This could lead to inconsistent values if accessed concurrently.

#HEJ1K, #088Y1: The increment operations on `pooledChunkAllocations` and `pooledChunkDeallocations` should be inside a locked block to ensure thread safety. Currently, they are incremented outside of the lock, which could lead to race conditions.
