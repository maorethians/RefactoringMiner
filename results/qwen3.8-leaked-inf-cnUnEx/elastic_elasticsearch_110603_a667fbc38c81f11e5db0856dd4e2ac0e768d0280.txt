One minor observation: the `TIMESTAMP_MULTIPLE_VALUES_FLAG` is created as a `StoredField` with `new byte[0]`. Since `onlyAddKey` explicitly does *not* add the field to the Lucene document's field list (per the `LuceneDocument` contract), the `StoredField` type is never actually persisted. However, using a `StoredField` here is slightly misleading to readers who might assume it gets stored. A `LongField(key, 0L, Field.Store.NO)` or a simple marker would better signal "this is just an in-memory flag." This is cosmetic, not a bug.
