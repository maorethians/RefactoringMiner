The early fetch of `now` avoids redundant `nowSupplier` calls. No correctness issues spotted.

#285WR: The test's `statusInterval` is `randomLongBetween(1, 10)` nanoseconds while `tickTime` is `randomLongBetween(10000, 100000)` nanoseconds. This guarantees `now > nextStatus` after every single iteration, so `unreportedIterations` is always exactly 1 when reported. The test never exercises the case where multiple iterations accumulate between periodic status updates (i.e., `unreportedIterations > 1`), which is the primary scenario the delta-based reporting is designed for.
Consider adding a case (or a second test) where `statusInterval > tickTime * iterationsPerTick`, so that the periodic-update branch fires less frequently than every iteration and the accumulated `unreportedIterations` is greater than 1. This would verify that the reset logic (`unreportedIterations = 0; lastStatusUpdateTime = now;`) correctly partitions the total across multiple status updates.
Additionally, the test does not cover the two WAITING exit paths (`currentIteration >= maxIterations` and `now - startTime >= maxTimeNanos`). A test that sets `maxIterations` smaller than the available pages (or `maxTime` very small) would confirm the delta is reported correctly on those early-exit paths.
