#PWYN9: This change in `TransportFindFieldStructureAction#doExecute` submits the heavy work via `threadPool.generic().execute(ActionRunnable.supply(...))` directly, while the two sibling transport actions (`TransportFindMessageStructureAction` and `TransportFindStructureAction`) route the same pattern through `TextStructExecutor.execute(...)`. This creates two problems:
1. **Bypasses the workaround abstraction.** When the workaround for issue #97916 is removed, the TODO in `TextStructExecutor` says to change callers to `ActionListener.completeWith(...)`. This call site won't be covered by that migration because it never went through `TextStructExecutor`.
2. **Inconsistent constructor wiring.** The other two classes switch their super-constructor argument from `threadPool.generic()` to `executor.handledTransportActionExecutorService()`. `TransportFindFieldStructureAction` is not updated here, so its `doExecute` still runs on the generic pool rather than the transport thread, making the threading model diverge from its siblings.
Consider injecting `TextStructExecutor` into `TransportFindFieldStructureAction` as well and replacing the inline `threadPool.generic().execute(ActionRunnable.supply(...))` with `executor.execute(delegate, () -> buildTextStructureResponse(messages, request))`, and updating the super-constructor call to `executor.handledTransportActionExecutorService()`.
