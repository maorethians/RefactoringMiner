#TV1TT, #QSYG9, #8XD02: The change from `randomIntBetween(0, 20)` to `randomIntBetween(1, 1000)` removes the zero-element boundary case from the test. Since this is `testEqualsAndHashCode`, the empty-collection scenario (zero doubles added) is a meaningful boundary condition — `equals()` and `hashCode()` implementations sometimes handle empty state differently (e.g., a cached hash of 0 vs. a computed hash, or a null vs. empty internal list). If the intent was to avoid a flaky or failing zero-element case, it would be better to keep a dedicated assertion for the empty case (e.g., explicitly test two fresh `StatsSummary` objects with no data) rather than silently dropping that path from the randomized test.
