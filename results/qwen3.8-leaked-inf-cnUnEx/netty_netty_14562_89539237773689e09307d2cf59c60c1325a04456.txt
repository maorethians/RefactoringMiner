#AJDKX: The message format here ("exceeded … bytes: …") deviates slightly from the pattern established by the other diffs in this chapter ("exceeds <limit>: <actual>"). Specifically, it uses past tense "exceeded" vs. present tense "exceeds", and appends the unit "bytes" only to the limit but not to the actual value. This is a very minor stylistic inconsistency and not a functional issue, but if the team wants a uniform message format across all `TooLongFrameException` sites, this one could be aligned (e.g., `"HTTP content length exceeds " + maxContentLength + " bytes: " + spdyDataFrame.content().readableBytes() + " bytes"`).
