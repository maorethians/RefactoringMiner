If a `Join` node within a fragment can ever have a non-lookup data source on its right side (e.g., a future regular/equi-join feature), `planConcreteIndices` would silently omit those indices. The current LOOKUP JOIN design makes this safe, but the method name `forEachFromRelation` and its javadoc ("ignores the right side of joins") make the assumption explicit enough. Consider adding a brief comment at the `Join`-skipping line in `forEachFromRelation` clarifying that this relies on the invariant that the right side of a `Join` is always a lookup table, so that a future contributor adding a non-lookup join type is aware of the constraint.
