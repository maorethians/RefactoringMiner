#KCMVQ: The `ActionListener.wrap(actual::set, e -> {})` silently swallows any exception passed to `onFailure`. While the subsequent `notNullValue()` assertion will cause the test to fail, the error message ("Test failed to call the onResponse handler") does not surface the actual exception, making debugging harder. Consider capturing the exception (e.g., into an `AtomicReference<Exception>`) and asserting on it, or at minimum rethrowing it in the failure consumer so the root cause is visible in the test output.
