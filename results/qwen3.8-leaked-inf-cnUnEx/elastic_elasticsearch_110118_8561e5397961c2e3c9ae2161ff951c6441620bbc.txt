The `localRefs.mustIncRef()` call is moved from before `client.execute(...)` to inside the `processResponse` callback, just before `channel.sendResponse(...)`.
**Analysis:**
- **Before:** The ref was acquired at request-acceptance time and held for the entire async operation (request → response → chunked continuation).
- **After:** The ref is acquired at the point the response is actually being processed, and released in the continuation callback (`decRef()`).
This is a more targeted assertion: it verifies the `RefCounted` object is still alive at the moment it is actually needed (during response processing), rather than at the moment the request was initiated. The acquire (`mustIncRef()`) and release (`decRef()`) are now tightly scoped around the chunked-response lifecycle, which is the behavior under test.
The `mustIncRef()` → `sendResponse(...)` → `decRef()` ordering is correct: the ref is held for the duration of the chunked response delivery, and the `decRef()` in the continuation supplier properly balances it.
No correctness, thread-safety, or resource-leak issues are apparent in this test-scoped change.
