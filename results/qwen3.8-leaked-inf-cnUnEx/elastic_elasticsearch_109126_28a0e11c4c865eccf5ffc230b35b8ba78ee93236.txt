#T2D5M: The assertion `assert configs.size() == accumulatedStatsMap.size()` and the subsequent `Objects.requireNonNullElse(accumulatedStatsMap.get(config.getModelId()), AccumulatedStats.EMPTY)` are slightly contradictory. If the assertion holds, the `requireNonNullElse` fallback is unreachable. If a config genuinely has no corresponding stats entry (which the fallback seems designed to handle), the assertion would fire in test/development mode before the graceful path is reached. Consider either removing the assertion (relying on the fallback) or removing the fallback (relying on the assertion to catch the invariant), so the intent is unambiguous.

#C0X66: The test relies on hardcoded column indices (`get(9)`, `get(10)`, …, `get(13)`) to locate the pipeline-count and ingest-stat cells. If columns are ever reordered or inserted before these positions, the test will silently assert against the wrong values.
