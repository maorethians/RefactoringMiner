#S827P, #FT4SS: The change in `#S827P` is a clean simplification: replacing the manual `ConfiguredTargetKey` construction with `target.getLookupKey()` is both more concise and semantically more correct. For alias targets, the manually constructed key (using `target.getLabel()`) could diverge from the key under which the target is actually registered in the lookup map, whereas `getLookupKey()` returns the canonical key by definition. The regression test in `#FT4SS` appropriately covers the alias case that motivated this fix.
