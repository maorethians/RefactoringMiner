#QES0C: The `failed` field is written in the `finally` block but never read anywhere in the test or in `FutureCompleter`. Since the exception that triggers the `finally` block will propagate to the `Future` returned by `cs.submit(...)`, and `safeGet` in the test will surface it as an `AssertionError`, the `failed` flag is dead code. Consider removing it to avoid implying there is an assertion on it that doesn't exist.
