#952C4, #4DY5X: The map is declared as `Map<Object, LeafStoredFieldSourceProvider>`, but the key used in `getSource` is `ctx.id()`, which is a `String` (per Lucene's `LeafReaderContext` API). Declaring the key type as `Object` is unnecessarily loose and loses type safety. Prefer `Map<String, LeafStoredFieldSourceProvider>` so that any accidental use of a non-String key is caught at compile time, and the intent is clearer to readers.
