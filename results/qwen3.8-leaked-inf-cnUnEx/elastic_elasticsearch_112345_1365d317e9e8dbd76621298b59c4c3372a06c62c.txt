#YCNA7, #4AWR1, #9VA2C, #NPB90, #M3M3T, #FA4NZ: The `@timestamp` field is unconditionally added to the parsed source for all index modes. While it is required for `TIME_SERIES`, it is extraneous for `STANDARD` and `LOGSDB`. This is harmless (it simply becomes an extra indexed field), but if a future change introduces strict mapping validation or field-count assertions in those modes, the extra field could cause unexpected test failures. Consider adding `@timestamp` only in the `TIME_SERIES` branch, or at minimum adding a brief comment explaining why it is present for all modes.
