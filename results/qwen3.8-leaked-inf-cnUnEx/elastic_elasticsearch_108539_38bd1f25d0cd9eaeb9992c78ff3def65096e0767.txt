#FF6JT: The `onRejection` fallback to `doRun()` on the current thread is a reasonable strategy to avoid task leaks during shutdown. One minor suggestion: consider adding a debug-level log (e.g., `logger.debug("executor rejected task during shutdown, executing on current thread", e)`) so that if this path is hit unexpectedly outside of shutdown, it is observable in logs. Without any logging, a silent fallback could mask a misconfiguration or an unexpected rejection cause.
