#AAM9M: The change replaces `assertAcked(...)` with a bare `.get(TimeValue.timeValueSeconds(30))` call, discarding the returned `Response` object. The previous `assertAcked` helper presumably verified that the cluster settings update was acknowledged by the cluster. By removing that assertion, a failed or unacknowledged settings update will be silently ignored, and the test will proceed as if `minMasterNodes` was successfully applied. This can lead to subtle, hard-to-diagnose test failures downstream (e.g., the cluster still has the old `discovery.zen.minimum_master_nodes` value, causing unexpected master election behavior).
Consider at minimum checking the response:
```java
ClusterUpdateSettingsResponse response = client().admin()
    .cluster()
    .prepareUpdateSettings()
    .setTransientSettings(Settings.builder().put(DISCOVERY_ZEN_MINIMUM_MASTER_NODES_SETTING.getKey(), minMasterNodes))
    .get(TimeValue.timeValueSeconds(30));
assertThat(response.isAcknowledged(), is(true));
```
or, if `assertAcked` still exists elsewhere in the codebase, prefer keeping it to preserve the existing assertion semantics.
