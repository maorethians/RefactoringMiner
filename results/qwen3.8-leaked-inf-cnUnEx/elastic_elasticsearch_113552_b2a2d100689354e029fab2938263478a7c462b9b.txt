#707RB: The two early-return guards are placed on a single line:
```java
if (traceRedact == false) return;
if (fieldValue == null) return;
```
While functionally fine, the project's existing style (visible in the surrounding code) tends to use block bodies or at least separate statements on their own lines. Splitting these into two lines (or using a combined condition) would be more consistent with the codebase's formatting conventions.
