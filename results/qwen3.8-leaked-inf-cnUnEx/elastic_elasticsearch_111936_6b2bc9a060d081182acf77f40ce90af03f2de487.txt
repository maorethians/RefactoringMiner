#65FH3: In `roundtripTest`, `inputData.close()` is called explicitly, and then `BytesReference.bytes(inputData)` is invoked, which internally calls `xContentBuilder.close()` again (as shown in the dependency). This results in a double-close. While `ByteArrayOutputStream` tolerates repeated closes, the explicit `inputData.close()` is redundant since `BytesReference.bytes` already handles closing the builder as part of its contract. Removing the explicit `close()` call would make the intent clearer and avoid relying on close idempotency.
