#1TPMB, #5506R: One minor performance note: in the old code, when `relation == CONTAINS` and the geometry was not a point, the method returned `MatchNoDocsQuery` *before* calling `LuceneGeometriesUtils.toXYGeometry`. In the new code, the conversion always runs first, even on the early-return path. For complex geometries (large polygons, multi-geometries) this means doing a non-trivial conversion that is immediately discarded. If this path is hit frequently (e.g., users writing `CONTAINS` against point fields with large shapes), consider keeping a cheap pre-check (such as the original `geometry.type() != ShapeType.POINT`) before the conversion, and only falling through to the `isPointGeometry` check when the types are ambiguous.
