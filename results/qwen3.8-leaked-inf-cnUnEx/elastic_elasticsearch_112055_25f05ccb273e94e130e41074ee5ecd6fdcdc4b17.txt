#YXCK1: The `ascending` method for Doubles computes the even-count median as `(values.getDouble(middle - 1) + values.getDouble(middle)) / 2`, but `doubleMedianOf` (used by `finish`) computes it as `(values[middle - 1] / 2 + values[middle] / 2)`. The latter form is overflow-safe (avoids `Infinity` when both values are near `Double.MAX_VALUE`), while the former can overflow. For consistency and correctness, the `ascending` method should use the same overflow-safe form: `values.getDouble(middle - 1) / 2 + values.getDouble(middle) / 2`.
