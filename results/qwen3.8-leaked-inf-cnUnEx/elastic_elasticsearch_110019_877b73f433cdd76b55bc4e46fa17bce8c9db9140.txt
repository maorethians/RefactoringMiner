#Q4GVC: `assertSame(exception, expectThrows(ExecutionException.class, ElasticsearchException.class, future0::result))` will always fail. `expectThrows` with two class parameters returns the *outer* exception (the `ExecutionException`), not the cause. Since `exception` is the `ElasticsearchException` instance and the returned value is the wrapping `ExecutionException`, the reference-equality assertion is comparing two different objects.
The fix is to unwrap the cause:
```java
assertSame(exception, expectThrows(ExecutionException.class, ElasticsearchException.class, future0::result).getCause());
```
The same applies to the next line for `future1::result`.
