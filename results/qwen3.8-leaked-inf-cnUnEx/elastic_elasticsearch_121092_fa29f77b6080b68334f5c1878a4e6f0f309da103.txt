#71WX8, #1N64B: Minor style: `inferenceFields().isEmpty() == false` is equivalent to `!inferenceFields().isEmpty()`. The `== false` comparison on a boolean is redundant and slightly less readable. Consider using the negation form for clarity, unless the project convention explicitly prefers the `== false` style.

#91T4G: The `newSnapshot` method is a reasonable extraction, but its name is quite generic for a method that encapsulates a specific decision (synthetic vs. non-synthetic snapshot selection for translog assertion). A name like `createAssertionSnapshot` or `newTranslogAssertionSnapshot` would better convey intent and reduce the chance of it being misused in a context where the inference-fields precondition (handled by the caller in #1N64B) is not met.
Additionally, the method does not itself check for inference fields — it relies entirely on the caller to ensure it is only invoked when appropriate. A brief Javadoc noting this precondition (e.g., "Caller must ensure the searcher is backed by an inference-processed reader when inference fields are present") would help future maintainers.
