#A64EE, #8EKZM, #SSDQS, #CT6E4, #CJHR1, #BSE7X: The `waitForRateLimitingAssignments` helper only asserts that the `elastic` service with `TaskType.SPARSE_EMBEDDING` has a non-empty assignment. However, several of the tests that call it (e.g., `testNumberOfNodesPerGroup_Decreases_When_NodeLeavesCluster`, `testGrouping_RespectsMaxNodesPerGroupingLimit`, `testInitialRateLimitsCalculation_Correct`, `testRateLimits_Decrease_OnNodeJoin`, `testRateLimits_Increase_OnNodeLeave`) subsequently iterate over **all** services in `SERVICE_NODE_LOCAL_RATE_LIMIT_CONFIGS.keySet()` and assert on their assignments. If the rate-limit calculator populates assignments for different services at different times (or in separate update cycles), waiting on a single service/task-type pair does not guarantee the others are ready, and the tests can still be flaky.
Consider either:
- Expanding the helper to iterate over all known services/task types (or at least the ones the calling test will assert on), or
- Passing the service/task-type to wait for as a parameter, and calling it once per service in the tests that need it.
This would make the wait condition match the actual assertions and reduce the risk of intermittent test failures.
