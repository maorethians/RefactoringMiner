#A64EE, #JD11Y, #8EKZM, #SSDQS, #CT6E4, #CJHR1, #BSE7X: The `waitForRateLimitingAssignments` method is introduced to wait for rate limiting assignments to be available. However, the `RATE_LIMIT_ASSIGNMENT_MAX_WAIT_TIME_IN_SECONDS` constant is set to 15 seconds, which might be too short or too long depending on the context and expected performance of the system. It would be beneficial to make this configurable or at least document why 15 seconds was chosen as the appropriate wait time.
