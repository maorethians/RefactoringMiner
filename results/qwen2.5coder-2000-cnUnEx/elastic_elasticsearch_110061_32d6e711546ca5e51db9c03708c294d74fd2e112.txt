However, the `pauseExecution` and `stop` methods are not synchronized, which could lead to race conditions if these methods are called concurrently with other operations that check or modify the `isRunning` state.

#823CJ, #SDV97: The `pauseExecution` and `stop` methods clear the schedules map without any synchronization. If `checkJobs` is running concurrently, it might still process some watches after the engine is paused or closed.
