#YQWZA: The loop that collects transport addresses can be simplified by using streams for better readability and conciseness.
```java
final List<TransportAddress> addresses = internalCluster().getInstances(Transport.class).stream()
    .flatMap(transport -> Stream.concat(
        Arrays.stream(transport.boundAddress().boundAddresses()),
        transport.profileBoundAddresses().values().stream()
            .flatMap(profileAddresses -> Arrays.stream(profileAddresses.boundAddresses()))
    ))
    .collect(Collectors.toList());
```
