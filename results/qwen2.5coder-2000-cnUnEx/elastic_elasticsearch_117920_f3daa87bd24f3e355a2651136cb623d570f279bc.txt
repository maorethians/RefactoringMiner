#B4JT9, #94Q6X: The use of `shutdownNow()` followed by `awaitTermination()` is a good approach to ensure that the worker service stops gracefully within a specified time frame. However, the timeout value of 30 seconds should be configurable or at least documented as it might not be suitable for all environments.

#B4JT9: The logging in the catch block after `awaitTermination()` could be improved by including more context about why the interruption occurred and what actions were taken as a result. This would help with debugging and monitoring.

#K33S7: Removing `handleAlreadyShuttingDownWorker()` is fine if it's no longer needed, but ensure that all scenarios where this method was previously called are now handled correctly by other methods like `notifyQueueRunnables()` or `signalAndWaitForWorkerTermination()`. This change might introduce a regression if there were specific cases handled only in `handleAlreadyShuttingDownWorker()`.
