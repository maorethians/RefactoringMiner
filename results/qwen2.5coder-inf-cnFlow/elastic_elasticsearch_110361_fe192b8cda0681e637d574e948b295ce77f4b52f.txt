#YPQ8R: The replacement of `acquireShared(-1)` with a busy-wait loop using `Thread.onSpinWait()` can lead to high CPU usage if multiple threads are waiting for the future to complete. This approach is generally not recommended for I/O-bound or long waits, as it consumes CPU cycles without making progress. Consider using a more efficient synchronization mechanism like `LockSupport.parkNanos` or a condition variable.
