#BCR52, #09SM0: The logic for handling ignored source fields in synthetic mode has been moved from `IgnoredSourceFieldMapper#postParse` to `DocumentParserContext#createCopyToContext`. However, there is a potential issue with the condition checks and the way `ignoredFieldValues` are being managed. Specifically, the removal of the loop that processes `copyToFields` and adds entries to `ignoredFieldValues` could result in missed cases where ignored source values are not properly marked.
