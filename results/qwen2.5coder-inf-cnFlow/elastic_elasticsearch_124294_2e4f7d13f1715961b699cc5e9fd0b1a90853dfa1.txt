#AFWQG: The addition of `.put(IndexSettings.INDEX_FAST_REFRESH_SETTING.getKey(), false);` to set the `index.fast_refresh` setting to `false` for non-system indexes is clear and understandable. However, it's important to ensure that this change does not inadvertently disable fast refreshes for indexes where they might be beneficial. This could potentially impact performance if there are use cases where fast refreshes are needed.
