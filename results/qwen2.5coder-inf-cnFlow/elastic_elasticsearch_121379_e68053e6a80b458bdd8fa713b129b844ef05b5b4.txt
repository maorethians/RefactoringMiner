#A64EE, #SXMBM, #8EKZM, #5ZN9Y, #CT6E4, #CH9K1, #BSE7X: The addition of `waitForRateLimitingAssignments` calls in multiple test methods is a good practice to ensure that the rate limit assignments are available before proceeding with assertions. However, it introduces a dependency on the `RATE_LIMIT_ASSIGNMENT_MAX_WAIT_TIME_IN_SECONDS` constant, which should be documented or have a clear reason for its value (15 seconds). Additionally, the method name could be more descriptive to indicate what exactly is being waited for.
