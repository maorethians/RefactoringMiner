#3JVJS: The logic introduced in this diff checks if any of the longitude values are exactly 180. However, depending on the context, this might not be sufficient or correct. Longitudes wrap around at 180 and -180 degrees, so a value of 180 is equivalent to -180. If the intention is to ignore longitudes that are effectively at the boundary (either 180 or -180), this check should account for both values.
