#2SSZT, #5XF40, #XW19Y: The logic for calculating remaining time seems correct, but there are a few areas that could benefit from improvement:
1. In `RemainingTime.from`, the calculation of `remainingNanos` and its comparison with `maxRemainingTime` should be reviewed to ensure it handles edge cases correctly, such as when `currentTime.get()` is exactly equal to `timeout`.
2. The test cases in `RemainingTimeTests` are not comprehensive enough. They should cover more boundary conditions, such as:
   - When the current time is exactly at the timeout.
   - When the remaining time is negative (though this might be handled by `Math.max(0, ...)`).
   - When the remaining time is very close to zero but still positive.

1. The error message includes the full request timeout, which might be confusing if the remaining time is significantly less than the original timeout. Consider including both the remaining time and the original timeout in the error message for clarity.
