#S827P: The change replaces the manual construction of `ConfiguredTargetKey` objects with a call to `target.getLookupKey()`. This simplification is generally good for maintainability and readability, assuming that `getLookupKey()` returns an object compatible with what was previously constructed. However, it's important to verify that `getLookupKey()` indeed provides the same key structure as the manually built `ConfiguredTargetKey`, especially considering the detailed configuration and label settings in the original code.
