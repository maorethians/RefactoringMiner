#9232G, #917KW, #C3XYV: The introduction of a new thread pool executor named `NODE_RESTARTER` with a maximum of 5 threads and a core of 1 is clear in its intent to manage node restart tasks separately. However, the decision to use a scaling executor builder with a keep-alive time of `TimeValue.ZERO` for both executors might lead to unnecessary thread creation and teardown if tasks are frequent or long-running. This could impact performance due to the overhead of starting and stopping threads frequently.
