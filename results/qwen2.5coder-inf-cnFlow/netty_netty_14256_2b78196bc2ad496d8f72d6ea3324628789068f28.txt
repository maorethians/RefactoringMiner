#6PAKF, #P60ZD, #5M04X, #3HXWH, #4AA33, #40WAF, #CJN3H, #5DG02, #QYVAJ, #3FK04: The introduction of `pooledChunkAllocations` and `pooledChunkDeallocations` for tracking chunk allocations and deallocations is clear and logical. However, the calculation in `numActiveChunks()` should be reviewed to ensure it accurately reflects the number of active chunks.
In `numActiveChunks()`, the calculation `pooledChunkAllocations - pooledChunkDeallocations` assumes that every allocation corresponds to a unique chunk and every deallocation removes a unique chunk. This might not hold true if multiple allocations can occur from the same chunk or if deallocations do not necessarily mean the chunk is completely freed.
To ensure correctness, it would be beneficial to have an explicit count of active chunks rather than relying on the difference between allocations and deallocations.
