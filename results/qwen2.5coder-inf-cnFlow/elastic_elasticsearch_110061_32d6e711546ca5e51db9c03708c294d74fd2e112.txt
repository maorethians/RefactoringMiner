#H1J49, #823CJ, #SDV97, #X07YR, #Z9YXV, #RN7JM: The `isRunning` flag is used to control the execution state of the engine. However, the `pauseExecution` method no longer uses the `synchronized` keyword, which could lead to race conditions if multiple threads attempt to pause the engine simultaneously. The same applies to the `stop` method.

#H1J49: The `start` method sets the `isRunning` flag before logging the start time. This might not be a problem in this specific context, but it's generally safer to perform state changes after confirming all necessary actions have been completed successfully. Consider moving the `isRunning.set(true)` call after the logging statement.

#823CJ: The `pauseExecution` method clears the schedules without any synchronization or checks for ongoing operations. This could lead to inconsistent states if jobs are being processed when the engine is paused. Ensure that all active jobs are completed before clearing the schedules.

#SDV97: Similar to `pauseExecution`, the `stop` method clears the schedules and closes the ticker without ensuring that no jobs are currently running. Consider adding a mechanism to wait for ongoing jobs to complete or cancel them safely.
