#VJN92: The addition of `this.id = stream.id();` ensures that the `id` field is set when a stream is associated with the `DefaultHttp2FrameStream`. However, this change assumes that `stream.id()` will always return a valid and unique identifier. It would be beneficial to add an assertion or validation to confirm that `stream.id()` is not negative or zero, as these values might indicate an invalid state.
