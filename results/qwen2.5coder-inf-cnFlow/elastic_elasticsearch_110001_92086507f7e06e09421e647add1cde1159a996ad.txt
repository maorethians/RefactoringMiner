#XCFTS, #0C7EF, #8WSF3, #35RH5, #ZHNPY, #080GS, #28MVQ, #70PH1: The introduction of `TreeSet` and the subsequent check with `containsAll` is unnecessary and can lead to incorrect behavior. The `TreeSet` constructor expects a comparator as its first argument if you intend to pass a collection, but here it seems like the intention was to create a `TreeSet` from the list. However, even if that were corrected, using a `TreeSet` just to check for equality with another set is inefficient and can lead to incorrect results if the elements are not naturally ordered or do not have a proper comparator defined. The original code using `containsInAnyOrder` directly on the lists is more straightforward and correct.
