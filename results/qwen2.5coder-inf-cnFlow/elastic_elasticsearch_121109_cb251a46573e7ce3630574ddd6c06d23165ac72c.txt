#R63ZA: The constructor for `Wrapper` takes a `RootObjectMapper` and a `DocumentParserContext`. The call to `ObjectMapper.Dynamic.getRootDynamic(in.mappingLookup())` is correct, but it's important to ensure that `in.mappingLookup()` does not return null. While the method `getRootDynamic` handles null by returning `Defaults.DYNAMIC`, it would be clearer if this were explicitly checked or documented in the constructor to make the intent more explicit.
