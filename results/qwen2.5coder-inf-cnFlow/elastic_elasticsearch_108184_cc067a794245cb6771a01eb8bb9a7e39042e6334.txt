#8YKRD, #CTVE0: The sorting of `reducedBuckets` is moved outside the `if (reduceContext.isFinalReduce())` block. This change means that the buckets will always be sorted by key before any other potential sorting or reversing logic is applied. If the intention was to sort only during final reduction, this change could lead to unnecessary sorting operations and potentially incorrect behavior if further sorting or reversing is expected without an initial sort.
