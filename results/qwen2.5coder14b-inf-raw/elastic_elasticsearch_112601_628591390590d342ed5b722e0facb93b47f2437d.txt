#24812, #0N6QY, #08G32, #XH91X, #RCX2V: The method `emitOrds` has been renamed to `flushRemaining`, and a new check has been added to ensure that the method is not called in the middle of a position. This change introduces a potential issue where an empty block might be flushed unnecessarily if the number of added items is not a multiple of `emitBatchSize`. The comment in the test case #TJ64N indicates that this behavior is intentional, but it should be documented more clearly to avoid confusion.
