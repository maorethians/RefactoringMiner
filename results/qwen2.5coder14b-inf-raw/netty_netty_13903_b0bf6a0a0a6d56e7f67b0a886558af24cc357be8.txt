#NPX5K: The addition of `hashCode` and `equals` methods in `DefaultHttp2FrameStream` is appropriate. However, the implementation of `equals` seems incomplete. It checks if `stream` is not null before comparing it with `thatStream`, but it should also handle cases where both streams are null or one is null and the other is not. Additionally, the comparison `stream.equals(thatStream)` assumes that `stream` is not null, which could lead to a NullPointerException if `stream` is indeed null.
