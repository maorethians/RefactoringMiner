This change ensures that the sequence numbers do not overflow beyond their maximum value. The introduction of the `incrementUintSegmentNumber` method is a good approach to handle the wrap-around logic. It correctly uses modulo operation with `0xFFFFFFFFL` to ensure the sequence number wraps around when it exceeds the 32-bit unsigned integer limit. The changes in `handleTCP`, `completeTCPWrite`, and `handlerRemoved` methods are consistent with the new data type for sequence numbers, ensuring that all operations involving these variables are updated accordingly. The update to the `logTCP` method signature to accept `long` instead of `int` for sequence numbers is necessary to maintain consistency with the rest of the codebase. The changes in the `TCPPacket.writePacket` method to cast `segmentNumber` and `ackNumber` back to `int` before writing them to the buffer are correct, as TCP headers require 32-bit integers.
