#KTQG9, #GQTC6: The change in KTQG9 replaces a call to `acquireShared(-1)` with a busy-wait loop using `Thread.onSpinWait()`. This approach can lead to increased CPU usage and potential performance issues, especially if the future completion is not immediate. The comment suggests avoiding false-positive deadlock detection, but this solution may introduce other concurrency issues or inefficiencies.
While GQTC6 introduces a new test method `testAssertCompleteAllowedAllowsConcurrentCompletes` to verify concurrent completions, it adds significant complexity and overhead by using multiple threads and synchronization primitives. This could potentially slow down the test suite and make it harder to maintain.
