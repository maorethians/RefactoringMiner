#FN04R: The test iterates over `suppliers.stream().toList()` (a snapshot) and calls `suppliers.add(...)` inside the loop. This is correct and avoids `ConcurrentModificationException`, but it's worth noting that the `evaluatorTypePart` switch will throw `IllegalArgumentException` for any `DataType` not explicitly listed. If new type suppliers are added to the `suppliers` list in the future (e.g., `FLOAT`, `BOOLEAN` is handled, but what about `FLOAT`?), this test will fail at runtime with a somewhat opaque error. Consider whether a `default -> "Object"` fallback (or a more descriptive message that lists the supported types) would make the failure easier to diagnose for a future contributor.
