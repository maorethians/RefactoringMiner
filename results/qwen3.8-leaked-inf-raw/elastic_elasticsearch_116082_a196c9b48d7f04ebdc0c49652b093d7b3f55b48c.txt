#HQ8H4, #J66ZP: The constant `XOR_BIT_COUNT_STRIDE_AS_INT` and its Javadoc ("For xorBitCount we stride over the values…") reference XOR, but the constant is only consumed by `andBitCount` / `andBitCountInt` / `andBitCountLong`. A future reader encountering `andBitCount` will be confused by a name and comment that talk about XOR. Consider renaming to something like `BIT_COUNT_STRIDE_AS_INT` (or `AND_BIT_COUNT_STRIDE_AS_INT`) and updating the comment to describe the general striding strategy rather than tying it to a specific operation.
