#DXK13, #6CKFB: The removal of the `assert path.isAbsolute()` / `assert relativePath.isAbsolute() == false` guards in `FilesEntitlement` is paired with the deletion of the two parser-level validation tests (`testEntitlementRelativePathWhenAbsolute` and `testEntitlementAbsolutePathWhenRelative`). Taken together, this means there is no longer any visible enforcement that a `path` entry is absolute or a `relative_path` entry is relative. If the validation logic was intentionally removed (e.g., because it was unsound on Windows where `/foo` is not absolute), that is reasonable—but it would be worth confirming that no other layer (e.g., the `PolicyParser` or `FilesEntitlement.build`) still rejects malformed entries, since a silently-accepted misconfigured policy is harder to diagnose at runtime than a parse-time error.

For consistency and to avoid creating a new temp directory on every invocation (e.g., if the test is retried), consider promoting `testPathToReadDir` to a static field initialized in `beforeClass()` alongside `TEST_ABSOLUTE_PATH_TO_FILE`.
