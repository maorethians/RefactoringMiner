#VA7BS, #QWHAB: The refactoring of the error messages is well-structured. Splitting `CANNOT_STOP_FAILED_TRANSFORM` into single/multiple variants and standardizing the "More details: [{1}]" suffix improves clarity for users. The use of `String.join(", ", ...)` in #QWHAB is a good improvement over the previous raw `List.toString()` concatenation, as it makes the formatting explicit and consistent with the template's `[{0}]` brackets.

#SE4SE, #REMEM, #82H6E: Switching from `matchesRegex` to `startsWith` in the integration tests is a solid improvement — it eliminates the need to escape brackets and other regex metacharacters in the expected strings, making the tests more readable and less brittle. The removal of the trailing `.*` is correctly paired with this change.

#DPH7Y: The new multi-failed-task test case is a good addition that covers the previously untested `CANNOT_STOP_MULTIPLE_FAILED_TRANSFORMS` path. One minor observation: the expected message strings are now hardcoded literals rather than derived from `TransformMessages.getMessage(...)`. This makes the tests verify the exact user-facing text (which is arguably better), but it means a future message template change requires updating both the constant and the test literal. This is an acceptable trade-off, just worth being aware of.
