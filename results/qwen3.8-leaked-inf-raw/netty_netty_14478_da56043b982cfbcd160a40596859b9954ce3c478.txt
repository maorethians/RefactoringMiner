#ZT8RS: The modulo constant is off by one. TCP sequence numbers are 32-bit unsigned and wrap at 2^32 (i.e., the valid range is `[0, 0xFFFFFFFF]`, which contains 2^32 = `0x100000000` values). Using `% 0xFFFFFFFFL` means the value `0xFFFFFFFF` is never produced (it maps to 0), and the wrap point is shifted by one. For example, `0xFFFFFFFE + 2` should wrap to `0`, but with this formula it yields `1`.
Fix: use `(sequenceNumber + value) & 0xFFFFFFFFL` or `(sequenceNumber + value) % 0x100000000L`.
