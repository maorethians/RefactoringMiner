#DZ352: The new `testDefaultsForTimeSeriesIndex` verifies that doc values are *present* by default in a time-series index, but it does not verify that an explicit `"doc_values": false` in the mapping still suppresses doc values. Since the whole point of this change is to alter the *default*, a companion assertion (or a second test) that sets `doc_values: false` on a binary field in a time-series index and asserts the `DocValuesType.BINARY` field is **absent** would guard against a regression where the explicit override is accidentally ignored in favor of the new default.
