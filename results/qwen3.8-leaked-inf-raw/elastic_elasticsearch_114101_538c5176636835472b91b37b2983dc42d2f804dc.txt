#ZHFJ4: The added `flush()` is a good fix to ensure the response body is actually sent to the client. However, the idiomatic way to finalize an `HttpExchange` response is to call `exchange.close()` rather than `exchange.getResponseBody().flush()`. The `close()` method both flushes and releases the underlying connection resources. Consider replacing the flush with `exchange.close()` to follow the standard `com.sun.net.httpserver` pattern and avoid leaking the connection in the mock server.
