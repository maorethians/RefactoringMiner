#EWNZ7: The `assertThat` call places the literal `"keyword"` as the *actual* value and the dynamically-evaluated mapping value inside `equalTo(...)` as the *expected*. While functionally correct (equality is symmetric), the conventional Hamcrest idiom is `assertThat(actual, equalTo(expected))`. The more readable form would be:
```java
assertThat(
    "incorrect k8s.pod.name field in mapping:" + mapping,
    ObjectPath.evaluate(mapping, "properties.k8s.properties.pod.properties.name.type"),
    equalTo("keyword")
);
```
This makes it immediately clear what is being checked (the mapping value) against what it should be (`"keyword"`), rather than the reverse.
