#HA000: The exception message reports `spdyDataFrame.content().readableBytes()` as the offending value, but the condition that triggers the exception is `content.readableBytes() > maxContentLength - spdyDataFrame.content().readableBytes()`, meaning the *total* (`content.readableBytes() + spdyDataFrame.content().readableBytes()`) is what exceeds the limit. Reporting only the frame's readable bytes can be misleading to a developer debugging the issue, since that single value may be well under `maxContentLength` while the cumulative total is what actually overflows. Consider reporting the combined total, e.g.:
```java
throw new TooLongFrameException(
        "HTTP content length exceeded " + maxContentLength + " bytes: "
                + (content.readableBytes() + spdyDataFrame.content().readableBytes()));
```
