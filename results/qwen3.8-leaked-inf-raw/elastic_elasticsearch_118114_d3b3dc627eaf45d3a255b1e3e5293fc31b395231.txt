#4JC7J: The guard `name.isEmpty() || name.startsWith(SYNTHETIC_ATTRIBUTE_NAME_PREFIX)` handles the empty-args edge case by returning an empty string. In practice this is always called with ≥ 2 parts, so this is fine, but a `parts.length == 0` guard (or at least a comment) would make the contract clearer.

#AZCW8, #E2P38, #NYP9M: The method now has two failure channels — `failures` (returned) for `FieldExtractExec` issues and `depFailures` (thrown as `IllegalStateException`) for dependency issues. This asymmetry means callers must handle both a returned collection and a possible exception. Worth a brief Javadoc note on `verify()` to document that it may throw.

#YFSZ2, #5V83V: The local variable `orginalAttributs` has a typo — it should be `originalAttributes` (missing the "i" in "original" and the "e" in "Attributes"). While this is a local variable and won't affect behavior, it will be visible to anyone reading or debugging this method and is easy to fix.
