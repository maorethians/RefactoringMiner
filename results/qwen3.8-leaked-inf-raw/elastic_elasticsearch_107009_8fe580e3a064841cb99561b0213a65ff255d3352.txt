#WG2N6: In the old code, `jobBuilder.build()` was called eagerly before the async `datafeedTimingStats` call, and the built job's ID was used (`context.getJob().getId()`). In the new code, `jobBuilder.getId()` is called on the builder directly, and `jobBuilder.build()` is deferred into the callback. This is a subtle behavioral shift: if `build()` has any side effects (e.g., validation, state mutation) or if `getId()` on the builder could differ from `getId()` on the built object, the semantics change. In practice this is almost certainly fine for a standard builder, but it's worth confirming that `jobBuilder.getId()` is guaranteed to equal `jobBuilder.build().getId()` and that `build()` is a pure construction with no side effects that other code in the chain might depend on having already occurred.
