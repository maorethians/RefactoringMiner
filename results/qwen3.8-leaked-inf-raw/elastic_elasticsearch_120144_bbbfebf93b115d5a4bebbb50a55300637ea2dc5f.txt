#EMJE0: The comparison `updatedIndex.equals(currentIndex) == false` will work correctly because `updatedIndex` comes from the full config and is guaranteed non-null. However, if `getIndexer()` is non-null but `getIndexer().getConfig()` were ever null (e.g., during a transient state), this would throw an NPE. Consider using `Objects.equals(updatedIndex, currentIndex)` for a more defensive and readable form, which also handles the null-`currentIndex` case (when indexer is null) without relying on the non-null side calling `.equals(null)`.
