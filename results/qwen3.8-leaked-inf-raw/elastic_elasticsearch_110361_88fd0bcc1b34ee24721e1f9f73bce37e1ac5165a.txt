#KTQG9: The spin-wait replacement is well-motivated and the comment clearly explains the rationale. One minor consideration: this is a busy-wait loop. While `Thread.onSpinWait()` is the correct hint and the expected wait duration is very short (just until the other thread finishes its completion), in a pathological case (e.g., the completing thread is preempted for an extended period due to a long GC pause or OS scheduling), the spinning threads will burn CPU. This is an acceptable trade-off given that the alternative (`acquireShared`) triggers false-positive deadlock detection, but it's worth being aware of. No change required, just noting the trade-off is understood.

#46JWT: The test effectively exercises the concurrent-completion scenario from the same thread pool, which is the exact case the fix targets. Two suggestions:
1. The test has no explicit assertion that the futures were actually completed. It relies on the absence of a deadlock-detection exception to signal success. Consider adding a check (e.g., capturing the last future and asserting `future.isDone()` after the loop, or counting successful completions) so the test also verifies the functional outcome, not just the absence of a failure.
2. The `safeSleep(1)` between future replacements is a timing-dependent mechanism to let worker threads observe the new future. This can make the test slightly flaky under heavy CI load. A `CyclicBarrier` or a small `CountDownLatch` per iteration would make the hand-off more deterministic, though with 4 threads × 20 iterations the current approach is likely acceptable in practice.
