#Q69Z4: When `acceptChannel` is `false`, the server rejects the connection. With the old per-iteration client, each retry started with a fresh connection. With the shared client, a rejected connection may leave the client's internal channel in a half-closed or error state, and the next `client.get(...)` call relies on the client's reconnection logic to recover. This is likely fine for a well-behaved Netty client, but if the test is flaky in CI, the shared-client pattern (vs. the previous fresh-client-per-iteration) could be the cause. Worth keeping in mind if flakiness appears.
