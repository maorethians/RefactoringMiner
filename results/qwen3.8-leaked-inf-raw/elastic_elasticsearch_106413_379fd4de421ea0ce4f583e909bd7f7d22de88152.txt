#EPG63: The refactoring of `finalListener` from `ActionListener.wrap` with an explicit `try-catch` to `delegateFailureAndWrap` removes the exception handling around `configurePageSize(...)` and `determineRunStateAtStart()`. In the original code, if either of those calls threw a `RuntimeException`, it was caught and routed to `listener.onFailure(e)`. In the new code, such an exception would propagate uncaught out of the lambda, potentially bypassing the listener's failure path and leaving the transform in an inconsistent state. Consider wrapping the body in a try-catch that delegates to `l.onFailure(e)`, or confirm that neither method can throw under any realistic condition.
