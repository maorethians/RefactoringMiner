#73MFH: The unconditional `fieldNames.addAll(IndexResolver.INDEX_METADATA_FIELD)` means every query path through this method now requests `_index` from the index, even when other concrete fields are already present. Since `_index` is a lightweight metadata field this is almost certainly negligible in practice, but it is a small, always-on overhead compared to the previous behavior where it was only requested in the empty-field fallback. Worth being aware of, though not a blocker.
