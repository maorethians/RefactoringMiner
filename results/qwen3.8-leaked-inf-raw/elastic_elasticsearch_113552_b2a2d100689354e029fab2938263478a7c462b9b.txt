#63A1F: The four constants are declared `protected static final`. Since they are only referenced from the same package (the test class `RedactProcessorTests` lives in `org.elasticsearch.xpack.redact`), package-private (no modifier) would be the more conventional choice for constants that don't need to be visible to subclasses. `protected` on a `static final` constant is legal but slightly unusual and may imply a subclass-extension intent that isn't present here. This is a very minor style nit and does not affect correctness.
