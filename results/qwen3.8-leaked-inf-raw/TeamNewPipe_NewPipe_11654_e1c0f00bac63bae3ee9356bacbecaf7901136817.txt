#C6CK2, #TERWQ: The XML layout declares `android:onClick="onClick"` on the button, which instructs the Android framework to look up a public `onClick(View)` method on the hosting **Activity** at click time. If no such method exists, a `RuntimeException` is thrown. Meanwhile, the click is already handled programmatically via `setOnClickListener` in `initViews`. This creates two problems:
1. **Runtime crash risk** – if the hosting Activity does not define a public `onClick(View)` method, tapping the button will crash the app before the `OnClickListener` even fires.
2. **Redundancy / double-handling** – if such a method *does* exist, both handlers will execute, leading to unexpected side effects.
Remove `android:onClick="onClick"` from the XML. The programmatic `setOnClickListener` in `initViews` is sufficient and is the idiomatic approach for Fragment-managed views.
