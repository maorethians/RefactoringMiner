#YFDJB: The `performRequestWithManageUser` helper mutates the passed-in `Request` object as a side effect (via `request.setOptions(...)`). While this is safe in the current call site (the request is created locally in `reloadSecureSettings()`), it makes the helper's contract less obvious — a future caller might pass a shared/reused `Request` and get unexpected auth headers. Consider either building a fresh `RequestOptions` locally and returning the response without mutating the input, or at minimum adding a brief note that the request is consumed/mutated.

If the body of `testReloadWithFailures` no longer throws checked exceptions this is fine, but the asymmetry between the two test methods is slightly inconsistent.
