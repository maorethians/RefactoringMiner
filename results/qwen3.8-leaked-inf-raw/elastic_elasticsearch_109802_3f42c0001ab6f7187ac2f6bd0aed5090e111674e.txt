#VF0R5, #EEB5J, #1E8V9, #5FWKZ: The changes are consistent and correctly implement the stated goal: a refresh failure no longer causes the bulk write response to fail. Instead, the `refreshed` flag simply reports `false`, while the write itself is acknowledged as successful. The removal of the `refreshFailure` field and its associated check in `maybeFinish()` is clean and eliminates the temporary workaround noted in the TODO.

#5FWKZ: One minor observation: the `Exception e` parameter in `onFailure` is now entirely discarded. While this is the intended behavior (don't propagate the failure to the caller), if the refresh mechanism does not already log the exception at a WARN/ERROR level upstream, this change would silently swallow refresh failures, making them harder to diagnose in production. Worth confirming that the refresh pipeline logs the failure independently before this callback is invoked.
