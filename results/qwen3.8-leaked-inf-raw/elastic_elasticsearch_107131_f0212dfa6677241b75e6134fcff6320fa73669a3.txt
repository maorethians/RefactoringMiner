#H4MQJ: The `aliases.resolve(child, child)` call performs only a top-level map lookup. If `child` is a compound expression (e.g., `f3 + languages`), nested attribute references within it will not be resolved. This is acceptable for the bug being fixed (direct alias-to-alias chains) and the tests confirm compound cases are handled by preserving the Eval node, but it's worth being aware of as a limitation if future queries introduce deeper alias chains within compound expressions.
