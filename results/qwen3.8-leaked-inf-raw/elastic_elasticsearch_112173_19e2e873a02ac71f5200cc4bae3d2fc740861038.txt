#ZRW3T: In `testLoadStoredFieldAndAdvance`, the second write call targets the wrong builder:
```java
var empty = XContentBuilder.builder(XContentType.JSON.xContent());
empty.startObject();
sut.write(result);   // <-- writes to the already-finalized `result` builder
empty.endObject();
assertEquals("{}", Strings.toString(empty));
```
This should be `sut.write(empty)`. As written, the assertion trivially passes (an empty object is always `{}`) without actually verifying that the loader correctly produces no output after `advanceToDoc(1)`. The `sut.write(result)` call may also throw or silently corrupt the already-closed `result` builder depending on the `XContentBuilder` implementation.
