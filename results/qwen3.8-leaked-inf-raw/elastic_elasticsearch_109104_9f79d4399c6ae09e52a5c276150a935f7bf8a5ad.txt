#G2YVN, #6741A, #JZWTB, #WQ2N5, #K0A43, #SYZZE, #A6Y6A, #A53T9: `TransportFindFieldStructureAction` bypasses the new `TextStructExecutor` and directly calls `threadPool.generic().execute(ActionRunnable.supply(...))` in its `doExecute` method, while `TransportFindMessageStructureAction` and `TransportFindStructureAction` both route through `executor.execute(...)`. This creates two problems:
1. **Inconsistent workaround removal path.** The `TextStructExecutor` class documents (via its TODO and method Javadoc) that removing the workaround requires updating "each consuming class." Because `TransportFindFieldStructureAction` never consumes the executor, a future developer removing the workaround will not find it here, and the direct `threadPool.generic()` call will silently remain — defeating the purpose of centralizing the change.
2. **Missing thread-pool assert in the lambda.** `TextStructExecutor.execute()` includes `assert ThreadPool.assertCurrentThreadPool(GENERIC)` inside the `ActionRunnable.supply` lambda. In `TransportFindFieldStructureAction` the equivalent lambda (`() -> buildTextStructureResponse(messages, request)`) has no such assert; the assert was instead placed inside `buildTextStructureResponse` itself (#SYZZE). While functionally equivalent for this call site, it diverges from the pattern established by the executor and makes the invariant harder to discover at the dispatch site.
Suggested fix: inject `TextStructExecutor` into `TransportFindFieldStructureAction`, pass `executor.handledTransportActionExecutorService()` to the `super(...)` constructor (matching the other two classes), and replace the direct `threadPool.generic().execute(...)` call with `executor.execute(delegate, () -> buildTextStructureResponse(messages, request))`. This keeps the workaround removal a single-point change and unifies the assert placement.
