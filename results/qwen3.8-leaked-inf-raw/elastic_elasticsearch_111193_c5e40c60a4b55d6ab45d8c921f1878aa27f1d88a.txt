#CDRZM, #W7KTZ, #E0WN3, #F888N, #9JYSH: The changes look correct and well-motivated. The core fix addresses a real bug: `geometry.type() != ShapeType.POINT` would incorrectly reject a `GeometryCollection` (or `MultiPoint`) wrapping a single point, because `type()` returns `GEOMETRY_COLLECTION` / `MULTI_POINT` rather than `POINT`. Converting to `XYGeometry[]` first and then checking `isPointGeometry` correctly identifies the geometric content regardless of the wrapper type.
One minor observation on the reordering in `#F888N`: the `LuceneGeometriesUtils.toXYGeometry(...)` call now executes unconditionally, whereas previously it was skipped for the `CONTAINS` + non-point short-circuit path. This is a negligible cost in practice (the conversion is needed for every other relation anyway), but if this method is on a hot path with many CONTAINS queries against large non-point geometries, the extra allocation of the `XYGeometry[]` array is now paid even when the result is immediately discarded in favor of `MatchNoDocsQuery`. Not a blocker, just worth being aware of.
The `isPointGeometry` helper is clear, the import cleanup in `#E0WN3` is consistent, and unmuting the test in `#W7KTZ` is the right follow-up.
