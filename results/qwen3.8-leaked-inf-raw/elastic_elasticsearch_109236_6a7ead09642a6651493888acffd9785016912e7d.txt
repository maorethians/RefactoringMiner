#0Z2R3, #ZQBTP, #WXXM5: The `enableStackOverflowAvoidance` field is declared and initialized but never used in `onConnectionClosed`. The first `if` unconditionally replaces `DIRECT_EXECUTOR_SERVICE` with `threadPool.generic()`, making the second `if` (direct invocation) dead code and rendering the deprecated setting a no-op. The condition should be `if (enableStackOverflowAvoidance && executor == EsExecutors.DIRECT_EXECUTOR_SERVICE)`. As written, the test in `#H4Q7X` that asserts the handler runs on the calling thread when the executor is DIRECT and the setting is disabled will fail.
