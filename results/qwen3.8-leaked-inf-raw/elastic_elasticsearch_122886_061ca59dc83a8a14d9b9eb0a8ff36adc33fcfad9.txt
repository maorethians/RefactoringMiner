#SBWF5, #D0CB0: The condition `System.getProperty("generateDocs") == null || true` is always `true`, which means `renderSignature()` and `renderDocs()` will unconditionally return early and never execute their logic. This appears to be a debugging leftover that accidentally disables the documentation generation mechanism entirely. The `|| true` should be removed, restoring the original guard `if (System.getProperty("generateDocs") == null)`.
