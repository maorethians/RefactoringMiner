#ZJNBE, #GBXEA: The constant `RESPONSE_PARAMS` and the method `responseParams(RestApiVersion)` in `BaseRestHandler` have very similar names but represent different things (a static set of always-supported response params vs. a version-dependent set). In the filter chain in #GBXEA, both are checked sequentially, which is correct, but the near-identical naming could trip up future readers. A slightly more distinctive name for the constant (e.g., `ALWAYS_RESPONSE_PARAMS` or `GLOBAL_RESPONSE_PARAMS`) might reduce confusion, though this is a stylistic suggestion.
