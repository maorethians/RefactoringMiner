#5NNPM, #TWEAB: In the test (`testNonNegativeDotProduct`), for the DOT_PRODUCT and COSINE cases, the assertions `assertThat(scorer.score(0, 1), equalTo(expected))` (where `expected = 0f`) and the immediately following `assertThat(scorer.score(0, 1), greaterThanOrEqualTo(0f))` are redundant — if the value equals `0f`, it is necessarily `>= 0f`. This is a minor stylistic point; the `greaterThanOrEqualTo` check adds value for the MAXIMUM_INNER_PRODUCT and EUCLIDEAN cases where `expected` is computed dynamically and could theoretically be a small positive value, but for the hardcoded `0f` cases it is superfluous.
