#FKMD4: `Integer` vs `int` for the timeout constant: `RATE_LIMIT_ASSIGNMENT_MAX_WAIT_TIME_IN_SECONDS` is declared as `Integer` (boxed). Since `assertBusy` expects a primitive `int`, this will auto-unbox, but using `int` is more conventional for a compile-time constant and avoids a needless boxing allocation.

#9REMD: `waitForRateLimitingAssignments` hardcodes a specific service/task type: The helper checks `ElasticInferenceService.NAME` / `TaskType.SPARSE_EMBEDDING` as a sentinel. This is reasonable as a "calculator has computed" probe, but it creates an implicit coupling: if `ElasticInferenceService` were ever removed from the supported services, the wait would time out even though other services are fine. A brief comment explaining why this particular service is used as the readiness probe would help future maintainers.

#X643E: Unused pattern-matching variable removed: Changing `sender instanceof HttpRequestSender httpSender` to `sender instanceof HttpRequestSender` is a correct cleanup since `httpSender` was never referenced.
