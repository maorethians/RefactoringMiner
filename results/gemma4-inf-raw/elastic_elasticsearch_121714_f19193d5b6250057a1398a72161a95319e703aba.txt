#5F01S: In `lookupExplosion`, the `finally` block only deletes the `sensor_data` index. However, `lookupExplosionData` (called at the start of the method) creates both `sensor_data` and `sensor_lookup` indices. To prevent resource leakage in the test cluster, `deleteIndex("sensor_lookup")` should be added to the `finally` block, consistent with the implementation in `lookupExplosionNoFetch` and `lookupExplosionBigString`.
