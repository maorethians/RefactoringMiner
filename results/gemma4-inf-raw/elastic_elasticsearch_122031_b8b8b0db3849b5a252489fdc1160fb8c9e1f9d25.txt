#PE8SZ: The `assertNoSearchesRunning` method contains nested calls to `assertBusy`. The inner `assertBusy` likely uses a default timeout, while the outer one specifies a 1-minute timeout. This structure is redundant and confusing; if the inner loop times out and throws an exception, the outer loop will simply restart the entire process (including recreating the `Request` object). It is highly probable that the original `assertBusy` call should have been replaced by the timed version rather than wrapped by it.
