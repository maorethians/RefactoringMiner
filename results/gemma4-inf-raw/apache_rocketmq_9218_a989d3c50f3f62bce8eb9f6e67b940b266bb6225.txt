#S1K7R: The logic change causes Pop consumers with the KV service enabled (`isPopConsumerKVServiceEnable() == true`) to fall into the `else` block, where `offsetManager.queryPullOffset` is called. Since Pop and Pull consumers use different offset management mechanisms, routing KV-enabled Pop consumers to the pull offset query will likely result in incorrect lag calculations.

#S1K7R: The removal of the fallback assignment `pullOffset = brokerOffset` when `pullOffset < 0` within the legacy pop logic path removes a safety mechanism. If both `popBufferMergeService.getLatestOffset` and `offsetManager.queryOffset` return negative values, `pullOffset` will remain negative, which may lead to incorrect results in subsequent calculations (e.g., calculating lag as `brokerOffset - (-1)`).
