#C078M: The test method `testUnclosedParenthesis` is misleadingly named. The provided test cases (e.g., `"row a = )"`, `"row ]"`) actually test for *extra* or *unbalanced* closing delimiters, rather than parentheses that were opened but never closed. Consider renaming it to `testExtraClosingParenthesis` or `testUnbalancedParenthesis` to accurately reflect the intent.
