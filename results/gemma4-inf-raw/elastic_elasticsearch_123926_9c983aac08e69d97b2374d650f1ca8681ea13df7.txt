#5FVR8, #S7KSF, #T11JZ, #C4S1S, #HCN0N: In `migrateDataStream` and `checkDataStreamMigrationStatus`, exceptions are thrown directly within the success callbacks of asynchronous actions (e.g., `throw new ElasticsearchException(...)`). Because these are wrapped in `ActionListener.wrap`, throwing an exception inside the success lambda will bypass the failure handler and prevent `markAsFailed(ex)` from being called. This means migration failures for data streams may not be recorded in the cluster state, leaving the feature in an inconsistent status. These should be replaced with explicit calls to `markAsFailed(ex)`.
