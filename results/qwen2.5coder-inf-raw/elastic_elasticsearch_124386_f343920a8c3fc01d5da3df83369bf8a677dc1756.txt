#E942D, #F65NF: The introduction of `waitingTaskLatch` is intended to ensure that there is at least one task waiting on the latch before proceeding. However, the usage of `safeAwait(waitingTaskLatch)` without a timeout in both `#E942D` and `#F65NF` can lead to potential deadlocks if the expected condition (a task reaching the latch) does not occur. It is advisable to include a timeout to prevent indefinite blocking.
