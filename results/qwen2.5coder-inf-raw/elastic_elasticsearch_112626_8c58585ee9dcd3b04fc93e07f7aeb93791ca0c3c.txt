#Q2QYN, #8FYCF, #9812Q: The introduction of a new constructor for `SafeScheduledThreadPoolExecutor` with a custom `RejectedExecutionHandler` is fine, but its usage in `ResourceWatcherService` should be reviewed for consistency and necessity. The executor is created only when the service is enabled, which means it will not be used if the service is disabled. However, the executor is still declared at the class level and initialized to null when the service is disabled. This could lead to confusion or potential issues if the executor is accessed elsewhere in the code without a null check.

#8FYCF: The initialization of `lowMonitor`, `mediumMonitor`, and `highMonitor` outside the `if (enabled)` block when the service is disabled should be reconsidered. Initializing these to null explicitly is clear but could be avoided by simply not initializing them at all if they are not needed.

#0CA5Y: The warning message "cannot add resource watcher, resource watcher service is closed" in the `add` method of `ResourceMonitor` might be misleading or incorrect. If the service is closed, adding a watcher should either fail silently, throw an exception, or have some other mechanism to indicate that the operation cannot proceed. A warning log might not be sufficient to convey this state change effectively.

#MAZ5Q, #NY8W5: The `service.close()` calls in the test methods are good practice as they ensure resources are released after each test. However, it is important to verify that these calls do not interfere with other tests or setup/teardown logic in the test suite.
