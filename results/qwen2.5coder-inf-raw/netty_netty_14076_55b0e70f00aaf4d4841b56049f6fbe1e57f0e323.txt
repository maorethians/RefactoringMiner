#DQ2PK, #RRJR7: The removal of the `duplicate()` and `slice(int index, int length)` methods from `ReadOnlyUnsafeDirectByteBuf` and their replacement with new classes (`ReadOnlySlicedByteBuf` and `ReadOnlyDuplicatedByteBuf`) in `ReadOnlyByteBufferBuf` is inconsistent. This inconsistency can lead to confusion and maintenance issues. It would be better to maintain a consistent approach across all `ReadOnly*ByteBuf` implementations.

#DQ2PK: The introduction of `ReadOnlySlicedByteBuf` and `ReadOnlyDuplicatedByteBuf` with overridden methods that return `this` for `asReadOnly()` is redundant since these classes are already read-only. The `asReadOnly()` method can be removed from these inner classes to simplify the code.

#GVSCZ: The test cases in `testDuplicateSliceRelease`, `testDuplicateDuplicateRelease`, and `testSliceDuplicateRelease` seem to have a mistake. They all call `buf.duplicate().duplicate()`, which should likely be `buf.duplicate().slice()` for `testDuplicateSliceRelease`. This could lead to incorrect testing of the slice functionality.
