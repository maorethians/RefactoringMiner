#16RK9: The transformation logic for `MvExpand` seems to be replacing fields that do not exist with an `Alias` containing a `Literal`. However, the `Literal.of(f, null)` call is incorrect because `Literal.of()` expects a value of the field's type, and passing `null` directly might lead to unexpected behavior or exceptions. It would be safer to use `Literal.nullLiteral(f.dataType())`.
