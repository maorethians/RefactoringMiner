#6PWWK, #99BXX, #HQ8H4, #J66ZP, #NJPBT, #01BJ5, #HDR1Z, #C9WXQ, #KRMV0: The description of the dot product in `vector-functions.asciidoc` is misleading. It states that when comparing two `bit` vectors, the dot product is the sum of the bitwise AND of the two vectors. This is incorrect; the dot product for `bit` vectors should be the sum of the positions where both bits are 1 (i.e., bitwise AND). The current implementation in `ESVectorUtil.java` and `BitBinaryDenseVector.java` correctly computes this, but the documentation needs to be updated.
