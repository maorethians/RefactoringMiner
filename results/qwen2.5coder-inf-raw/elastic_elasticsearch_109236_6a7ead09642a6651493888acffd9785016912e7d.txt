#MHT5A: The assertion in the constructor of `ForkingResponseHandlerRunnable` is incorrect. It should check `executorUsed` instead of `handler`.
```java
assert executorUsed != EsExecutors.DIRECT_EXECUTOR_SERVICE : "forking handler required, but got " + handler;
```
should be
```java
assert executorUsed != EsExecutors.DIRECT_EXECUTOR_SERVICE : "forking handler required, but got " + executorUsed;
```
