#E3THM, #BJY0X, #0S0Z0, #P473A: The removal of `handleAlreadyShuttingDownWorker` and the change to use `shutdownNow` followed by `awaitTermination` in multiple places suggests a shift in how worker shutdown is managed. However, there are some concerns:
- The use of `shutdownNow` immediately cancels all tasks without allowing them to complete gracefully. This might lead to data loss or inconsistent states if the tasks were in the middle of processing.
- The `awaitTermination` method with a fixed timeout (30 seconds) might not be sufficient for all scenarios, leading to potential resource leaks if the worker does not terminate within this time.
