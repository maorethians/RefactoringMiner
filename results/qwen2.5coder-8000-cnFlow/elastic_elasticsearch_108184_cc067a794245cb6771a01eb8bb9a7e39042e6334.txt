#8YKRD, #CTVE0: The sorting of `reducedBuckets` is moved outside the `if (reduceContext.isFinalReduce())` condition in both `InternalDateHistogram` and `InternalHistogram`. This change means that the buckets will be sorted regardless of whether it's a final reduce or not. If the intention was to sort only during the final reduce, this change would introduce incorrect behavior.

#9PD3P, #83025: The method signature change in `createIndex` to remove the `aliases` parameter is not reflected in the usage within the new test class `AggregationsIT`. The `createindex` method calls `createIndex` with only three parameters, which will cause a compilation error since the original method now expects five parameters.
