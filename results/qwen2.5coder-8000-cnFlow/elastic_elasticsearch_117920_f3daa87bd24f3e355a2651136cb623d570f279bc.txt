#K33S7, #B4JT9, #94Q6X: The removal of `handleAlreadyShuttingDownWorker()` and the direct call to `shutdownNow()` followed by `awaitTermination()` can lead to a situation where tasks in the queue are not properly notified if the worker is already shutting down. The original method checked if the worker was already shut down and handled it accordingly, which included notifying queued runnables. This logic should be preserved to ensure that all pending tasks are appropriately informed of their rejection or failure.
