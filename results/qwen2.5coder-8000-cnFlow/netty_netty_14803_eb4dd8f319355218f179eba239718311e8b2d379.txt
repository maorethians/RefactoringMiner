#VJN92: The addition of `this.id = stream.id();` in the `setStreamAndProperty` method can lead to inconsistent state if the assertion fails. The assertion checks that either `id` is `-1` or it matches `stream.id()`. If the assertion passes, setting `this.id` to `stream.id()` is correct. However, if the assertion fails, setting `this.id` could overwrite an existing valid stream ID with another, which might not be intended behavior. Consider handling this case explicitly or ensuring that the assertion will never fail in practice.
