#EWDN2, #ASX49, #BMM83: The removal of `refreshFailure` and its usage in `maybeFinish` and setting `refreshed` to false in `onFailure` can lead to incorrect behavior. The `refreshFailure` was used to capture any exceptions during the refresh process, which would then be passed to `respond.onFailure`. By removing this mechanism, any failures during the refresh are now silently ignored, and `refreshed.get()` is directly passed to `respond.onSuccess`, potentially leading to a false success notification.
