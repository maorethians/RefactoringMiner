#RCM5Y, #4C8YZ: The logic for checking index resolution in lookup mode has been moved from `resolveIndex` to `postAnalysisVerification`. However, the check in `#4C8YZ` does not handle the case where `indexNameWithModes.size()` is greater than 1 or the mode is not `LOOKUP`, which was previously handled in `#RCM5Y`. This could lead to inconsistent behavior between the two methods.

#NMZGT, #4C8YZ: The `LookupJoin` class now implements `PostAnalysisVerificationAware` and overrides `postAnalysisVerification`. However, the method does not call `super.postAnalysisVerification(failures)` before adding its own checks. This could lead to missing verification steps defined in the superclass.
