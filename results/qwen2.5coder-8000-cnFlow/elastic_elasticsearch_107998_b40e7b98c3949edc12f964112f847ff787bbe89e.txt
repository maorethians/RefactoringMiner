#K443E, #ENBVS, #RS0FS: The addition of try-catch blocks to handle `StackOverflowError` is a good step towards robustness. However, catching `StackOverflowError` is generally discouraged because it can lead to unpredictable behavior and may not always be recoverable. It's better to prevent the stack overflow by optimizing the recursive logic or increasing the stack size if possible.

#7RQZC: The test case `testNoStackOverflow` is designed to verify that a `ParsingException` with a specific message is thrown when a query causes a stack overflow. However, the test does not explicitly assert that no `StackOverflowError` is thrown, which is the primary concern here. It would be more precise to add an assertion to ensure that the exception caught is indeed a `ParsingException` and not a `StackOverflowError`.
