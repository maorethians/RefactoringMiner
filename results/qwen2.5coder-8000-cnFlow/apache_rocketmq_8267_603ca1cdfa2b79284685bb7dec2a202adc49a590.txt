#1KHJ2, #Q6Y6B, #8HHZB: The method `updateSingleTopicConfigWithoutPersist` is introduced to handle updating a single topic configuration without persisting it immediately. However, this method does not include any validation or checks specific to the scenario where persistence is skipped. This could lead to inconsistencies if other parts of the system assume that all updates are persisted immediately. In `updateTopicConfigList`, after updating each topic configuration without persisting, a call to `this.persist()` is made to persist all changes at once. This approach can be problematic in terms of atomicity and error handling. If an exception occurs during the persistence step, some configurations might have been updated but not persisted, leading to an inconsistent state. The method `updateTopicConfig` now delegates its update logic to `updateSingleTopicConfigWithoutPersist` before persisting the specific topic configuration. This change is mostly correct but could lead to confusion if other parts of the system expect `updateTopicConfig` to handle persistence internally. It might be beneficial to rename `updateTopicConfig` to something like `updateAndPersistTopicConfig` to clarify its behavior.
