#F4KMX, #Y2VXN, #CE1K4: The use of `VersionUtils.randomCompatibleVersion(random(), VersionUtils.getPreviousVersion())` assumes that there is at least one compatible version with the previous version. This might not always be true and could lead to an empty list in `randomCompatibleVersion`, causing a `NoSuchElementException`. It would be safer to handle this case explicitly or ensure that there is always at least one compatible version.
