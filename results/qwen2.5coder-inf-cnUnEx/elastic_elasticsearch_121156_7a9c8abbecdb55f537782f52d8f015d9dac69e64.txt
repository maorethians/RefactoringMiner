This rule should be reintroduced or its logic integrated into `PruneRedundantOrderBy` to ensure that queries with unbounded sorts are handled correctly.

The `OrderExec` class was responsible for handling sorting at the physical execution level, and its removal without a corresponding replacement or alternative mechanism will cause runtime errors.
