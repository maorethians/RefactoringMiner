#VJN92: The assignment of `this.id = stream.id();` should be reviewed for thread safety. Since `id` is marked as `volatile`, it ensures visibility across threads, but the compound action of checking `assert id == -1 || stream.id() == id;` and then setting `this.id = stream.id();` is not atomic. This could lead to race conditions if multiple threads are calling `setStreamAndProperty` concurrently.
