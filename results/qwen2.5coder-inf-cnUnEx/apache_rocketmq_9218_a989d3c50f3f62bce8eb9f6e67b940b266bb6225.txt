#2B020, #1HKDY, #7JRNV: The addition of `!brokerConfig.isPopConsumerKVServiceEnable()` to the conditionals introduces a dependency on the broker configuration that was not previously present. This change should be accompanied by appropriate documentation and possibly test cases to ensure that the behavior is as expected when `popConsumerKVServiceEnable` is toggled.

#7JRNV: The removal of the fallback to `brokerOffset` when `pullOffset` is less than 0 could lead to incorrect calculations if `offsetManager.queryOffset(group, topic, queueId)` also returns a negative value. This should be addressed by adding a check or ensuring that `queryOffset` cannot return a negative value in this context.
