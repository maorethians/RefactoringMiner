#FGSTP, #V5YPZ, #D6EGT, #JE61V, #ESNB6, #VC5FF, #MB1TE: In several places, `MergeReason.MAPPING_UPDATE` is hardcoded (e.g., #FGSTP, #V5YPZ, #D6EGT, #JE61V, #ESNB6, #VC5FF, #MB1TE). This might be fine for most cases but could lead to issues if different reasons are needed in the future.

#E0Z9R, #K264F, #86CWN, #5FH91, #GWANS, #KE8JR: In other places, a variable `reason` is used (e.g., #E0Z9R, #K264F, #86CWN, #5FH91, #GWANS, #KE8JR). This is more flexible and should be preferred when the reason can vary.
Ensure that all places where `parseMapping` or `merge` are called consider whether a specific `MergeReason` other than `MAPPING_UPDATE` might be appropriate. For example, if a mapping is being updated due to an index template, `MergeReason.INDEX_TEMPLATE` should be used instead of `MAPPING_UPDATE`.
