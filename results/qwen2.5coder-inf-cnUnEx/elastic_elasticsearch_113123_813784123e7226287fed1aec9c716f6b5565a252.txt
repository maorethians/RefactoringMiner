#N0DAX, #E0HHP: The test cases `testIsNotNullOnCase` and `testIsNotNullOnCase_With_IS_NULL` use `Expressions.names(caseF.children())` to assert the names of the children of a `Case` expression. However, the `names` method expects expressions that have a name, such as fields or aliases. The conditions in the `Case` statement (e.g., "emp_no > 10000", "\"1\"") are not named expressions and will likely result in an error or unexpected behavior when passed to `Expressions.names`.
