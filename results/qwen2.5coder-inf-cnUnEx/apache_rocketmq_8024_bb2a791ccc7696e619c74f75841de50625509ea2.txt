#4707G, #WA7R7, #ZSPRK, #M9PSE: These methods introduce new functionality for pulling messages with a `MessageSelector` that blocks if no messages are found. The logic appears correct and follows the pattern of existing methods.
However, there is a potential issue with consistency in exception handling:
- `#4707G` and `#WA7R7` throw `MQClientException`, `RemotingException`, `InterruptedException`, and optionally `MQBrokerException`.
- `#ZSPRK` and `#M9PSE` only propagate the exceptions thrown by `defaultMQPullConsumerImpl.pullBlockIfNotFoundWithMessageSelector`.
To ensure consistency, it's recommended to explicitly declare all possible exceptions that can be thrown by these methods.
