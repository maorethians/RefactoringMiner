#95FN4: The constructor for `Wrapper` initializes `super` with `ObjectMapper.Dynamic.getRootDynamic(in.mappingLookup())`. This implies that every time a `Wrapper` is created, it fetches the root dynamic setting from the mapping lookup. If this operation is expensive or has side effects, it could lead to performance issues or unintended behavior. Consider caching the result of `getRootDynamic` if it does not change frequently.
