#45W1N, #285WR: The variable `lastStatusUpdateTime` is updated only when a status update occurs, but it should be updated every time the loop iterates to accurately reflect the last time an update was made. This could lead to incorrect calculations of the time since the last status update in some cases.

#45W1N, #285WR: The variable `unreportedIterations` is reset only when a status update occurs. However, if the loop exits due to reaching `maxIterations`, `now - startTime >= maxTimeNanos`, or `isFinished()`, `unreportedIterations` will not be included in the final status update. This could lead to inaccurate reporting of iterations.

Consider renaming `lastStatusUpdateTime` to something like `lastStatusUpdateTimestamp` for clarity.
