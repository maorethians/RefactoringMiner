#BATM3, #SA0J7: The `union` method in `Sets.java` now takes a varargs parameter for additional sets, but in `BaseRestHandler#handleRequest`, it is called with three separate arguments (`RestResponse.RESPONSE_PARAMS`, `ALWAYS_SUPPORTED`, `supported`). This will not work as intended because the varargs parameter will treat `ALWAYS_SUPPORTED` and `supported` as individual elements rather than collections to be unioned. The call should be modified to pass a single array or collection containing all sets.
