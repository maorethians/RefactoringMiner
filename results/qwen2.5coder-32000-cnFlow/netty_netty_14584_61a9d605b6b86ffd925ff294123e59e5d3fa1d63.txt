#6V64K: The change to `CENTRAL_QUEUE_CAPACITY` ensures that it is at least 2 by using `Math.min(2, ...)`. However, this logic seems incorrect because `Math.min(2, ...)` will always return 2 if the system property value or `NettyRuntime.availableProcessors()` is greater than 2. This effectively caps the capacity to a minimum of 2 but not allowing it to exceed 2 unless the system property explicitly sets it to a higher value. If the intention was to ensure that the capacity is at least 2, the logic should be adjusted to `Math.max(2, ...)`.
