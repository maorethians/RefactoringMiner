#3HXWH, #4AA33, #40WAF: The methods `numChunkAllocations`, `numChunkDeallocations`, and `numActiveChunks` lock and unlock the arena for each call. This could lead to performance issues if these methods are called frequently in a high-concurrency environment. Consider using atomic variables or other concurrency-friendly mechanisms to avoid locking.

#40WAF: The calculation of active chunks in `numActiveChunks` uses `max(val, 0)`. While this ensures non-negative values, it might be worth adding a comment explaining why negative values could occur and why they are being clamped to zero. This would improve code clarity for future maintainers.
