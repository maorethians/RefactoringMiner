#H1J49, #823CJ, #X07YR, #Z9YXV, #RN7JM: The `isRunning` flag is used to control the execution state of the engine. However, the `pauseExecution` method no longer uses the `synchronized` keyword, which could lead to race conditions if multiple threads attempt to pause the engine simultaneously. The same applies to the `stop` method.

#823CJ: The `pauseExecution` method now clears the schedules without ensuring that the engine is not running. This might cause issues if there are ongoing operations that depend on the schedules being intact during a pause.

#SDV97, #Z9YXV: Similar to `pauseExecution`, the `stop` method clears the schedules and closes the ticker without ensuring that the engine has stopped running, which could lead to inconsistent states or resource leaks.
