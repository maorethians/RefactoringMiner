#H1J49, #823CJ, #X07YR, #Z9YXV, #RN7JM: The `isRunning` flag is used to control the execution state of the engine. However, the `pauseExecution` method no longer uses the `synchronized` keyword, which could lead to race conditions if multiple threads attempt to pause the engine simultaneously. The same applies to the `stop` method.

#823CJ, #SDV97: The `pauseExecution` and `stop` methods now clear the schedules without ensuring that the engine is not running or has fully stopped. This could lead to inconsistent states where watches are cleared while the engine might still be in a transitional state between running and paused, or result in watches being removed while the engine is still processing jobs, leading to potential data inconsistencies or lost job executions.
