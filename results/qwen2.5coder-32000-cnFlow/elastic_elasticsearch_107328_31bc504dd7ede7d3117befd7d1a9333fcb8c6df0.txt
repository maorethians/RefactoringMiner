#9415T, #RC2RR, #ZQ4TR, #3HF6B: The logic for combining upper groupings with lower projections seems correct based on the provided context. However, there are a few areas that could benefit from additional clarity and potential improvements:
In `combineUpperGroupingsAndLowerProjections`, the comment "Replace any matching attribute directly with the aliased attribute from the projection." is somewhat vague. It would be helpful to clarify what exactly is meant by "matching attribute" and how the alias resolution works in this context.
Additionally, the method `resolve` in `AttributeMap` has a potential issue where it could enter an infinite loop if there are circular references in the aliases. The current implementation relies on a hardcoded limit (`allowedLookups = 1000`) to prevent this, which might not be sufficient or optimal for all cases. It would be better to handle cycles more gracefully, perhaps by keeping track of visited keys.
In `testCombineProjectionWithPruning` and the newly added `testEliminateDuplicateRenamedGroupings`, the expected output comments are detailed but could benefit from being more explicit about how the alias resolution affects the grouping attributes. This would help future maintainers understand the intent behind these tests.
