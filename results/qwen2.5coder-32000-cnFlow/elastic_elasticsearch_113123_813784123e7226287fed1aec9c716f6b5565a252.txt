#F6GD5, #GYWFX: The change to `skipExpression` now includes `Case` expressions, but the corresponding test cases in `testIsNotNullOnCase` and `testIsNotNullOnCase_With_IS_NULL` do not seem to cover all possible scenarios for `Case` expressions. Specifically, they check if the children of the `Case` expression are correctly identified, but they do not verify that the `InferIsNotNull` rule behaves as expected when encountering a `Case` expression in an `is not null` condition. It would be beneficial to add more test cases that cover different structures and conditions within `Case` expressions.
