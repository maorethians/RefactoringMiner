#ZZ2A4: The change from checking `totalTargetAllocations()` to `getTaskParams().getNumberOfAllocations()` might not be semantically equivalent. The method `totalTargetAllocations()` sums up target allocations across all nodes, whereas `getTaskParams().getNumberOfAllocations()` retrieves the number of allocations requested by the user for a specific model deployment task. This change could lead to incorrect behavior if there are multiple model deployments with different allocation requests.
