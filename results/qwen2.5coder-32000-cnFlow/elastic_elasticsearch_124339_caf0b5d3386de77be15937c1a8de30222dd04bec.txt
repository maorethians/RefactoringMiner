#15JG8, #GKJ3S: The change in `#15JG8` modifies the range of `positionCount` to include 0, which could lead to a test case where `b.values()` is empty. This might not be intentional and should be considered if the logic in `#GKJ3S` assumes at least one position. The introduction of `anyNonNullValue` in `#GKJ3S` changes the condition for `mask.hadMultivaluedFields()`. This new condition checks if there are any non-null values in `b.values()`, which might not accurately reflect whether there were multivalued fields. The original test checked directly if `hadMultivaluedFields()` was true, implying that the presence of multivalued fields was already determined by the block's nature. The new logic could lead to incorrect assertions if the block's multivalue status is not correctly captured by the presence of non-null values.
