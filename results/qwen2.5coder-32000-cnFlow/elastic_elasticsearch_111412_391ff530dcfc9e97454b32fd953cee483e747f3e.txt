#ZYKT5, #PPNG5, #971VZ: The `createCache` method in `EnrichCache` and its usage in constructors do not handle the case where `weigher` is null. This could lead to a NullPointerException when the cache tries to use the weigher.

#6W5BT: The new parser for `CACHE_SIZE` does not handle invalid string inputs gracefully, potentially throwing an exception if the input cannot be parsed as a long.

#YVAYZ: The fallback logic in the `EnrichPlugin` constructor could lead to unexpected behavior if `JvmInfo.jvmInfo().getConfiguredMaxHeapSize()` returns 0 or a negative value. Additionally, the hardcoded fallback of 1000 entries might not be appropriate for all environments.
