#DBFHJ, #1VVZJ, #AKP20, #63968, #PG42R: The chosen maximum depth values for expressions (`MAX_EXPRESSION_DEPTH`) and queries (`MAX_QUERY_DEPTH`) are both set to 500. While this is a reasonable starting point, it might be beneficial to make these configurable via system properties or settings, allowing users to adjust them based on their specific needs and the complexity of their ESQL statements.

#1VVZJ, #63968: The `expressionDepth` and `queryDepth` counters are incremented at the beginning of the respective methods and decremented in a `finally` block. This ensures that the depth is correctly adjusted even if an exception occurs. However, it's important to ensure that no other code paths within these methods could bypass the `finally` block, which would lead to incorrect depth tracking.
