#8YKRD, #CTVE0: The sorting of `reducedBuckets` is moved outside the `if (reduceContext.isFinalReduce())` block. This change affects both `InternalDateHistogram` and `InternalHistogram`. The original code only sorted the buckets if it was a final reduce operation, which suggests that sorting might be unnecessary or redundant in non-final reduce operations. Moving the sort operation outside this condition could lead to additional processing in scenarios where it is not needed.
