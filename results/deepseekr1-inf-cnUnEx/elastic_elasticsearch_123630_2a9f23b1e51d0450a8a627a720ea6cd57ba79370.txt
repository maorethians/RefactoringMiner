#BKJ08, #9S3AR, #7XYT9, #M90MX, #JPEZ4, #278PK, #6R282, #WHY7V, #KCS0C, #AQ0WB: The introduction of `MAX_DELETE_EXCEPTIONS` and `DeletionExceptions` record is a good approach to limit and track deletion exceptions. However, there's a potential issue where `useOrMaybeSuppress` doesn't check if `MAX_DELETE_EXCEPTIONS` is reached before creating a new `DeletionExceptions` instance. This could lead to memory leaks if exceptions are continuously added beyond the limit. Additionally, the use of `AtomicReference<DeletionExceptions>` and repeated creation of new `DeletionExceptions` instances could introduce performance overhead in high-concurrency scenarios. Consider using a more efficient data structure or mechanism to track exceptions.
