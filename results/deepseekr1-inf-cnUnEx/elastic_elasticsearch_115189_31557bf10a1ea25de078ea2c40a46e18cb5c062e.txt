#NMYD8: The test `testScale_GivenTrainedModelAllocationAndNoMlNode` has several issues:
1. The test creates a `ClusterState` but does not add any nodes to it. This means the `isEmpty()` check in `MlAutoscalingContext` will return `true`, leading to a downscale instead of an upscale as intended.
2. The test does not mock the `NodeLoadDetector`, which means the scaling decision may not be properly controlled, making the test less deterministic.
3. The test does not cover the case where ML nodes are explicitly absent but trained models are present, which is the scenario the test aims to simulate.
These issues make the test incomplete and potentially misleading in its assertions about scaling behavior.
