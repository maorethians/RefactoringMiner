#YYZNY: The logic for updating `sendSegmentNumber` and `receiveSegmentNumber` is incorrect. The same method `incrementUintSegmentNumber` is called twice, both times with `receiveSegmentNumber`. This will result in incorrect segment numbers being used.

#AQTZM: Casting `long` to `int` can cause overflow issues if the value exceeds the maximum value of an integer. Since the segment number and ACK number are expected to wrap around at the uint32 max, they should remain as `long` and be cast to `int` only when writing to the `ByteBuf`.
