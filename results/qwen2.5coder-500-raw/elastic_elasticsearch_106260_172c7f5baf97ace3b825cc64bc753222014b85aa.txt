#JV72Z: The `getLogLevels` method collects log levels into a `NavigableMap`. This choice is fine for maintaining order, but it might be overkill if ordering is not necessary. Consider using a regular `Map` unless the ordered nature is required elsewhere in the codebase.
In the `runWithLoggersRestored` method, the removal of additional loggers and restoration of original levels are done separately. This could lead to inconsistencies if an exception occurs between these two operations. Consider combining these actions into a single atomic operation or ensuring that both steps are completed successfully.
