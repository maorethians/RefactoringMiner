#28YPX: The filter condition `.filter(node -> node.getVersion().onOrAfter(V_8_2_0))` assumes that the `TrainedModelCacheInfoAction.Request` can only be executed on nodes running version 8.2.0 or later. This might lead to unexpected behavior if there are mixed-version clusters where some nodes are below 8.2.0 and others are at or above 8.2.0. Ensure that this assumption is correct and documented, or consider handling the case where no nodes meet the version requirement.
