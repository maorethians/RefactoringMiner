This change introduces redundancy and complexity. Specifically, the log statement in `#D9QWQ` for histograms could be misleading because it logs "PENDING" when there are remaining counts, which might not clearly indicate that the assertion is still being evaluated rather than failing.

This could make it difficult to read and understand which assertions are remaining, especially if there are many or similar keys.
