#GXW45, #01BAY, #NPX5K: The changes to the `hashCode` methods introduce inconsistencies and potential issues:
- In `AbstractHttp2StreamFrame`, returning a constant value of 31 when `stream` is null can lead to poor hash distribution.
- In `DefaultHttp2PingFrame`, using `(int) (content ^ (content >>> 32))` for the initial hash calculation might not be optimal and could result in collisions if `content` values are similar.
- In `Http2FrameCodec.DefaultHttp2FrameStream`, returning `id` when `stream` is null is inconsistent with other implementations and may lead to unexpected behavior.
