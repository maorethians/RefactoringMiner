#DBFHJ, #1VVZJ, #AKP20, #63968, #PG42R: The maximum depth values (`MAX_EXPRESSION_DEPTH` and `MAX_QUERY_DEPTH`) are set to 500, which seems arbitrary without any explanation or configuration option. Consider making these configurable via settings or providing a rationale for the chosen value.

#1VVZJ, #63968: In both `expression` and `visitCompositeQuery`, the depth checks occur before parsing/processing the expression/query. This means that if an exception is thrown during parsing, the depth will not be decremented in the `finally` block. Ensure that any exceptions thrown by `typedParsing` or other operations within these methods do not leave the depth counters in an inconsistent state.
