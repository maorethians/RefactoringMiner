#8YKRD, #CTVE0: The sorting of `reducedBuckets` is moved outside the `if (reduceContext.isFinalReduce())` block. This change means that the buckets will be sorted regardless of whether it's a final reduce or not. If this behavior is intended, then it's fine. However, if the original logic was intentional to avoid sorting in non-final reduces for performance reasons, this change could introduce unnecessary overhead.
