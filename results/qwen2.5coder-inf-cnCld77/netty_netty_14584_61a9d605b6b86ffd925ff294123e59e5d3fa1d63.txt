#6V64K: The change to limit `CENTRAL_QUEUE_CAPACITY` to a minimum of 2 could lead to unexpected behavior if `NettyRuntime.availableProcessors()` returns a value less than 2. This might not align with the original intent, which seems to be setting the capacity based on the number of available processors. Consider adding a check or ensuring that the default value is at least 2 without altering the dynamic calculation.
