#F4KMX, #1YM5G, #CE1K4: The change from using `VersionUtils.randomVersionBetween` to `VersionUtils.randomCompatibleVersion` might not cover all compatible versions as intended. The original method allowed specifying a range between the minimum compatibility version and the previous version, ensuring that only compatible versions within this specific range were considered. The new method simply picks any random compatible version without considering the upper bound of the previous version. This could lead to selecting a version that is compatible but outside the expected range.
