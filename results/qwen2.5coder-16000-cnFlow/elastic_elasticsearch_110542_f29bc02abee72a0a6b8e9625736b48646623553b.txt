#KTFT2, #C7216, #QZKCY: The changes introduce a `CountDownLatch` for synchronization in test methods, which is generally appropriate for ensuring that asynchronous operations complete before assertions are made. However, the use of `AtomicReference` and `CountDownLatch` can be simplified by directly using an `ActionListener` that captures the result and signals completion.

#KTFT2: The change to call `rescheduleImmediately.onResponse(false);` when `stopped` is true ensures that the listener is notified, which is correct. However, it might be worth adding a comment explaining why this notification is necessary, especially if the behavior of not notifying was intentional before.
