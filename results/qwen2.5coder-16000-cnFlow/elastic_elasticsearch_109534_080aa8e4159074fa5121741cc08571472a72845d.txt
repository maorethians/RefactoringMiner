#2SSZT, #5XF40, #XW19Y: The test cases in `RemainingTimeTests` are incorrect. The first assertion in both `testRemainingTime` and `testRemainingTimeMaxValue` checks if the remaining time is greater than `TimeValue.ZERO`, but the second assertion immediately follows checking if it is equal to `TimeValue.ZERO`. This will always fail because a value cannot be both greater than zero and equal to zero simultaneously.
