#T81NP, #SF9S9: The use of `assertBusy` without specifying a timeout might lead to indeterminate test durations. It's better to specify a reasonable timeout explicitly. Additionally, the use of `Thread.sleep` in `assertBusy` can be replaced with a more precise wait mechanism to avoid unnecessary delays.

#KNKFS: The new method `userEventTriggered` handles `SslCloseCompletionEvent`. It checks if the event is successful and the channel is active before closing the connection. This logic seems correct, but it might be beneficial to add logging or metrics for cases where the channel is not active when a successful close event is received.
