#DT641, #Y2B46, #DJZE0, #SGGKS: The introduction of `ENABLE_STACK_OVERFLOW_AVOIDANCE` setting and its usage in `onConnectionClosed` method seems to be a reasonable change aimed at avoiding stack overflows by controlling the execution context of response handlers. However, there are a few points to consider:
- The setting is marked as deprecated, which implies it might be removed in future releases. Ensure that this deprecation is communicated clearly and that there is a plan for replacing or removing its functionality.
- The logic in `onConnectionClosed` now checks the value of `enableStackOverflowAvoidance` before deciding whether to execute the handler on the current thread or submit it to the generic thread pool. This introduces additional complexity. Ensure that this change does not introduce race conditions, especially since the setting can be changed at runtime.
- The test cases added (`testOnConnectionClosedUsesHandlerExecutor`, `testOnConnetionCloseStackOverflowAvoidance`) cover different scenarios but could benefit from more detailed assertions to ensure that the handler is indeed executed on the expected thread.
